# The call with Danny in the app: the orchestrator's decisions (Nov 16)

The owner finished La casa and wants to call Danny. He said yes (Nov 16) to test spending (up to 6,000 ElevenLabs credits for simulated tests); his own calls cost about 650 credits a minute. Facts about the old call are in the reading agent's report, summarised here where needed. Owner's rules that bind: Danny is slow, plain, A1/A2 words only, says "Sí." or "Eso." and moves on, does not talk back a lot, no pet names; anything the learner says must be heard by the app or by Danny; Spanish on screen except menus (English allowed for menus and for the "stuck" line).

## The call, version 1 (place 1, La casa)

- The job (from page 02): say hello, say who you are, accept a drink, say thanks and goodbye. About 5 minutes.
- A speaking call: no touches. Danny may show one picture at a time to prompt what to say (a glass of juice when he offers a drink, the house, the family). Pictures come from the place's ¿Cuál? items (the "si" picture of a word's pair, drawn with the same code cual.js uses), keyed by the word.
- Words: the words of La casa (the focus_words of its 11 stories, historia/cuentos/<id>.json where "place" is "La casa"), each marked "open" (word record ear.etapa >= 3) or "heard". Danny uses only these words plus the plain glue words of A1 (sí, no, y, el, la, un, una, es, está, de, ¿…?). He asks the learner to SAY open words; he only uses heard words himself.
- Length: the page sends "(fin)" at 6 minutes; the call is ended by the page at 7 minutes at the latest (about 4,500 credits at most).

3. Hablar tab: one card per place, in place order. La casa's card opens when every story of La casa has been played (`CURSO.lugares[0].cuentos.every(hecho)`, the app's own `hecho`; stories the level check skipped count as played). Open card: title "Call Danny", subtitle "La casa · about 5 min", a green 48 px call disc. Locked cards: grey, "Opens when you finish <place>". Remove the round-3 "Calls open later" card.
4. Call screen: full screen like a lesson (calm dark grey #1B1B1F, tab bar hidden), top bar with the close X (48 px) at left and a timer (mm:ss, 12 px) at right. In the middle the picture Danny shows (whole-number scaled, 16 px margins), and under it Danny's last line in Spanish (18 px, white, at most two lines) and, under that, what the app heard the learner say (16 px, #9AD5A8). In the bottom third: the big mic disc (72 px) and its state label, and an outline "Help" button (48 px; sends "(ayuda)").
5. Mic states (the research): idle before the call: green disc with a phone icon, label "Start call"; connecting: the disc pulses, label "Calling…"; Danny speaking: a blue #3B5BDB animated wave around the disc, label "Danny"; learner's turn (mode "listening"): a green ring around the disc whose thickness follows the microphone level (the SDK's input volume), label "Your turn"; processing: when the learner's level has been low for 300 ms during his turn, three pulsing dots replace the ring until Danny speaks. Every change of state shows within 200 ms.
6. Start only from the tap: before startSession, stop every other sound (the app's hush/ac), set a one-call lock, and let the SDK ask for the microphone. A refused microphone shows one plain line ("The microphone is off. Allow it in your browser to call.") and the close X.
7. Dynamic variables sent at start: `place` ("La casa"), `job` (the line above, in English for Danny), `words_open` and `words_heard` (comma-separated), `minutes` ("5"), `opening` ("¡Hola! Soy Danny. ¿Cómo estás?"). show_picture(key): shows that word's picture if the key is known, ignores it otherwise, always answers "shown".
8. Hearing: every learner line Danny's side transcribes (onMessage, source user) is shown and matched against the place's words (accents and case ignored); each matched word gets a mouth record: `PAL.responde(word,"mouth",true,ms)` (read palabras.js to confirm the signature; do not change how records are stored).
9. Ending: the X, the page's 7-minute limit, or Danny ending the call. Then a summary screen in the lesson end layout: H1 "Call done", one line "N min · M words said", the words said as small chips, and a blue "Back to Hablar" button. Clean up on every path: endSession, clear timers, release the lock; on pagehide end the session.
10. No call can be placed from localhost (the agent is locked to the published origin). For tests on 8765, stub window.ELConv with a fake Conversation that walks through the modes and sends two of Danny's lines and two learner lines, so every screen and state can be screenshotted. The stub is used only when the URL has `?callstub=1`.

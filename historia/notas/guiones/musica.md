# La música: three stories (draft 1, before the reviews)

Place 10, El fuerte. Scene 2 of 4 (scene id `musica`). Stories 111 to 113. The learner is the little frog (Coquí). He has finished stories 1 to 110.

Scene words (page 02): música, canción, baila, canta, guitarra, tambor, ríe, feliz, divertido, ¿quieres bailar?, claro, ¡qué bueno!
How they are shared out: 111 música, canción, guitarra, tambor; 112 baila, canta, ¿quieres bailar?, claro; 113 ríe, feliz, divertido, ¡qué bueno!

The picture: the fort's courtyard at night, with the party. One view. At the back, the fort's inner wall with lanterns on it. In front of the wall, at the left, a low stage of boards: two stools, a microphone on a stand, and a stand that holds the instruments. In the middle and at the right, the open floor where people dance. At the right, a bench against the wall. No roof.

People: the two músicos (one with a hat, one with a beard); the family; the people of every place.

Voices, the signal for a right move, what follows a wrong move, pokes and the corner card are as in La calle. Every story has two scored rounds; the card shows two places. Every story ends with the shared `fin`. Every story starts fresh. Words: the everyday Puerto Rican word wins over the plan's (page 16). In a game nobody looks at, turns to or points at any of the things he can choose. Where a step says "signed" after somebody's own line, that line is the ask. After a wrong move the mistake stays in the picture until he touches the sign.

## Story 111: La música

- **Goal he can see:** on the open floor, three couples wait to dance; they tap their feet and look at the stage.
- **At stake:** the dancers (3 couples). The wrong instrument in a músico's hands makes a sour noise: a couple covers its ears, and walks off to the bench.
- **New words:** música, canción, guitarra, tambor.
- **Back again:** fiesta, fuerte, noche, toca, quiere, otro, me gusta, mucho, bonito, ahora, mira, por favor, gracias, sí, no.

**Start:** night; lanterns. On the stage the two músicos sit on their stools with empty hands; the instruments (a guitar, a drum) hang on their stand. Three couples on the open floor. The frog on the stage's edge. Abuela beside the stage.

**The opening** (big sign)

1. NARR: "Abuela dice: Coquí, en una fiesta hay música. Mira." (signed, the stage.) He touches it: the músico with the hat waves. NARR: "El músico dice: ¡Hola, Coquí! ¿Quieres música?" COQUÍ [TURN: sí (sí, si)]: "¡Sí!"
2. NARR: "El músico dice: Para la música, la guitarra, por favor." (signed, the guitar.) He touches it: the frog pushes the guitar off its stand into his hands; he strums: small notes float up; the couples sway. NARR: "El músico dice: Esta es mi guitarra."
3. The músico with the beard taps his knees. NARR: "El otro músico dice: Y yo toco el tambor." (signed, the drum.) He touches it: the frog pushes the drum over; he beats it: bigger notes; the couples step. NARR: "Abuela dice: La guitarra y el tambor. ¡Qué bonita la música!"
4. They play together, and stop. NARR: "El músico dice: Esa es una canción. ¿Te gusta la canción?" COQUÍ [TURN: mucho (mucho, sí mucho)]: "¡Sí, mucho!" NARR: "El músico dice: Ahora, otra canción." They hang the instruments back on the stand.

**The turn**

5. The músicos wait with their hands open; each new song begins with one instrument. The three couples stand ready. The card shows two places. The signs go.

**The game.** Before each song the two músicos shut their eyes to think of it, and one of them (drawn) asks for the instrument the song begins with (drawn, each round on its own). The frog pushes the guitar or the drum into the hands of the one who asked.

6. **Decision A, touch an instrument (the guitar, the drum; tests guitarra against tambor).** NARR: "El músico dice: Para esta canción, el tambor." (Or, drawn: "Para esta canción, la guitarra.")
   - Right: he plays; notes float up; the couples dance. A star.
   - Wrong: he plays what he gets, his eyes shut: the guitar is beaten like a drum (or the drum is strummed): a sour noise, in jagged notes. A couple covers its ears and walks off to the bench. NARR: "El músico dice: ¡No, Coquí! ¡El tambor!" A cross. The sign is on the right instrument; he touches it.
7. **Decision B, touch an instrument (the same game; drawn again).** NARR: "El otro músico dice: Para esta canción, la guitarra." (Drawn.) Right and wrong as in A.

**The ending**

8. The song goes on; the couples that are left dance.
   - Three couples. Stars and a heart.
   - Two couples; one watches from the bench.
   - One couple; two watch from the bench, and tap their feet.
9. NARR: "Abuela dice: ¡Qué bonita canción! Coquí, ¿quieres otra?" COQUÍ [TURN: otro (otra, otra por favor, otro)]: "¡Otra, por favor!" NARR: "En la fiesta hay música: una guitarra, un tambor y una canción."

**Fewest hearings before a word can cost:** guitarra 3 (steps 2, 3), tambor 2 (step 3) (to fix in the reviews: a third). Not deciding: música 4 (steps 1, 2, 3), canción 3 (step 4).
**A miss is filed under:** the instrument that was asked for.
**Chance without the Spanish:** one in two, each round on its own.
**Speaking turns:** three (steps 1, 4, 9): sí; mucho; otro.

## Story 112: ¿Quieres bailar?

- **Goal he can see:** over the stage hang three big paper notes, lit from inside: the song's three parts. The músicos point at them.
- **At stake:** the song's parts (3 notes). Someone at the wrong place trips on the microphone's cable, or sings where nobody hears: the music stops for a moment, and a note goes dark.
- **New words:** baila, canta, ¿quieres bailar?, claro.
- **Back again:** música, canción, guitarra, tambor, quiero, también, cansado, ahora, yo, tú, por favor, gracias, sí, no.

**Start:** night. The músicos play softly. The microphone stands at the stage's front. On the open floor, nobody yet. Abuelo, abuela, Luis and Carmen stand at the right. The frog between the stage and the floor.

**The opening** (big sign)

1. Abuelo bows to abuela. NARR: "Abuelo dice: ¿Quieres bailar?" NARR: "Abuela dice: ¡Claro!" (signed, the open floor.) He touches it: the frog leads them onto the floor; they dance. NARR: "Luis dice: Mira, Coquí. Abuela baila con abuelo."
2. Carmen runs to the microphone. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¡Yo canto!" **(canto: a form of canta)** (signed, the microphone.) He touches it: the frog tips it down to her height; she sings: notes. NARR: "Luis dice: Carmen canta la canción."
3. NARR: "Carmen dice: Luis, ¿quieres bailar?" NARR: "Luis dice: No... Yo no bailo." **(bailo: a form of baila)** He looks at his feet. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¡Claro que sí! Luis baila y yo canto." She pushes him onto the floor; he dances, stiff, then well.
4. Abuela, turning, calls. NARR: "Abuela dice: Coquí, ¿quieres bailar?" COQUÍ [TURN: quiero (quiero, sí quiero)]: "¡Sí, quiero!" He hops in a circle. NARR: "Abuela dice: ¡Coquí baila!"

**The turn**

5. The song ends; everybody comes off the floor, and Carmen comes away from the microphone. The three paper notes over the stage light up: a long song begins. People stand at the right, ready. The card shows two places. The signs go.

**The game.** The músico with the hat, his eyes on his guitar, says what one of the people does now (the person is drawn from abuela, abuelo, Luis and Carmen; what he does is drawn on its own: any of them may dance, any of them may sing). The frog leads that one to the open floor, or to the microphone.

6. **Decision A, touch a place (the open floor, the microphone; tests baila against canta).** NARR: "El músico dice: Ahora abuelo canta." (Or, drawn: "Ahora abuelo baila." The person is drawn too.)
   - Right: he sings into the microphone (notes), or dances on the floor. A star.
   - Wrong: he dances at the microphone, and his foot catches its cable: the music stops. (Or he sings on the floor, far from the microphone: nobody hears, and the músicos stop to listen.) NARR: "El músico dice: ¡No, Coquí! Abuelo canta." One of the paper notes goes dark. A cross. The sign is on the right place; he touches it: the frog leads him across.
7. **Decision B, touch a place (the same game; another person).** NARR: "El músico dice: Ahora Carmen baila." (Drawn.) Right and wrong as in A.

**The ending**

8. The song comes to its end.
   - Three notes lit: everybody dances, and the one at the microphone sings. Stars and a heart.
   - Two notes lit.
   - One note lit: a short song.
9. NARR: "Carmen dice: Coquí, ¿tú cantas?" **(cantas: a form of canta)** The frog hops onto the stage and sings into the microphone: "¡Coquí! ¡Coquí!" NARR: "El músico dice: ¡Qué bonita canción! ¿Otra?" COQUÍ [TURN: otro (otra, sí otra, otro)]: "¡Otra!" NARR: "Abuela baila, Carmen canta. Y Coquí canta también."

**Fewest hearings before a word can cost:** baila 3 and bailo once (steps 1, 3, 4), canta 2 and canto twice (steps 2, 3) (to weigh: a third exact). Not deciding: ¿quieres bailar? 3 (steps 1, 3, 4); claro 2 (steps 1, 3) (to fix: a third).
**Forms:** bailo with baila; canto, cantas with canta.
**A miss is filed under:** the one that was said (baila or canta).
**Chance without the Spanish:** one in two, each round on its own: who is named does not say where he goes.
**Speaking turns:** two (steps 4, 9): quiero; otro.

## Story 113: ¡Qué bueno!

- **Goal he can see:** abuela's camera makes paper photos; a small window on its back shows three squares: three photos are left. She hangs the camera round the frog's neck.
- **At stake:** the photos (3). A photo of the wrong person is a photo wasted.
- **New words:** ríe, feliz, divertido, ¡qué bueno!
- **Back again:** baila, canta, come, duerme, juega, fiesta, música, contento, mira, quiero, claro, ¿quieres bailar?, aquí, gracias, sí, no.

**Start:** night; the party is at its height. On the open floor and round it, four people, each doing one thing: someone dances, someone sings at the microphone, someone eats at the bench, someone laughs. Abuela beside the frog with her camera.

**The opening** (big sign)

1. Luis tells Carmen something in her ear; Carmen laughs, bent double. NARR: "Abuela dice: Mira, Coquí: Carmen ríe." (signed, Carmen.) He touches her: she laughs harder. NARR: "Abuela dice: Carmen ríe mucho. ¡Está feliz!"
2. NARR: "Luis dice: ¡Qué fiesta divertida!" He laughs too. NARR: "Abuela dice: Luis ríe también. La fiesta es divertida."
3. Abuela lifts her camera. NARR: "Abuela dice: Quiero una foto de alguien que ríe." **(alguien: heard; a word of the last scene)** (signed, Carmen.) He touches her: a flash; a paper photo slides out of the camera: Carmen, laughing. NARR: "Abuela dice: ¡Qué bueno! Mira la foto: Carmen ríe."
4. Abuela hangs the camera round the frog's neck; its small window shows three squares. NARR: "Abuela dice: Ahora tú, Coquí. ¿Estás feliz?" COQUÍ [TURN: contento (contento, sí contento, muy contento)]: "¡Sí, contento!" NARR: "Abuela dice: ¡Qué bueno!"

**The turn**

5. Abuela sits down on the bench with an album open on her knees. Round the floor, four people (drawn from the family and the people of every place): one dances, one sings, one eats, one laughs. The card shows two places. The signs go.

**The game.** Abuela, her eyes on her album, asks for a photo of someone who does one of four things (drawn; round B's is not round A's; one of the two rounds is "ríe"). The four people are in the picture, each doing his thing. The frog touches one: a flash.

6. **Decision A, touch a person (four; tests ríe among baila, canta and come).** NARR: "Abuela dice: Quiero una foto de alguien que ríe." (Or, drawn: "...de alguien que baila." "...de alguien que canta." "...de alguien que come.")
   - Right: a flash; the photo slides out; abuela puts it in her album. NARR: "Abuela dice: ¡Qué bueno!" A star.
   - Wrong: a flash; the photo slides out; abuela looks at it. NARR: "Abuela dice: No, Coquí. Alguien que ríe." The photo goes into her pocket; one square of the camera's window goes dark. A cross. The sign is on the right person; he touches him.
7. **Decision B, touch a person (the same game; the four have changed places and things).** NARR: "Abuela dice: Quiero una foto de alguien que baila." (Drawn.) Right and wrong as in A.

**The ending**

8. Abuela turns the camera round: the last photo is of the frog himself.
   - Three squares were left: three photos in the album, and the frog's. Stars and a heart.
   - Two squares.
   - One square: the frog's photo only just comes out.
9. NARR: "Abuela dice: Coquí, ¿quieres bailar?" COQUÍ [TURN: claro (claro, claro que sí)]: "¡Claro!" They dance. NARR: "La fiesta es divertida. Todos ríen. ¡Qué bueno!" **(todos, ríen: heard)**

**Fewest hearings before a word can cost:** ríe 5 (steps 1, 2, 3). Not deciding: feliz 2 (steps 1, 4), divertido 2 with divertida (step 2) and once in the ending, ¡qué bueno! 2 (steps 3, 4) and in each right round.
**Forms:** divertida with divertido; ríen with ríe.
**A miss is filed under:** the thing that was asked for (ríe, baila, canta or come).
**Chance without the Spanish:** one in four, each round on its own.
**Speaking turns:** two (steps 4, 9): contento; claro.

# El lago: three stories (draft 1, before reviews)

Place 2, El parque. Scene 3 of 4. The learner is the little frog (Coquí). He has finished place 1, La casa (eleven stories), El camino (stories 12 to 14) and El campo (stories 15 to 17).

Words he has met (place 1): casa, puerta, ventana, abuela, hola, toca, abre, cierra, sí, no, grande, pequeña, adiós, silla, se sienta, vaso, jugo, agua, toma, quiero, por favor, gracias, de nada, ¿cómo estás?, bien, mamá, papá, abuelo, bebé, familia, hermano, hermana, él, ella, yo, tú, soy, eres, me llamo, niño, niña, juega, flor, sol, aquí, allá, amigo, ven, mira, vamos, ¿qué es esto?, hasta mañana.
Words he has met (El camino): perro, gato, árbol, parque, camina, corre, espera, rápido, despacio, hay, ¿dónde está?, uno, dos, tres.
Words he has met (El campo): bola, tira, coge, salta, dame, mi, tu, arriba, abajo, más, otra vez, tiene.
Heard along the way so far: pájaro, bizcocho, lluvia, mesa, balcón, sala, patio, portón, foto, hace calor, para, con, también, ahora, quiere, entra, come, está, están, dónde, quién, es, un, una, y, de, en, se llama, este, esta, a jugar, muy, va, por arriba, por abajo, no más, Luis, Carmen, Canelo.

Scene words for El lago (from the 40-scene list): pájaro, pato, pez, vuela, nada, rojo, azul, verde, amarillo, blanco, negro, ¿de qué color?, ¿cuál?

The picture: the pond of the park. Water fills the lower half of the picture. On the left bank, a short wooden dock. A wooden post stands in the water in the middle. Reeds grow along the right bank and behind them the water goes on out of sight. Sky and hills above. Luis (the boy, green shirt) and Carmen (the girl, braids, pink dress) are here. Canelo is asleep on the bank, tired out; he does nothing in this scene.

The animals:
- the duck (el pato): white, orange bill. It paddles on the water, and it can fly.
- the fish (el pez): orange. It swims under the clear water where we can see it, and it jumps.
- the bird (el pájaro): small and blue, the same kind as in the trees of El camino. It sits on the post, and it flies.

Voices: the narrator speaks to the learner. A person's lines are spoken by the narrator and start with who says it ("Carmen dice:"); the words show in a bubble at that person. The frog has his own voice (COQUÍ).

The signal for a right move is the same as always: chime, stars, the frog hops, then "¡Sí! ¡Muy bien!".
After a wrong move, always: the cost; what he did stays done; a line that says what was right ("No, Coquí." and then a whole sentence, true at the moment it is said); then the big sign shows the right one, he does it, and the right side plays.
Poking is free: anything he touches outside an answer is named ("Es un pato." "Es el agua." "Es Carmen.").
A decision's line is said once; a touch before it ends does not count; if he waits it plays again, free.
Nobody points with a hand or looks toward the answer in a decision. Nobody else moves until he has touched.

Answer bubbles over the frog, as before:
- **Speaking bubbles**: no words in them; each says its line aloud in his voice, one after the other; he picks by ear. Sides drawn fresh every time, also under the big sign.
- **Picture bubbles** (story 19): a picture he can pick. Places drawn fresh every time.

In the corner of the screen, from the turn on, a card with three places, one for each round of the game. A round done right fills its place with a star. A round done wrong fills it with the mark of what went wrong. Both stay.

## Story 18: Pan para el pato

- **Goal he can see, from the first frame:** Carmen has a paper bag of bread. A duck, a fish and a bird all watch the bag. Give each one its piece.
- **At stake:** the piece of bread in play. Given wrongly, another animal eats it, and the one it was for goes without. The card shows an eaten crust for that round. A right round shows a star.
- **New words:** pato, pez, pájaro, nada, vuela.
- **Back again:** agua, tira, arriba, hay, mira, quiere, para, tiene, ¿dónde está?
- **Heard along the way:** lago, pan, come.

**Start:** the frog stands at the end of the dock. Carmen stands beside him with the bag. Luis sits on the dock with his feet over the water. The duck paddles near the dock. The fish swims under the water in front of it. The bird sits on the post.

1. NARR: "Este es el lago." NARR: "Mira. Hay un pato." (The duck twinkles.) NARR: "El pato nada." NARR: "Hay un pez." (The fish jumps and twinkles.) NARR: "El pez nada." NARR: "Y hay un pájaro." (It twinkles.) The bird flies a loop over the pond and lands on the post again. NARR: "El pájaro vuela."
2. Carmen holds up the bag. NARR: "Carmen tiene pan." The duck quacks, the fish jumps, the bird hops on its post. NARR: "El pato quiere pan. El pez quiere pan. El pájaro quiere pan."

**The opening** (big sign, nothing can go wrong)

3. Carmen gives the frog a piece. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¡Pan para el pato!" The big sign is on the duck. He touches it. The frog throws the bread; the duck eats it. Right signal. NARR: "El pato come pan."
4. A new piece. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¡Pan para el pez!" The sign is on the fish. He touches it. The frog drops the bread on the water; the fish comes up and eats it. Right signal.
5. A new piece. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¡Pan para el pájaro!" The sign is on the bird. He touches it. The frog throws the bread up; the bird takes it in the air and goes back to its post. Right signal.
6. **He says what the duck does, taught.** Luis is busy with his shoe, head down. The duck beats its wings, takes off and flies round over the pond. NARR: "Luis dice: ¿Dónde está el pato?" Two speaking bubbles: "¡El pato vuela!" and "¡El pato nada!" The big sign is on "¡El pato vuela!" He touches it. COQUÍ: "¡El pato vuela!" Luis looks up. Right signal. NARR: "Luis dice: ¡Sí! ¡El pato vuela!"
7. The duck comes down on the water and paddles. Luis is at his shoe again. NARR: "Luis dice: ¿Y ahora? ¿Dónde está el pato?" The two speaking bubbles; the sign is on "¡El pato nada!" He touches it. COQUÍ: "¡El pato nada!" Right signal. NARR: "Luis dice: Sí. El pato nada."
8. **Bread for a duck he cannot see, taught.** The duck paddles away behind the reeds on the right. Carmen walks along the bank to where she can see behind them. A new piece of bread in the frog's hands. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¡El pato vuela! ¡Tira el pan arriba!" The big sign is on the sky above the reeds. He touches it. The frog throws the bread high; the duck flies up from behind the reeds and takes it. Right signal.
9. The duck comes down behind the reeds again. A new piece. NARR: "Carmen dice: Ahora el pato nada. ¡Tira el pan al agua!" The sign is on the water in front of the reeds. He touches it. The frog throws the bread onto the water; the duck paddles out and eats it. Right signal.

**The turn**

10. Luis takes a piece and throws it for the duck, without looking. It lands in front of the fish. The fish eats it. The duck looks at the fish, then at Luis, and turns its back. NARR: "¡Ay, no! El pez come el pan del pato." The card with three empty places shows in the corner. The duck paddles back to its place near the dock; the bird is on its post; the fish is under the water. Carmen comes back to the dock. The signs go away.

**The game** (no sign)

11. **Decision A, Touch (who the bread is for).** A new piece in the frog's hands. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¡Pan para el pez!" (Or "el pato", or "el pájaro". Drawn fresh, each as often as the others.) He touches the duck, the fish or the bird.
   - Right: the frog throws it to that one; it eats. Right signal. A star on the card.
   - Wrong: the frog throws it to the one he touched; that one eats it. The one it was for turns its back. A crust on the card. NARR: "No, Coquí. El pan es para el pez." (Or the one that was named.) A new piece. The sign; he touches the right one; it eats.
12. **Decision B, Touch (up or onto the water; he cannot see the duck).** The duck paddles away behind the reeds. Carmen walks along the bank to where she can see it. A new piece in the frog's hands. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¡El pato vuela!" (Or: "¡El pato nada!" Drawn fresh.) He touches the sky above the reeds or the water in front of them.
   - Right, "vuela": he throws it high; the duck flies up from behind the reeds and takes it. Right signal. A star.
   - Right, "nada": he throws it onto the water; the duck paddles out and eats it. Right signal. A star.
   - Wrong (he throws it onto the water; the duck is flying): the duck flies up from behind the reeds and circles, looking for it. NARR: "No, Coquí. El pato vuela." The fish comes up and eats the bread. A crust. A new piece; the duck goes back behind the reeds. The sign shows the sky; he touches it; the duck takes that one.
   - Wrong (he throws it high; the duck is on the water): the duck paddles out from behind the reeds and looks about on the water. NARR: "No, Coquí. El pato nada." The bird takes the bread in the air. A crust. A new piece; the duck goes back behind the reeds. The sign shows the water; he touches it; the duck eats that one.
13. **Decision C, Say, last (he sees the duck; Luis does not).** Carmen and the duck are back at the dock. Luis has a piece of bread in his hand and is tying his other shoe, head down. The duck does one of two things, drawn fresh: it takes off and flies round over the pond; or it paddles round the post. NARR: "Luis dice: ¿Dónde está el pato?" Two speaking bubbles: "¡El pato vuela!" and "¡El pato nada!"
   - Right: the frog says what it is doing. Luis, still looking down, throws the bread that way (high, or onto the water); the duck gets it. Right signal. A star.
   - Wrong: the frog says the other. Luis throws the bread the wrong way. NARR: "No, Coquí. El pato vuela." (Or "El pato nada.") The fish eats it (or the bird takes it). A crust. Luis takes a new piece; the duck does the same thing again. The sign shows the right bubble; he says it; Luis throws; the duck gets it.

**The ending** (the same lines in every ending)

14. By the stars on the card:
   - Three stars: the duck climbs onto the dock and sits down beside the frog; the bird lands on the frog's head; the fish jumps high in front of them. Stars and a heart.
   - One or two stars: the duck paddles up to the dock and looks at him; no heart.
   - No star: the duck stays out by the post with its back to him.
15. NARR: "El pato nada. El pez nada. Y el pájaro vuela." COQUÍ: "¡Adiós, pato!" The duck quacks.

**Fewest hearings before a word can cost:** pato 9 (steps 1 twice, 2, 3 twice, 6 three times and more), pez 5 (steps 1 twice, 2, 4, 10), pájaro 4 (steps 1 twice, 2, 5), vuela 6 (step 1; step 6 four times: the bubble twice, the frog, Luis; step 8), nada 7 (step 1 twice; step 6, the bubble; step 7 three times; step 9).

**A miss is filed under:** A: the animal that was named (pato, pez or pájaro). B and C: vuela or nada, the one that was true.

## Story 19: Los botes

- **Goal he can see, from the first frame:** three little toy boats on the dock, and Carmen waiting on the far bank with her arms out. Send the boats across to her.
- **At stake:** the boat in play. Sent wrongly, a boat drifts into the reeds and sticks there. It stays there. The card shows a boat in reeds for that round. A right round shows a star.
- **New words:** rojo, azul, verde, ¿de qué color?
- **Back again:** pez, pato, nada, dame, mi, tu, hay, tres, mira, va, es.
- **Heard along the way:** bote, pinta.

**Start:** the frog and Luis on the dock. Three toy boats stand in a row on the dock: each a small hull with one sail, the whole boat one colour: red, blue, green. A fourth boat, bare wood with a white sail, stands behind them, with three small pots of paint (red, blue, green) and a brush. Carmen stands on the far bank, on the right, in front of the reeds. The duck paddles by the post.

1. NARR: "Mira. Hay tres botes." (They twinkle one by one.) NARR: "Un bote rojo. Un bote azul. Y un bote verde."

**The opening** (big sign, nothing can go wrong)

2. NARR: "Luis dice: Coquí, ¡dame el bote rojo!" The big sign is on the red boat. He touches it. The frog pushes it to Luis; Luis sets it on the water and blows; it sails across to Carmen, who lifts it out. Right signal. NARR: "El bote rojo va."
3. NARR: "Luis dice: ¡Dame el bote azul!" The sign is on the blue boat. He touches it. It sails across. Right signal.
4. NARR: "Luis dice: ¡Dame el bote verde!" The sign is on the green boat. He touches it. It sails across. Right signal. Carmen puts all three boats in a little cart and wheels them back round the pond to the dock. They stand in their row again (the order drawn fresh).
5. **He says a colour, taught.** A fish jumps in front of the dock: a red one. Carmen, wheeling the cart, has her back to the water. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¡Un pez! ¿De qué color es el pez?" Three speaking bubbles: "¡Es rojo!", "¡Es azul!", "¡Es verde!" The big sign is on "¡Es rojo!" He touches it. COQUÍ: "¡Es rojo!" Right signal. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¡Un pez rojo!"
6. Another fish jumps: a blue one. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¿Y este pez? ¿De qué color es?" The three speaking bubbles; the sign is on "¡Es azul!" He touches it. COQUÍ: "¡Es azul!" Right signal.
7. **He paints, taught.** Luis picks up the bare boat. NARR: "Luis dice: Mira mi bote. ¿De qué color? ¡Mi bote es verde!" The big sign is on the pot of green paint. He touches it. The frog takes the brush, dips it in the green pot and paints: the boat is green. Right signal. Luis sails it across to Carmen. A new bare boat stands by the pots.

**The turn**

8. Luis sets the red boat on the water and blows too hard, to one side. The boat turns, drifts along the bank into the reeds and sticks there. NARR: "¡Ay, no! El bote rojo." It stays there. Carmen, on the far bank now, lifts her empty hands. Luis fetches another red boat from his bag for the row. The card with three empty places shows in the corner. The signs go away.

**The game** (no sign)

9. **Decision A, Touch (which boat).** The three boats stand in their row (the order drawn fresh). NARR: "Luis dice: Coquí, ¡dame el bote azul!" (Or "rojo", or "verde". Drawn fresh, each as often as the others.) He touches a boat.
   - Right: the frog pushes it to Luis; it sails across to Carmen. Right signal. A star.
   - Wrong: the frog pushes the boat he touched to Luis. Luis, not looking, sets it on the water; Carmen shakes her head and does not reach for it; it drifts into the reeds and sticks. A boat in reeds on the card. NARR: "No, Coquí. Luis dice: ¡El bote azul!" (The colour that was called.) Luis takes another boat of the lost colour from his bag for the row. The sign; he touches the right boat; it sails across.
10. **Decision B, Say (he sees the fish; Carmen does not).** A fish jumps in front of the dock: red, blue or green, drawn fresh. Carmen is lifting a boat out of the water, her head down. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¡Un pez! ¿De qué color es el pez?" Three speaking bubbles: "¡Es rojo!", "¡Es azul!", "¡Es verde!"
   - Right: Carmen sets the boat of that colour on the water for the fish; the fish swims under it and pushes it across to the dock. Right signal. A star.
   - Wrong: Carmen sets the boat of the colour he said on the water. The fish does not come to it. The boat drifts into the reeds and sticks. A boat in reeds. NARR: "No, Coquí. El pez es azul." (Its colour.) The fish jumps again. The sign shows the right bubble; he says it; the fish pushes that boat across.
11. **Decision C, Touch, last (he paints).** Luis picks up the bare boat. NARR: "Luis dice: ¿De qué color? ¡Mi bote es rojo!" (Or "azul", or "verde". Drawn fresh.) He touches one of the three pots.
   - Right: the frog paints the boat that colour. Luis sails it across to Carmen. Right signal. A star.
   - Wrong: the frog paints the boat the colour of the pot he touched. It stays that colour. Luis looks at it and shakes his head; he sets it on the water and it drifts into the reeds and sticks. A boat in reeds. NARR: "No, Coquí. Luis dice: ¡Mi bote es rojo!" (The colour he said.) A new bare boat. The sign; he touches the right pot; the frog paints it; it sails across.

**The ending** (the same lines in every ending)

12. By the stars on the card:
   - Three stars: only the first red boat is in the reeds. All the others sail back across the pond in a line toward the dock; the duck swims behind them. Stars and a heart.
   - One or two stars: one or two more boats are stuck in the reeds; the rest sail back.
   - No star: four boats are stuck in the reeds. Carmen walks round and starts to pull them out.
13. NARR: "Un bote rojo. Un bote azul. Un bote verde." COQUÍ: "¡Adiós, botes!"

**Fewest hearings before a word can cost:** rojo 5 (steps 1, 2 twice, 5 three times and more), azul 5 (steps 1, 3, 6 three times), verde 4 (steps 1, 4, 7 twice; and the bubble in steps 5 and 6), ¿de qué color? 4 (steps 5, 6, 7; and it is the question, not the answer).

**A miss is filed under:** the colour that was called or was true (rojo, azul or verde).

## Story 20: ¿Cuál?

- **Goal he can see, from the first frame:** three ducks out on the water, a white one, a black one and a small yellow one, and bread in the frog's hands. Bring the ducks to the dock.
- **At stake:** how near the ducks are. Each wrong move, all three paddle a step further out toward the post. It stays. The card shows a ring of water for that round. A right round shows a star, and the ducks come a step nearer.
- **New words:** amarillo, blanco, negro, ¿cuál?
- **Back again:** pato, pájaro, pez, rojo, azul, verde, ¿de qué color?, vuela, nada, tres, hay, para, sí.
- **Heard along the way:** pan, el pato blanco (the colour after the thing).

**Start:** the frog and Carmen on the dock, the bag of bread between them. Luis sits on the bank with Canelo's head on his knee. Three ducks paddle half-way between the dock and the post: one white, one black, one small and yellow.

1. NARR: "Mira. Hay tres patos." NARR: "Un pato blanco." (It twinkles.) NARR: "Un pato negro." (It twinkles.) NARR: "Y un pato amarillo." (It twinkles.)

**The opening** (big sign, nothing can go wrong)

2. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¿Cuál es el pato blanco?" The big sign is on the white duck. He touches it. The frog throws it bread; it eats. Right signal. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¡Sí! El pato blanco."
3. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¿Cuál es el pato negro?" The sign is on the black duck. He touches it. Bread; it eats. Right signal.
4. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¿Y cuál es el pato amarillo?" The sign is on the yellow one. He touches it. Bread; it eats. Right signal.
5. **He says a colour, taught.** A bird lands on the post: a yellow one. Luis is scratching Canelo's ears and does not look up. NARR: "Luis dice: ¡Un pájaro! ¿De qué color es?" Three speaking bubbles: "¡Es blanco!", "¡Es negro!", "¡Es amarillo!" The big sign is on "¡Es amarillo!" He touches it. COQUÍ: "¡Es amarillo!" Right signal. The bird flies off.
6. A white bird lands on the post. NARR: "Luis dice: ¿Y este pájaro? ¿De qué color es?" The three speaking bubbles; the sign is on "¡Es blanco!" He touches it. COQUÍ: "¡Es blanco!" Right signal. The bird flies off.
7. **He asks which one, taught.** Carmen gives the frog a piece of bread. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¡Pan para el pato!" The three ducks all look up. Two speaking bubbles: "¿Cuál?" and "¡Sí!" The big sign is on "¿Cuál?" He touches it. COQUÍ: "¿Cuál?" NARR: "Carmen dice: El pato negro." The frog throws it to the black duck; it eats. Right signal.
8. Another piece. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¡Pan para el pato amarillo!" The two speaking bubbles; the sign is on "¡Sí!" He touches it. COQUÍ: "¡Sí!" The frog throws it to the yellow duck; it eats. Right signal.

**The turn**

9. Luis gets up, takes a piece and throws it hard, without a word. It splashes down between the ducks. They flap, quack, and paddle away a long step toward the post. NARR: "¡Ay, Luis!" The card with three empty places shows in the corner. The signs go away.

**The game** (no sign)

10. **Decision A, Touch (which duck).** The three ducks paddle about and stop in a new order. A piece of bread in the frog's hands. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¿Cuál es el pato negro?" (Or "blanco", or "amarillo". Drawn fresh, each as often as the others.) He touches a duck.
   - Right: the frog throws it bread; it eats; all three come a step nearer the dock. Right signal. A star.
   - Wrong: the frog throws bread to the duck he touched; the other two flap, and all three paddle a step further out. A ring of water on the card. NARR: "No, Coquí. Este es el pato negro." (The right duck twinkles.) A new piece. The sign; he touches it; it eats.
11. **Decision B, Say (he sees the bird; Luis does not).** A bird lands on the post: white, black or yellow, drawn fresh. Luis is scratching Canelo's ears. NARR: "Luis dice: ¡Un pájaro! ¿De qué color es?" Three speaking bubbles: "¡Es blanco!", "¡Es negro!", "¡Es amarillo!"
   - Right: Luis looks up and nods. The bird flies down to the ducks and they follow it a step nearer the dock. Right signal. A star.
   - Wrong: Luis looks up and shakes his head. NARR: "No, Coquí. El pájaro es blanco." (Its colour.) The bird flies off over the ducks' heads; they duck, and paddle a step further out. A ring of water. Another bird of the same colour lands. The sign shows the right bubble; he says it; the bird flies down to the ducks.
12. **Decision C, Say, last (does he know which duck?).** Carmen gives the frog a piece of bread. One of two lines, drawn fresh. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¡Pan para el pato!" (which duck is not said). Or: "Carmen dice: ¡Pan para el pato blanco!" (or "negro", or "amarillo"). Two speaking bubbles: "¿Cuál?" and "¡Sí!"
   - Right ("¿Cuál?" when she did not say which): NARR: "Carmen dice: El pato blanco." (One of the three.) The frog throws it to that duck; it eats; all three come a step nearer. Right signal. A star.
   - Right ("¡Sí!" when she said which): the frog throws it to that duck; it eats; all three come a step nearer. Right signal. A star.
   - Wrong ("¡Sí!" when she did not say which): the frog throws the bread out among the ducks; all three go for it at once, flap and quack, and paddle a step further out. A ring of water. NARR: "No, Coquí. ¿Cuál pato?" The sign shows "¿Cuál?"; he says it; Carmen says which; that duck eats.
   - Wrong ("¿Cuál?" when she said which): Carmen puts her hands on her hips. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¡El pato blanco, Coquí!" (The colour she had said.) While he waits, the ducks give up and paddle a step further out. A ring of water. The sign shows "¡Sí!"; he says it; the frog throws; that duck eats.

**The ending** (the same lines in every ending)

13. By the stars on the card:
   - Three stars: the three ducks climb onto the dock and sit in a row beside the frog. Stars and a heart.
   - One or two stars: the ducks are near the dock, on the water.
   - No star: the ducks are far out by the post.
14. NARR: "Un pato blanco. Un pato negro. Y un pato amarillo." NARR: "Carmen dice: ¿Cuál es tu pato, Coquí?" COQUÍ: "¡El pato amarillo!" The small yellow duck quacks.

**Fewest hearings before a word can cost:** blanco 5 (steps 1, 2 twice, 6 twice and more), negro 4 (steps 1, 3, 7; and the bubble in steps 5 and 6), amarillo 6 (steps 1, 4, 5 three times, 8), ¿cuál? 6 (steps 2, 3, 4; step 7 three times: the bubble twice and the frog).

**A miss is filed under:** A and B: the colour that was called or was true (blanco, negro or amarillo). C: ¿cuál?

## For the owner, one line each

- "Lago" is said for the pond, as on your list.
- Colours come after the thing, as in Spanish: "el bote rojo".
- "¿Cuál?" is taught as the thing to say when you were not told which one.

# A comer: the orchestrator's decisions on speaking turns (Nov 17)

From the proposal in comer-turnos.md. His ask (Nov 16) says every story from A comer on has speaking turns with earlier words; docs/hablar/03-para-historias-nuevas.md says 2 to 4 per story.

## Reading of the rule
- "Never a word that is new in this story" applies to the targets. A turn line may carry a word new in this story if it has been heard in this play before the turn; that word is not a target.

## Kept
- 34: c34_cq_lleno ("Yo estoy lleno.", signed ask, step 4, target lleno). A turn fires only when lleno is open for speaking, so the third hearing it replaces is not needed by a learner who already has the word; on a first play nothing changes.
- 34: c34_cq_bp_papa ("¡Buen provecho, papá!", ending, target papá).

## Not kept
- 35: c35_cq_mucho ("Sí, mucho, por favor.", target sí). In an ask turn the sign and bubbles are hidden, and the other option "Sí, un poco, por favor." also holds "sí": a wrong amount would count as heard right. A target must tell the two options apart, and the words that do (mucho, un poco) are new in this story.
- 35: c35_cq_huele ("¡Huele a café!"). A remark, not a reply (03 lists answering, greeting, thanking, asking, sí or no).

## So: new lines
33 has none, 34 two, 35 none. Add new short coquí lines to the script, so that each story has 2 or 3 turns in all:
- 33: two or three; 35: two or three; 34: none or one more.
- Each new line: a natural reply inside the scene, set up by the line just before it (a character greets him, thanks him, offers him something, asks him a yes/no question, asks his name); 1 to 5 words; its targets are words in focus_words of a story before A comer; never inside a decide; never next to another turn; not an echo of the line just heard; the coquí says it as a normal recorded clip ("who": "tu").
- A new line must not change any count, any draw, or which answer is right in any round; it must not give a hearing of a deciding word before the round (unless the line is outside the run-up to that round, e.g. in the ending), and must not hint at an answer.
- Prefer moments the story already has (a character handing him something, a greeting when someone arrives, the ending); add the smallest set-up line where none exists.
- New clips are recorded on Azure; keep the new characters small and give the count.

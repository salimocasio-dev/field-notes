# La sala: three stories (draft 3, after three outside reviews)

Place 1, La casa. Scene 3 of 4. The learner is the little frog (the coquí), a guest in abuela's house. This is abuela's family; nothing on screen or in the Spanish says it is his.
He has played five stories: three at the front of the house, two on the porch (El jugo, ¡Qué calor!).

Words he has met so far: casa, puerta, ventana, abuela, hola, toca, abre, cierra, sí, no, grande, pequeña, adiós, silla, se sienta, vaso, jugo, agua, toma, quiero, por favor, gracias, de nada, ¿cómo estás?, bien.
Heard along the way so far: mira, puedes, dónde está, también, ahora, quiere, quieres, tiene, entra, pájaro, bizcocho, palma, lluvia, tú, y tú, o, mesa, balcón, hace calor, dos, un, y, de, pone, pequeño, soy yo.

Scene words for La sala (from the 40-scene list): mamá, papá, abuelo, hermano, hermana, bebé, familia, yo, tú, él, ella, soy / eres / es, me llamo.

The picture: abuela's living room. A warm wall with three picture frames, the door to the porch on the left, a window on the right. A long sofa, abuelo's armchair, a floor lamp, a low table, the baby's blanket on the tile floor. The family are people, like abuela: mamá (long hair, red dress) and papá (short hair, blue shirt) on the sofa, abuelo (white hair at the sides, a moustache, glasses) in his armchair, the baby on his blanket, and from story 7 a boy and a girl of the same size (the boy: short hair, green shirt; the girl: two braids, a pink dress).

Voices: the narrator speaks to the learner. A person's lines are spoken by the narrator and start with who says it ("Mamá dice:", "Abuelo dice:"); the words show in a bubble at that person. The frog has his own voice (COQUÍ).

The signal for a right move is the same as always: chime, stars, the frog hops, then "¡Sí! ¡Muy bien!". It plays after every right answer, also where it is not written out.
Poking is free: anything he touches outside an answer is named ("Esta es mamá." "Es el sofá." "Es la butaca de abuelo.").
A decision's line is said once; a touch before it ends does not count; if he waits it plays again, free.

## Story 6: La familia

- **Goal he can see, from the first frame:** a covered cake steams on the low table. The frog sniffs at it. A cake for everyone, and three slices for him.
- **At stake:** his own three slices (they show on a plate in the top corner, with his face beside it). The cake is cut in seven: three on his plate, four on the dish for mamá, papá, abuelo and the baby. He carries each slice to the person named. Give it to someone else and that person takes one bite; abuela puts the bitten slice on HIS plate and takes a whole one from his plate to the person who was named.
- **New words:** mamá, papá, abuelo, bebé, familia.
- **Back again:** abuela, bizcocho, toma, gracias, de nada, quiere, pequeño, hola, ¿cómo estás?, bien, no, es.
- **Heard along the way:** sala, para, este, esta, come, y, ahora no, está caliente.

**Start:** the family is in the room. Abuelo dozes in his armchair. The frog hops in through the door.

1. NARR: "Esta es la sala." The frog goes straight to the cake and sniffs (a slice of cake in his bubble). Abuela wags a finger. NARR: "Abuela dice: Ahora no. Está caliente." (A line he knows from El bizcocho.)
2. COQUÍ: "¡Hola!" NARR: "Mira. Esta es la familia de abuela."

**The opening** (big sign, nothing can go wrong)

3. NARR: "Esta es mamá." (She twinkles.) "Y este es papá." (He twinkles.) Then: "¿Puedes tocar a mamá?"
   - He touches mamá. Right signal. She blows him a kiss (a heart). NARR: "Mamá dice: ¡Hola!"
4. NARR: "¿Y papá? ¿Dónde está papá?"
   - He touches papá. Right signal. NARR: "Papá dice: ¡Hola! ¿Cómo estás?" COQUÍ: "¡Bien, gracias!"
5. NARR: "Mira: abuela y abuelo." (Each twinkles in turn.) Then: "¿Puedes tocar a abuelo?"
   - He touches abuelo. Right signal. Abuelo wakes with a start; his glasses jump. NARR: "Abuelo dice: ¡Hola!"
6. NARR: "Y este es el bebé." (The baby twinkles and claps.) "El bebé es pequeño." Then: "¿Dónde está el bebé?"
   - He touches the baby. Right signal. The baby giggles.

**The turn**

7. Abuela lifts the cover off the cake: seven slices. NARR: "¡Mira! Abuela tiene un bizcocho. Bizcocho para la familia." COQUÍ: "¡Mmm! ¡Bizcocho!" Three slices fly to the frog's own plate in the top corner; four stay on the dish. The signs go away.

**The game** (no sign; one guess a round). Who is named in each round is drawn fresh; nobody is named twice. Abuela puts a slice in the frog's hands each time. She stands at her place facing the frog and never turns or points toward anyone; her bubble holds no picture. Only people who have not been served yet can be answers.

8. **Round 1, Do (four people).** NARR: "Abuela dice: Este es para papá." (Or "para mamá", "para abuelo", "para el bebé".)
   - Right: the frog hops over and gives it to him. Right signal. Papá takes one happy bite and keeps the slice on his plate. NARR: "Papá dice: Gracias." COQUÍ: "De nada." (The baby, when it is his, squeals and claps; no line.)
   - Wrong: he gives it to, say, mamá. She takes one bite and stops. NARR: "No. Esa es mamá." (Or "Ese es papá." "Ese es abuelo." "Ese es el bebé.") Abuela puts the bitten slice on the frog's plate and carries a whole slice from his plate to papá. NARR: "Abuela dice: Toma, papá." His plate still holds three, but one of them now has a bite out of it.
9. **Round 2, Do (three people left).** A new sentence. NARR: "Ahora, abuelo quiere bizcocho." (Or "Ahora, mamá quiere bizcocho." "Ahora, papá quiere bizcocho." "Ahora, el bebé quiere bizcocho.")
   - Right and wrong as in round 1.
10. **Round 3, Do (the two left; both are named).** Which sentence comes first is drawn fresh. NARR: "Abuela dice: Este no es para mamá." Then: "Abuela dice: Es para el bebé." (Or, the other way round: "Abuela dice: Este es para el bebé." Then: "Abuela dice: No es para mamá.")
   - Right and wrong as before.
11. Abuela carries the last slice on the dish to the one who has had none. NARR: "Abuela dice: Y este es para mamá." (Whoever that is.)

**The ending** (the same lines in every ending)

12. Abuela brings the frog his plate and the drink he picked in ¡Qué calor! (juice if nothing was saved). NARR: "Abuela dice: Toma. Bizcocho y jugo." (Or "Bizcocho y agua.")
   - Three whole slices: he eats all three; his belly is round. Stars and a heart.
   - Two or one whole: he eats them, bitten ones and all.
   - None whole: three bitten slices. He looks at them. The baby crawls over and holds out a piece of his own. He eats. It is funny. It is not a fail screen.
13. COQUÍ: "¡Gracias, abuela!" NARR: "La familia come bizcocho." Now everyone eats their slice together. Abuela stands by with the empty dish.

**Fewest hearings of each new word on a run with no mistakes:** mamá 4, papá 5, abuelo 4, bebé 4, familia 3.

## Story 7: La foto

- **Goal he can see:** three empty picture frames on the wall. Abuelo has an old box camera, the kind you look down into.
- **At stake:** the three frames. Each round one photo is taken, right or wrong, and hangs for good. A wrong one hangs crooked.
- **New words:** hermano, hermana, él, ella.
- **Back again:** abuelo, mamá, papá, bebé, familia, es, sí, no, hola.
- **Heard along the way:** foto, con, una, ahora, tres, del.

**Start:** the family is in the room. A boy and a girl run in and hug the baby on his blanket.

1. NARR: "Mira. Este es el hermano del bebé." (The boy twinkles.) "Y esta es la hermana del bebé." (The girl twinkles.)

**The opening** (big sign, nothing can go wrong)

2. NARR: "¿Dónde está el hermano? ¿Puedes tocar al hermano?"
   - He touches the boy. Right signal. The boy waves. NARR: "Él dice: ¡Hola!"
3. NARR: "¿Y la hermana? ¿Dónde está la hermana?"
   - He touches the girl. Right signal. She waves. NARR: "Ella dice: ¡Hola!"
4. NARR: "Él es papá." (Papá twinkles.) "Ella es mamá." (Mamá twinkles.)
5. **The photo, taught.** Abuelo gets up with his camera and looks down into it. The boy and the girl stand side by side. NARR: "Abuelo dice: Una foto con ella." The big sign is on the girl. He touches her. The frog hops beside her. A flash. A small photo of the two pops up. Right signal.
6. NARR: "Abuelo dice: Ahora, una foto con él." The big sign is on the boy. He touches him. The frog hops beside him. A flash. Right signal.

**The turn**

7. The three empty frames on the wall twinkle. Abuelo winds his camera. NARR: "Abuelo quiere tres fotos de la familia." The signs go away.

**The game** (no sign; one guess a round). Abuelo looks down into the camera, so his eyes never point at anyone.

8. **Round 1, Do (hermano or hermana).** The boy and the girl stand apart; who is on which side is drawn fresh. NARR: "Abuelo dice: Una foto con la hermana." (Or "con el hermano". Drawn fresh.)
   - Right: the frog hops beside her. A flash. Right signal. The photo of the frog and the girl flies into the first frame and hangs straight.
   - Wrong: the frog hops beside the boy. Abuelo swings the camera to the girl and shoots. NARR: "No. Él es el hermano. Ella es la hermana." (Or "No. Ella es la hermana. Él es el hermano.") She waves. The photo shows her alone, with only the frog's foot at the edge. It flies into the frame and hangs crooked.
9. **Round 2, Do (él or ella).** Mamá and papá stand up side by side; sides drawn fresh. NARR: "Abuelo dice: Ahora, una foto con él." (Or "con ella". Drawn fresh.)
   - Right and wrong as in round 1. Wrong: "No. Ella es mamá. Él es papá." (Or "No. Él es papá. Ella es mamá.") The photo shows the right one alone, the frog's foot at the edge, and hangs crooked.
10. **Round 3, Say (sí or no).** One of the two children stands alone in the middle (which one is drawn fresh). A small square by the camera shows what the camera sees: a blur. Abuelo turns the lens. While he asks, a free touch on that child gets a wave and "¡Hola!", not the child's name. The two answer bubbles show: the frog nodding, the frog shaking his head. NARR: "Abuelo dice: ¿Es el hermano? ¿Sí o no?" (Or "¿Es la hermana?" Drawn fresh, apart from who is standing there.)
   - Right: COQUÍ: "¡Sí!" (or "¡No!"). Right signal. The square comes sharp. A flash. That child's photo hangs straight in the third frame.
   - Wrong: the frog says the other answer. The square comes sharp on the child. NARR: "No. Es la hermana." (Or "Sí. Es el hermano." and so on: it says who it is.) Abuelo looks up over the camera, puzzled, and it goes off at his feet. A photo of his two slippers hangs crooked in the third frame.

**The ending** (the same lines in every ending)

11. The wall: straight photos twinkle; crooked ones swing a little. Three straight: stars and a heart.
12. Abuelo sets the camera on the low table and shuffles in. Everyone gathers, the frog in front. NARR: "Abuelo dice: Ahora, una foto de la familia." A big flash. COQUÍ: "¡Familia!"
13. The three photos stay on the wall, as he made them, in the next story.

**Fewest hearings of each new word on a run with no mistakes:** hermano 4, hermana 4, él 3, ella 3 before round 1; whichever are drawn in the rounds get one more each.

## Story 8: ¿Quién eres tú?

- **Before it starts, one line in English on the opening card** (the plan's rule for a new pattern; text only, never spoken, gone at the first touch): "Yo soy: I am. Tú eres: you are. Me llamo: my name is."
- **Goal he can see:** abuelo has lost his glasses. They are pushed up on top of his own head, in plain view from the first frame. Without them the room is a blur and he cannot tell who is who. Help him.
- **At stake:** abuelo's cup of coffee, three sips full (it shows in the top corner). A wrong answer makes him laugh or turn, and coffee slops out of the cup. Every answer that is right the first time puts a heart beside the cup, and it stays.
- **New words:** yo, tú, soy, eres, me llamo.
- **Back again:** él, ella, es, hermano, hermana, mamá, papá, abuelo, hola, sí, dónde.
- **Heard along the way:** quién, Carmen, Luis, de, mis espejuelos.

The speaking bubbles from ¡Qué calor! are used here: two bubbles over the frog, no words in them; each says its line aloud in his voice, one after the other; he picks by ear. Sides are drawn fresh.

**Start:** the boy and the girl are with the frog. The three photos from La foto hang on the wall as he made them. In the background abuelo wanders about with a cup of coffee, his glasses up on his head, looking for them.

Which child leads the opening is drawn fresh; the other child plays decision B. Written here with the girl leading.

1. NARR: "La hermana dice: Hola. Me llamo Carmen. Soy la hermana de Luis."
2. NARR: "El hermano dice: Y yo me llamo Luis. Soy el hermano de Carmen."

**The opening** (big sign, nothing can go wrong)

3. NARR: "La hermana dice: ¿Y tú? ¿Quién eres tú?" Two speaking bubbles: "Yo soy Coquí." and "Yo soy Carmen." The big sign is on "Yo soy Coquí." He touches it. COQUÍ: "Yo soy Coquí. Me llamo Coquí." Right signal.
4. NARR: "La hermana dice: ¡Ah! Tú eres Coquí." She points at him.
5. She points at herself. NARR: "La hermana dice: ¿Y yo? ¿Quién soy yo?" Two speaking bubbles: "Tú eres Carmen." and "Yo soy Carmen." The big sign is on "Tú eres Carmen." He touches it. COQUÍ: "Tú eres Carmen." Right signal. NARR: "La hermana dice: ¡Sí! Yo soy Carmen."

**The turn**

6. Abuelo comes forward, bumps the low table, and laughs at himself. For a moment the whole picture goes to a blur: this is what he sees. NARR: "Abuelo dice: ¡Ay! ¿Dónde están mis espejuelos?" The cup appears in the top corner. The signs go away.

**The game** (no sign). Decisions A and B come in an order drawn fresh; C is last.

7. **Say decision A: abuelo bends down to the frog.** NARR: "Abuelo dice: ¿Quién eres tú?" Two speaking bubbles: "Yo soy Coquí." and "Tú eres Coquí."
   - Right: COQUÍ: "Yo soy Coquí." Right signal. NARR: "Abuelo dice: ¡Ah! Tú eres Coquí." He pats the frog's head.
   - Wrong: COQUÍ: "Tú eres Coquí." Abuelo taps his own chest and laughs. NARR: "Abuelo dice: ¿Yo? No. Yo soy abuelo." The laugh slops coffee out; the cup shows one sip less. The sign shows the right bubble; it speaks again; he touches it. COQUÍ: "Yo soy Coquí." NARR: "Abuelo dice: ¡Ah! Tú eres Coquí."
8. **Say decision B: guess who.** The other child (here the boy) creeps up behind the frog and covers his eyes. NARR: "El hermano dice: ¿Quién soy yo?" Two speaking bubbles: "Tú eres Luis." and "Yo soy Luis."
   - Right: COQUÍ: "Tú eres Luis." Right signal. He laughs and lets go. NARR: "El hermano dice: ¡Sí! Yo soy Luis."
   - Wrong: COQUÍ: "Yo soy Luis." The boy giggles and keeps his hands there. Abuelo turns to the frog. NARR: "Abuelo dice: ¡Hola, Luis!" Coffee slops out as he turns. The sign shows the right bubble; it speaks; he touches it. COQUÍ: "Tú eres Luis." NARR: "El hermano dice: ¡Sí! Yo soy Luis."
9. **Say decision C, last: él or ella.** Mamá and papá stand side by side (sides drawn fresh). Abuelo peers at them. NARR: "Abuelo dice: ¿Quién es mamá?" (Or "¿Quién es papá?" Drawn fresh.) Two speaking bubbles: "Ella es mamá." and "Él es mamá." (Or "Él es papá." and "Ella es papá.")
   - Right: COQUÍ: "Ella es mamá." Right signal. Abuelo walks over to her. NARR: "Abuelo dice: ¡Hola, mamá!"
   - Wrong: COQUÍ: "Él es mamá." Abuelo walks to papá. NARR: "Abuelo dice: ¡Hola, mamá!" Papá laughs; abuelo laughs; coffee slops out. The sign shows the right bubble; it speaks; he touches it. COQUÍ: "Ella es mamá." Abuelo goes to her. NARR: "Abuelo dice: ¡Hola, mamá!"

**The ending** (the same lines in every ending)

10. The baby, on mamá's arm, reaches up and pulls abuelo's glasses down onto his nose. Abuelo blinks. Everything is sharp. NARR: "Abuelo dice: ¡Ah! ¡Mis espejuelos!"
11. By the cup:
   - Full: he drinks his coffee with a sigh. Stars and a heart.
   - One or two sips left: he drinks what there is.
   - Empty: he turns the cup upside down; one drop falls. Abuela brings him a fresh one, laughing.
12. NARR: "Abuelo dice: Y tú eres Coquí." COQUÍ: "¡Sí! Yo soy Coquí. Me llamo Coquí."

**Fewest hearings on a run with no mistakes, not counting the bubbles reading themselves out:** yo 9, tú 8, soy 9, eres 7, me llamo 4.

**A miss is filed under:** story 6, the person named in rounds 1 and 2, "no" in round 3; story 7, round 1 hermano or hermana, round 2 él or ella, round 3 hermano or hermana; story 8, A under yo, B under tú, C under él or ella.

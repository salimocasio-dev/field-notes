# La sala: three stories (draft 1, for review)

Place 1, La casa. Scene 3 of 4. The learner is the little frog (the coquí). Abuela is his grandmother.
He has played five stories: three at the front of the house, two on the porch (El jugo, ¡Qué calor!).

Words he has met so far: casa, puerta, ventana, abuela, hola, toca, abre, cierra, sí, no, grande, pequeña, adiós, silla, se sienta, vaso, jugo, agua, toma, quiero, por favor, gracias, de nada, ¿cómo estás?, bien.
Heard along the way so far: mira, puedes, dónde está, también, ahora, quiere, quieres, tiene, entra, pájaro, bizcocho, palma, lluvia, tú, y tú, o, mesa, balcón, hace calor, dos, un, y, de, pone, pequeño.

Scene words for La sala (from the 40-scene list): mamá, papá, abuelo, hermano, hermana, bebé, familia, yo, tú, él, ella, soy / eres / es, me llamo.

The picture: abuela's living room. A warm wall with three picture frames, a window on the right, the door to the porch on the left. A long sofa, abuelo's armchair, a rug, a low table. The family are people, like abuela: mamá and papá (grown-ups, on the sofa), abuelo (white hair, a moustache, glasses, in his armchair), the baby (on the rug), and from story 7 a boy and a girl of the same size (el hermano: short hair, blue shirt; la hermana: two braids, a pink dress).

Voices: the narrator speaks to the learner. A person's lines are spoken by the narrator and start with who says it ("Mamá dice:", "Abuelo dice:"); their bubble shows. The frog has his own voice (COQUÍ).

The signal for a right move is the same as always: chime, stars, the frog hops, then "¡Sí! ¡Muy bien!". It plays after every right answer, also where it is not written out.
Poking is free: anything he touches outside an answer is named ("Esta es mamá." "Es el sofá.").
A decision's line is said once; a touch before it ends does not count; if he waits it plays again, free.

## Story 6: La familia

- **Goal he can see:** abuela has baked a cake. A slice for everyone, and a plate of three slices kept for the frog (it shows in the top corner, with his face beside it).
- **At stake:** his own three slices. He carries each slice to the person abuela names. Give it to someone else and that person eats it; abuela then takes a slice off HIS plate for the one who was left out.
- **New words:** mamá, papá, abuelo, bebé, familia.
- **Back again:** abuela, bizcocho, toma, gracias, de nada, quiere, pequeño, hola.
- **Heard along the way:** sala, para, este, esta, come, y.

**Start:** the family is in the room. The frog hops in through the door.

1. NARR: "Esta es la sala."
2. COQUÍ: "¡Hola!" Everyone waves. NARR: "Mira. Esta es la familia de abuela."

**The opening** (big sign, nothing can go wrong)

3. NARR: "Esta es mamá." (She twinkles and waves.) "Y este es papá." (He twinkles and waves.) Then: "¿Puedes tocar a mamá?"
   - He touches mamá. Right signal. NARR: "Mamá dice: ¡Hola!"
4. NARR: "¿Y papá? ¿Dónde está papá?"
   - He touches papá. Right signal. NARR: "Papá dice: ¡Hola!"
5. NARR: "Mira. Este es abuelo." (He twinkles, in his armchair.) "Abuela y abuelo." (Each twinkles in turn.) Then: "¿Puedes tocar a abuelo?"
   - He touches abuelo. Right signal. NARR: "Abuelo dice: ¡Hola!"
6. NARR: "Y este es el bebé." (The baby twinkles and claps.) "El bebé es pequeño." Then: "¿Dónde está el bebé?"
   - He touches the baby. Right signal. The baby giggles.

**The turn**

7. Abuela lifts the cover off a cake on the low table. NARR: "¡Mira! Abuela tiene bizcocho. Bizcocho para la familia." COQUÍ: "¡Mmm! ¡Bizcocho!" The frog's own plate, with three slices, appears in the top corner. The signs go away.

**The game** (no sign; one guess a round). Three rounds. Who is named is drawn fresh, and nobody is named twice in one play. Abuela puts a slice in the frog's hands each time. All four people can be touched.

8. **Round 1, Do.** NARR: "Abuela dice: Este es para papá." (Or mamá, abuelo, el bebé.)
   - Right: the frog hops over and gives it to him. Right signal. Papá eats it and keeps smiling, a crumb on his plate. NARR: "Papá dice: Gracias." COQUÍ: "De nada."
   - Wrong: he gives it to, say, mamá. She eats it; that stays. NARR: "No. Esa es mamá." (Or "Ese es papá." "Ese es abuelo." "Ese es el bebé.") Abuela takes a slice off the frog's plate and carries it to papá herself. NARR: "Abuela dice: Toma, papá." The frog's plate shows one slice less.
9. **Round 2, Do.** A new sentence. NARR: "Ahora abuelo quiere bizcocho." (Any of the three not yet named.)
   - Right and wrong as in round 1. Right: "Abuelo dice: Gracias." Wrong: "No. Ese es el bebé." "Abuela dice: Toma, abuelo."
10. **Round 3, Do.** A new sentence. NARR: "Abuela dice: Y este es para el bebé." (Any of the two not yet named.)
   - Right and wrong as before. If it is the baby and it is right, the baby squeals and smears it on his face.

**The ending** (the same lines in every ending)

11. Abuela brings the frog his plate, with whatever is left on it, and the drink he picked in ¡Qué calor!. NARR: "Abuela dice: Toma. Bizcocho y jugo." (Or "Bizcocho y agua.")
   - Three slices: he eats all three; his belly is round. Stars and a heart.
   - Two or one: he eats them.
   - None: the plate is empty. The baby crawls over and gives him a piece of his own. It is funny. It is not a fail screen.
12. COQUÍ: "¡Gracias, abuela!" NARR: "La familia come bizcocho."

**Fewest hearings of each new word on a run with no mistakes:** mamá 3, papá 3, abuelo 3, bebé 3, familia 3. Each person named in a round gets two more.

## Story 7: La foto

- **Goal he can see:** three empty picture frames on the wall. Abuelo has a camera.
- **At stake:** the three frames. Each round one photo is taken. A wrong move and that photo comes out wrong and hangs crooked, for good.
- **New words:** hermano, hermana, él, ella.
- **Back again:** abuelo, mamá, papá, familia, es, sí, no, hola.
- **Heard along the way:** foto, con, una, ahora, tres.

**Start:** the family is in the room. A boy and a girl run in.

1. NARR: "Mira. Este es el hermano." (He twinkles.) "Y esta es la hermana." (She twinkles.)

**The opening** (big sign, nothing can go wrong)

2. NARR: "¿Puedes tocar al hermano?"
   - He touches the boy. Right signal. NARR: "El hermano dice: ¡Hola!"
3. NARR: "¿Y la hermana? ¿Dónde está la hermana?"
   - He touches the girl. Right signal. NARR: "La hermana dice: ¡Hola!"
4. NARR: "Él es el hermano." (The boy twinkles.) "Ella es la hermana." (The girl twinkles.) "Él es papá." (Papá twinkles.) "Ella es mamá." (Mamá twinkles.)
5. **The photo, taught.** Abuelo stands up with his camera. The boy and the girl stand side by side. NARR: "Abuelo dice: Una foto con ella." The big sign is on the girl. He touches her. The frog hops beside her. A flash. A small photo of the two pops up. Right signal.
6. NARR: "Abuelo dice: Ahora, una foto con él." The big sign is on the boy. He touches him. The frog hops beside him. A flash. Right signal.

**The turn**

7. The three empty frames on the wall twinkle. Abuelo winds his camera. NARR: "Abuelo quiere tres fotos de la familia." The signs go away.

**The game** (no sign; one guess a round)

8. **Round 1, Do (hermano or hermana).** The boy and the girl stand apart; who is on which side is drawn fresh. NARR: "Abuelo dice: Una foto con la hermana." (Or "con el hermano". Drawn fresh.)
   - Right: the frog hops beside her. A flash. Right signal. The photo of the frog and the girl flies into the first frame and hangs straight.
   - Wrong: the frog hops beside the boy. A flash. NARR: "No. Él es el hermano." (Or "No. Ella es la hermana.") The photo of the frog with the wrong one flies into the frame and hangs crooked.
9. **Round 2, Do (él or ella).** Mamá and papá stand up side by side; sides drawn fresh. NARR: "Abuelo dice: Ahora, una foto con él." (Or "con ella". Drawn fresh.)
   - Right and wrong as in round 1. Wrong: "No. Ella es mamá." (Or "No. Él es papá.")
10. **Round 3, Say (sí or no).** Abuelo squints through the camera at one of the children, who stands alone in the middle (which one is drawn fresh). The two answer bubbles show: the frog nodding, the frog shaking his head. NARR: "Abuelo dice: ¿Es el hermano? ¿Sí o no?" (Or "¿Es la hermana?" Drawn fresh, apart from who is standing there.)
   - Right: COQUÍ: "¡Sí!" (or "¡No!"). Right signal. A flash. That child's photo hangs straight in the third frame.
   - Wrong: the frog says the other answer. NARR: "No. Es la hermana." (Or "Sí. Es el hermano." and so on: it says who it is.) A flash at the wrong moment: the child blinks. The photo hangs crooked.

**The ending** (the same lines in every ending)

11. The wall: straight photos twinkle; crooked ones swing a little. Three straight: stars and a heart.
12. Everyone gathers for one last photo, the frog in front. NARR: "Abuelo dice: Ahora, una foto de la familia." A big flash. COQUÍ: "¡Familia!"

**Fewest hearings of each new word on a run with no mistakes:** hermano 4, hermana 4, él 4, ella 4 (and one or two more for whichever is drawn).

## Story 8: ¿Quién eres tú?

- **Before it starts, one line in English on the opening card** (the plan's rule for a new pattern): "Yo soy: I am. Tú eres: you are. Él es, ella es: he is, she is."
- **Goal he can see:** abuelo cannot find his glasses (they are pushed up on top of his own head) and cannot tell who is who. He squints. Help him.
- **At stake:** abuelo's cup of coffee, three sips full (it shows in the top corner). After a wrong answer he goes off, mistaken, and greets the wrong thing; coffee slops out of the cup.
- **New words:** yo, tú, soy, eres, me llamo.
- **Back again:** él, ella, es, hermano, hermana, mamá, papá, abuelo, hola, sí.
- **Heard along the way:** quién, Ana, Luis.

The speaking bubbles from ¡Qué calor! are used here: two bubbles over the frog, no words in them; each says its line aloud in his voice, one after the other; he picks by ear. Sides are drawn fresh.

**Start:** the boy and the girl are with the frog on the rug.

1. NARR: "La hermana dice: Hola. Yo soy la hermana. Me llamo Ana."
2. NARR: "El hermano dice: Yo soy el hermano. Me llamo Luis."

**The opening** (big sign, nothing can go wrong)

3. NARR: "La hermana dice: ¿Y tú?" Two speaking bubbles: "Yo soy Coquí." ... "Yo soy Ana." The big sign is on "Yo soy Coquí." He touches it. COQUÍ: "Yo soy Coquí. Me llamo Coquí." Right signal.
4. NARR: "La hermana dice: ¡Sí! Tú eres Coquí." She points at him.
5. She points at herself and tilts her head. NARR: "La hermana dice: ¿Y yo?" Two speaking bubbles: "Tú eres Ana." ... "Tú eres Luis." The big sign is on "Tú eres Ana." He touches it. COQUÍ: "Tú eres Ana." Right signal. NARR: "La hermana dice: ¡Sí! Yo soy Ana."

**The turn**

6. Abuelo walks in with his cup of coffee, his glasses up on his head, patting his pockets, squinting. He bumps the low table. A pair of glasses and a question mark in his bubble. The cup appears in the top corner. The signs go away.

**The game** (no sign). Decisions A and B come in an order drawn fresh; C is last.

7. **Say decision A: abuelo squints at the frog.** NARR: "Abuelo dice: ¿Quién eres tú?" Two speaking bubbles: "Yo soy Coquí." and "Tú eres Coquí."
   - Right: COQUÍ: "Yo soy Coquí." Right signal. NARR: "Abuelo dice: ¡Ah! Tú eres Coquí." He pats the frog's head.
   - Wrong: COQUÍ: "Tú eres Coquí." Abuelo, puzzled, turns to the lamp and pats it. Coffee slops out; the cup shows one sip less. The sign shows the right bubble; it speaks again; he touches it. COQUÍ: "Yo soy Coquí." NARR: "Abuelo dice: ¡Ah! Tú eres Coquí."
8. **Say decision B: guess who.** One of the children (drawn fresh) creeps up behind the frog and covers his eyes. NARR: "La hermana dice: ¿Quién soy yo?" (Or "El hermano dice:".) Two speaking bubbles: "Tú eres Ana." and "Yo soy Ana." (Or Luis.)
   - Right: COQUÍ: "Tú eres Ana." Right signal. She laughs and lets go. NARR: "La hermana dice: ¡Sí! Yo soy Ana."
   - Wrong: COQUÍ: "Yo soy Ana." She giggles and keeps her hands there. Abuelo, hearing it, greets the frog as Ana and pats his head; coffee slops out. The sign shows the right bubble; it speaks; he touches it. COQUÍ: "Tú eres Ana."
9. **Say decision C, last: él or ella.** Mamá and papá stand side by side (sides drawn fresh). Abuelo squints at them. NARR: "Abuelo dice: ¿Quién es mamá?" (Or "¿Quién es papá?" Drawn fresh.) A soft light falls on the one he asks about. Two speaking bubbles: "Ella es mamá." and "Él es mamá." (Or "Él es papá." and "Ella es papá.")
   - Right: COQUÍ: "Ella es mamá." Right signal. Abuelo walks over to her. NARR: "Abuelo dice: ¡Hola, mamá!"
   - Wrong: COQUÍ: "Él es mamá." Abuelo walks to papá and greets him. NARR: "Abuelo dice: ¡Hola, mamá!" Papá laughs. Coffee slops out. The sign shows the right bubble; it speaks; he touches it. COQUÍ: "Ella es mamá."

**The ending** (the same lines in every ending)

10. The baby, on mamá's arm, reaches up and pulls abuelo's glasses down onto his nose. Abuelo blinks. Everything is clear. NARR: "Abuelo dice: ¡Ah! ¡La familia!"
11. By the cup:
   - Full: he drinks his coffee with a sigh. Stars and a heart.
   - One or two sips left: he drinks what there is.
   - Empty: he turns the cup upside down; one drop falls. Abuela brings him a fresh one, laughing.
12. NARR: "Abuelo dice: Y tú eres Coquí." COQUÍ: "¡Sí! Yo soy Coquí."

**Fewest hearings on a run with no mistakes:** yo 9, tú 8, soy 8, eres 6, me llamo 3.

# Escuchar for El parque: first set (draft 1, before reviews)

Place 2, El parque. The listening set that goes with its twelve stories (12 to 23), built like La casa's (historia/notas/guiones/escuchar-casa.md):

- **Otra vez**: each of the twelve map stories told straight through, nothing to touch. Already works; nothing to write.
- **Cuentos con dibujos**: five new picture stories in the four scenes already drawn. He only watches and hears.
- **Haz esto**: four do-this routines, one for each scene. He does what he is told. Nothing is lost.

Nothing in Escuchar is scored. It only adds minutes listened.

Words he has met by now (places 1 and 2): casa, puerta, ventana, abuela, hola, toca, abre, cierra, sí, no, grande, pequeña, adiós, silla, se sienta, vaso, jugo, agua, toma, quiero, por favor, gracias, de nada, ¿cómo estás?, bien, mamá, papá, abuelo, bebé, familia, hermano, hermana, él, ella, yo, tú, soy, eres, me llamo, niño, niña, juega, flor, sol, aquí, allá, amigo, ven, mira, vamos, ¿qué es esto?, hasta mañana, perro, gato, árbol, parque, camina, corre, espera, rápido, despacio, hay, ¿dónde está?, uno, dos, tres, bola, tira, coge, salta, dame, mi, tu, arriba, abajo, más, otra vez, tiene, pájaro, pato, pez, vuela, nada, rojo, azul, verde, amarillo, blanco, negro, ¿de qué color?, ¿cuál?, hombre, mujer, señor, señora, joven, viejo, cansado, contento, triste, come, bebe, duerme.
Heard along the way so far: bizcocho, lluvia, mesa, balcón, sala, patio, portón, foto, hace calor, para, con, también, ahora, quiere, entra, está, están, dónde, quién, es, un, una, y, de, en, se llama, este, esta, a jugar, muy, va, van, por arriba, por abajo, no más, pan, lago, bote, pinta, piragua, del, de la, Luis, Carmen, Canelo.

Rule for this set: only words from those two lists, in sentences of the kinds he has heard. Where a line needs one more word, it is marked **(new)** and the picture must carry it.

Voices as in the stories: the narrator; "X dice:" for what a person says; the frog in his own voice (COQUÍ).

## Cuentos con dibujos

Each runs about a minute. Stage directions are in English and are not heard. Every line is said while the thing it tells is in the picture.

### 1. El gato y Canelo (El camino)

The path. Luis holds Canelo on a loose lead. Carmen has her basket. A grey cat sits washing itself by the middle tree. Birds sit in the trees. The frog stands in the middle.

1. NARR: "Este es el parque."
2. The cat twinkles. NARR: "Mira. Un gato." NARR: "El gato es pequeño."
3. Canelo twinkles. NARR: "Y Canelo. Canelo es grande."
4. Canelo stands up and stares at the cat. NARR: "Canelo mira al gato." The cat stops washing and stares back. NARR: "El gato mira a Canelo."
5. Canelo barks and pulls loose. NARR: "¡Canelo corre!" The birds fly up. The cat shoots up into the trees. NARR: "¡El gato corre!"
6. Canelo stands under the trees, looking up. NARR: "¿Dónde está el gato?" The cat's face shows in the leaves of the third tree. NARR: "El gato está arriba, en el árbol." NARR: "Canelo está abajo."
7. NARR: "Luis dice: ¡No, Canelo! ¡Ven!" Canelo walks back to Luis and sits.
8. Carmen walks under the tree and holds her basket up. NARR: "Carmen dice: Ven, gato. Ven." The cat drops into the basket and curls up.
9. NARR: "Ahora el gato duerme." Canelo lies down. NARR: "Y Canelo espera." A heart.

### 2. La bola de Canelo (El campo)

The field. Luis on the left, Carmen on the right, the frog between them. Canelo sits on his patch of earth under the tree. Luis holds a ball.

1. NARR: "Luis tiene una bola."
2. Luis throws; Carmen catches. NARR: "Luis tira la bola. Carmen coge la bola."
3. Carmen throws high. NARR: "Carmen tira la bola. ¡Arriba!" The frog jumps and catches it. NARR: "Coquí salta. ¡Coquí coge la bola!"
4. The frog throws to Luis. Canelo runs in, jumps and takes the ball out of the air. NARR: "¡Ay! Canelo tiene la bola."
5. Canelo runs off with it, round the field. NARR: "Canelo corre rápido."
6. NARR: "Luis dice: Canelo, ¡dame la bola!" Canelo lies down on his patch with the ball between his paws.
7. NARR: "Canelo no quiere."
8. Carmen takes another ball out of the basket. NARR: "Carmen tiene otra bola." **(new: otra, heard in "otra vez")**
9. She throws it to the frog; Canelo drops the first ball and runs for the new one. NARR: "Canelo quiere más. ¡Otra vez!" The children laugh. A heart.

### 3. Pan para todos (El lago)

The pond. Luis sits on the dock. Carmen stands by him with her bag of bread. The white duck paddles near the dock; a fish circles under the water; a bird sits on the post. The frog stands on the dock.

1. NARR: "Carmen tiene pan."
2. Carmen throws a piece to the duck; it eats. NARR: "Pan para el pato. El pato come."
3. She throws a piece to the fish; it jumps and takes it. NARR: "Pan para el pez. ¡El pez salta!"
4. The bird flies up and round. NARR: "¡Mira! El pájaro vuela." NARR: "El pájaro también quiere pan."
5. She throws a piece up; the bird takes it and settles on the post. NARR: "Pan para el pájaro."
6. Carmen holds a piece out to the frog. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¿Y tú, Coquí? ¿Quieres pan?" COQUÍ: "No, gracias."
7. The frog throws his piece to the duck. NARR: "Coquí dice: Pan para el pato." The duck eats and quacks.
8. NARR: "El pez nada." NARR: "El pájaro vuela." NARR: "Y el pato nada y vuela." COQUÍ: "¡Adiós, amigos!"

### 4. La piragua de Luis (El banco)

The benches. The piragua man at his cart. Luis stands at the cart with a red piragua in his hand; Carmen beside him with one too. A pigeon on the wire. The frog in front.

1. NARR: "Luis tiene una piragua. Carmen tiene una piragua."
2. Luis runs to the benches. NARR: "Luis corre."
3. The ice slides out of its paper onto the ground. Luis stops and looks down. NARR: "¡Ay, no! La piragua de Luis."
4. His head goes down; tears. NARR: "Luis está triste."
5. Carmen walks over and holds hers out. NARR: "Carmen dice: Toma, Luis. Mi piragua es para ti." **(new: ti)**
6. Luis takes it and eats. NARR: "Ahora Luis está contento."
7. The frog hops to the cart. COQUÍ: "Una piragua para Carmen, por favor." The piragua man hands one over; the frog carries it to Carmen.
8. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¡Gracias, Coquí!" She eats. NARR: "Carmen está contenta."
9. The pigeon flies down and pecks at the ice on the ground. NARR: "¿Y el pájaro? El pájaro come." NARR: "El pájaro también está contento." A heart.

### 5. Buenas noches, parque (El banco)

The benches in evening light. The old gentleman on the first bench; the woman on the second; the young man on the third. The old dog and the young dog by the cart. Abuela, Luis, Carmen, Canelo and the frog stand in front.

1. NARR: "Mira el sol." The light goes down; the lamp comes on.
2. The old gentleman gets up and walks slowly to the gate. NARR: "El señor camina despacio." COQUÍ: "¡Adiós, señor!"
3. The woman gets up and goes. COQUÍ: "¡Adiós, señora!"
4. The young man runs to the gate. NARR: "El joven corre." COQUÍ: "¡Adiós, joven!"
5. The piragua man shuts his umbrella and pushes his cart away; the two dogs go with him. NARR: "El perro viejo camina despacio. El perro joven corre."
6. The pigeons on the wire tuck their heads in. NARR: "El pájaro duerme."
7. Canelo lies down flat. NARR: "Canelo está cansado." Luis and Carmen droop. NARR: "Luis está cansado. Carmen está cansada." **(new form: cansada)**
8. NARR: "Abuela dice: Vamos a casa." They walk to the gate. The frog stays a moment and looks at the empty benches.
9. COQUÍ: "Hasta mañana, parque." He hops out through the gate. The lamp glows. A heart.

## Haz esto

How a routine works (as in La casa): a line is said; he touches or does it; the thing happens. No praise after each line; one "¡Muy bien!" at the end. Nothing is lost on a wrong touch: "No. Ese es el gato." (what he touched), then the big sign shows the right one. If he waits, the line is said again. Where two touches are fair, both count. Each routine ends with a line in two parts, which can be done in either order.

### A. En el camino

The path: Luis, Carmen, Canelo, the cat on the ground, the three small trees (one bird in the first, two in the second, three in the third), the big tree, the gate.

1. "Toca al perro." (Canelo wags and barks once.)
2. "¿Dónde está el gato?" (The cat arches its back and purrs.)
3. "Toca el árbol grande." (It shakes; a leaf falls.) The small trees are the other answers.
4. "¿Dónde hay dos pájaros?" (The two birds in the second tree sing.) The other two trees are the other answers.
5. "¿Dónde hay tres pájaros?" (The three sing.)
6. Doing bubbles (walk, run, wait, as in story 12): "Luis dice: ¡Corre, Coquí!" (The frog and Canelo run to the next stone.)
7. "Luis dice: ¡Espera!" (They stand still; Luis laughs.)
8. "Luis dice: ¡Camina despacio!" (They walk on.)
9. Two parts: "Toca al niño y a la niña." (Each waves.) The cat is the other answer. "¡Muy bien!" A heart.

### B. En el campo

The field: Luis with his ball, Carmen with hers, the frog's ball at his feet, Canelo on his patch, the basket, the tree.

1. "¿Dónde está Canelo?" (He barks.)
2. "Luis dice: Toca mi bola." (Luis holds his ball up.) Carmen's ball and the frog's are the other answers.
3. "Carmen dice: Toca mi bola." (Carmen holds hers up.)
4. "Luis dice: ¿Dónde está tu bola, Coquí?" (The frog picks his ball up.)
5. Doing bubbles (throw, catch, jump, as in stories 15 and 16): "Carmen dice: ¡Tira la bola!" (The frog throws it to her.)
6. "Carmen dice: ¡Coge la bola!" (She throws it back; he catches it.)
7. "Luis dice: ¡Salta!" (The frog jumps; Canelo jumps too.)
8. "Luis dice: ¡Dame la bola!" He touches Luis. (The frog carries his ball to Luis.) Carmen is the other answer.
9. Two parts: "Toca el árbol y a Canelo." (The tree shakes; Canelo barks.) The basket is the other answer. "¡Muy bien!" A heart.

### C. En el lago

The pond: three ducks (white, black, yellow), a red, a blue and a green boat on the water, a fish circling, a bird on the post, Luis and Carmen.

1. "Toca el pato blanco." (It quacks.) The other two ducks are the other answers.
2. "¿Cuál es el pato negro?" (It quacks.)
3. "¿Dónde está el bote rojo?" (It rocks.) The other boats are the other answers.
4. "Toca el bote verde." (It rocks.)
5. "¿Quién vuela?" (The bird flies round and comes back.) The fish is the other answer. A duck is fair too: a touch on a duck makes it fly, and counts.
6. "¿Quién nada?" (The fish jumps.) The bird is the other answer. A duck is fair too.
7. Carmen gives the frog a piece of bread. "Carmen dice: Pan para el pato amarillo." (He throws it; the yellow duck eats.) The other ducks are the other answers.
8. Two parts: "Toca el bote azul y el pato blanco." (The boat rocks; the duck quacks.) The red boat is the other answer. "¡Muy bien!" A heart.

### D. En el banco

The benches: the old gentleman on the first bench; the woman, eating a piragua, and the man, drinking water, on the second; the young man on the third, sad, his piragua on the ground. The old dog asleep by the cart; the young dog sitting beside him.

1. "Toca al señor." (He lifts his hat.) The woman and the young man are the other answers.
2. "¿Dónde está la señora?" (She waves.)
3. "¿Dónde está el joven?" (He looks up.)
4. "¿Quién come?" (The woman holds her piragua up.) The man, who is drinking, is the other answer.
5. "¿Quién bebe agua?" (The man holds his bottle up.)
6. "¿Quién duerme?" (The old dog opens one eye and shuts it again.) The young dog is the other answer.
7. "¿Quién está triste?" (The young man.) The frog brings him a new piragua. Then: NARR: "Ahora el joven está contento."
8. "¿Dónde está el perro joven?" (He runs once round the cart.) The old dog is the other answer.
9. Two parts: "Toca al hombre y a la mujer." (Each waves.) The old gentleman is the other answer. "¡Muy bien!" A heart.

- "Canelo mira al gato" has "al" before the animal, as people say it.

# El patio: three stories (draft 1, for review)

Place 1, La casa. Scene 4 of 4, the last of this place. The learner is the little frog (the coquí), a guest at abuela's house.
He has played eight stories: three at the front of the house, two on the porch, three in the living room.

Words he has met so far: casa, puerta, ventana, abuela, hola, toca, abre, cierra, sí, no, grande, pequeña, adiós, silla, se sienta, vaso, jugo, agua, toma, quiero, por favor, gracias, de nada, ¿cómo estás?, bien, mamá, papá, abuelo, bebé, familia, hermano, hermana, él, ella, yo, tú, soy, eres, me llamo.
Heard along the way so far: mira, ven, puedes, dónde está, también, ahora, quiere, tiene, entra, pájaro, bizcocho, palma, lluvia, y tú, o, mesa, balcón, sala, hace calor, dos, tres, un, una, y, de, del, con, para, pone, come, foto, quién, es, este, esta, Carmen, Luis.

Scene words for El patio (from the 40-scene list): niño, niña, amigo, juega, flor, sol, aquí, allí, ven, mira, vamos, ¿qué es esto?, hasta mañana.

The picture: abuela's back yard. Grass, a wooden fence along the back with a garden gate in it, the back wall of the house on the left with its door, a tree on the right with a bird's nest in it. Two flower beds of bare earth: one at the front (near), one at the back by the fence (far). A big sun in the sky. The two children from the living room are here: Luis (the boy, short hair, green shirt) and Carmen (the girl, two braids, pink dress). The bird from the earlier stories lives in the tree.

Voices: the narrator speaks to the learner. A person's lines are spoken by the narrator and start with who says it ("Luis dice:", "Carmen dice:", "Abuela dice:"); the words show in a bubble at that person. The frog has his own voice (COQUÍ). The bird only chirps.

The signal for a right move is the same as always: chime, stars, the frog hops, then "¡Sí! ¡Muy bien!". It plays after every right answer, also where it is not written out.
Poking is free: anything he touches outside an answer is named ("Es una flor." "Es el sol." "Este es Luis.").
A decision's line is said once; a touch before it ends does not count; if he waits it plays again, free.
Nobody points with a hand or looks toward the answer in a decision; a speaker's picture bubble stays empty.

## Story 9: Las flores

- **Goal he can see, from the first frame:** Carmen holds a tray of flowers in little pots. The two beds are bare earth with empty holes. Plant abuela's flowers while there is daylight.
- **At stake:** the daylight. The sun stands high. After a wrong move it sinks one step toward the fence and the sky turns more orange. Three steps and it is sunset.
- **New words:** niño, niña, juega, flor, sol, aquí, allí.
- **Back again:** hola, no, sí, tiene, es, yo, también.
- **Heard along the way:** patio, bola, con, una, está.

**Start:** Luis bounces a ball. Carmen stands with her tray. The frog hops out of the back door.

1. NARR: "Este es el patio de abuela."
2. NARR: "Mira. Luis y Carmen." Then: "Luis es un niño." (He twinkles.) "Carmen es una niña." (She twinkles.)

**The opening** (big sign, nothing can go wrong)

3. NARR: "¿Puedes tocar al niño?"
   - He touches Luis. Right signal. Luis waves. NARR: "Luis dice: ¡Hola, Coquí!" He kicks the ball up and catches it. NARR: "El niño juega con la bola."
4. NARR: "¿Y la niña? ¿Dónde está la niña?"
   - He touches Carmen. Right signal. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¡Hola!" She holds up her tray. NARR: "La niña tiene flores."
5. NARR: "Mira las flores." (The tray twinkles.) Then: "¿Puedes tocar una flor?"
   - He touches the tray. Right signal. A butterfly lands on it.
6. NARR: "Mira el sol." (The sun twinkles.) Then: "¿Dónde está el sol?"
   - He touches the sun. Right signal. It beams.
7. **Planting, taught.** Carmen walks to the front bed and stands beside it. NARR: "Carmen dice: Una flor aquí." The big sign is on the front bed, where she stands. He touches it. The frog hops there with a pot and plants it. The flower opens and stays. COQUÍ: "¡Aquí!" NARR: "La flor está aquí."
8. She does not move. NARR: "Carmen dice: Y una flor allí." The big sign is on the back bed, away from her. He touches it. The frog hops over and plants one. It opens. COQUÍ: "¡Allí!" NARR: "La flor está allí."

**The turn**

9. The sun slips down a notch with a soft tick; the shadows grow. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¡Mira! ¡El sol!" The signs go away. Three pots are left on the tray.

**The game** (no sign). In each round the speaker walks to one of the two beds and stands beside it; which bed is drawn fresh, and so is the word. "Aquí" is the bed where the speaker stands. "Allí" is the other one.

10. **Round 1, Do.** Carmen stands by one bed. NARR: "Carmen dice: Una flor aquí." (Or "Una flor allí." Drawn fresh.)
   - Right: the frog plants it in the right bed. Right signal. It opens and stays. A butterfly comes.
   - Wrong: he plants it in the other bed. It stays there. NARR: "No. Allí." (Or "No. Aquí.": it says the word she said.) The sun sinks a step. The sign shows the right bed. He plants one there, and the right side plays.
11. **Round 2, Do.** Luis puts his ball down and stands by one bed. NARR: "Luis dice: Ahora, una flor allí." (Or "aquí". Drawn fresh.)
   - Right and wrong as in round 1.
12. **Round 3, Do (both words are said).** Carmen stands by one bed. Which sentence comes first is drawn fresh. NARR: "Carmen dice: Aquí no." Then: "Carmen dice: Allí." (Or: "Carmen dice: Allí." Then: "Carmen dice: Aquí no." Or the same two pairs with the words swapped.)
   - Right and wrong as before.

**The ending** (the same lines in every ending)

13. By the sun:
   - Still high (no wrong moves): the beds are full of open flowers, butterflies everywhere. Stars and a heart.
   - One or two steps down: an orange sky, long shadows, the flowers still open.
   - Sunset (three wrong): the sun sits on the fence, the sky is red and purple, the flowers have closed for the night, and the first firefly comes out. It is pretty. It is not a fail screen.
14. Luis kicks the ball to the frog. NARR: "El niño juega. La niña juega." COQUÍ: "¡Yo también!" The frog bounces the ball on his head.

**Fewest hearings of each new word on a run with no mistakes:** niño 4, niña 5, juega 3, flor 6, sol 4, aquí 3 and allí 3 before round 1 (then at least one more of each).

**A miss is filed under:** the word the speaker said (aquí or allí); in round 3, under "no".

## Story 10: ¿Qué es esto?

- **Goal he can see:** Luis has a cloth sack with lumps in it. The frog has a little basket. Fill the basket with what comes out of the sack.
- **At stake:** three things, one a round. Get it right and it goes in his basket. Get it wrong and the bird snatches it and carries it up to its nest in the tree, where it stays in view. Two scores show in the top corners: his basket, the bird's nest.
- **New words:** ¿qué es esto?, amigo, mira, ven.
- **Back again:** pájaro, flor, vaso, silla, bizcocho, jugo, es, un, una, sí, no, tú, eres, yo, soy.
- **Heard along the way:** mi, aquí, allí.

The speaking bubbles from ¡Qué calor! and ¿Quién eres tú? are used here: two bubbles over the frog, no words in them; each says its line aloud in his voice, one after the other; he picks by ear. Sides are drawn fresh.

**Start:** Luis sits on the grass with his sack. Carmen is by the tree. The bird is in its nest. The frog is at the back door with his basket.

**The opening** (big sign, nothing can go wrong)

1. NARR: "Luis dice: ¡Ven, Coquí!" The big sign is on Luis. He touches Luis. The frog hops over to him. Right signal.
2. The bird flies a loop over the tree. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¡Mira! ¡El pájaro!" The big sign is on the bird. He touches it. Right signal. The bird lands on the frog's head for a moment. NARR: "Luis dice: El pájaro es mi amigo." COQUÍ: "¡Hola, amigo!"
3. **The sack, taught.** Luis pulls a flower out of the sack and holds it up. NARR: "Luis dice: ¿Qué es esto?" Two speaking bubbles: "Es una flor." and "Es un vaso." The big sign is on "Es una flor." He touches it. COQUÍ: "Es una flor." Right signal. NARR: "Luis dice: ¡Sí! Es una flor." The flower goes into the frog's basket.

**The turn**

4. The bird drops down, pecks at the sack and pulls at its string. NARR: "Luis dice: ¡No, amigo! ¡No!" It flaps up to a branch just above them and waits, eyes on the sack. The two scores appear. The signs go away.

**The game** (no sign). Decisions A and C are the same kind with different answers; B is between them.

5. **Decision A, Do (ven or mira).** Carmen stands apart with something behind her back. The bird hops along its branch with something in its beak. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¡Ven!" (Or "Carmen dice: ¡Mira!" Drawn fresh.) He can touch Carmen or the bird.
   - "¡Ven!", and he touches Carmen: the frog hops to her. Right signal. She gives him what she was hiding (a small glass) for his basket.
   - "¡Mira!", and he touches the bird: the frog looks up. Right signal. The bird does a loop and drops what it carried (a flower) into his basket.
   - Wrong: NARR: "No. ¡Ven!" (Or "No. ¡Mira!") The thing is lost to the nest: if she called him and he looked at the bird, the bird takes her thing from her hand; if she said look and he went to her, the bird flies its own thing up to the nest. One on the bird's score.
6. **Decision B, Say (¿qué es esto?).** Luis pulls a thing out of the sack: a glass, a slice of cake, or a small chair (drawn fresh). NARR: "Luis dice: ¿Qué es esto?" Two speaking bubbles: the right name and one other, drawn fresh from "Es un vaso.", "Es un bizcocho.", "Es una silla."
   - Right: COQUÍ: "Es un vaso." Right signal. NARR: "Luis dice: ¡Sí! Es un vaso." It goes into his basket.
   - Wrong: the frog says the other name. NARR: "Luis dice: No." The bird snatches the thing and flies it to the nest. One on the bird's score. The sign shows the right bubble; it speaks again; he touches it. COQUÍ: "Es un vaso."
7. **Decision C, Do (ven or mira).** As decision A, with the word A did not use, and with Luis calling. NARR: "Luis dice: ¡Mira!" (Or "¡Ven!")

**The ending** (the same lines in every ending)

8. By the basket:
   - Three things in his basket: stars and a heart; the bird sulks, then hops down to look.
   - One or two: the nest shows what the bird got.
   - None: the nest is full, the basket holds only the flower from the opening.
   - In every ending the bird then brings one thing down from the tree and drops it in his basket, as a friend. (If its nest is empty it brings a flower from the tree.)
9. NARR: "Luis dice: Coquí es mi amigo." COQUÍ: "¡Y tú eres mi amigo!" The bird chirps. COQUÍ: "¡Y tú, amigo!"

**Fewest hearings of each new word on a run with no mistakes:** ¿qué es esto? 2 before it is staked (it is the question, not the answer; the answers are words he knows), 3 in all; amigo 6; mira 3 and ven 3 before either can cost, counting the earlier stories' "mira" and "ven al balcón" not at all.

**A miss is filed under:** A and C, ven or mira; B, the thing that was shown.

## Story 11: Hasta mañana

- **Goal he can see:** the sun is on the fence and the sky is red. Time to go home. The garden gate is shut. Three fireflies drift over the grass.
- **At stake:** the fireflies that light his way. A right goodbye and one comes to fly beside him, for good. A wrong one and it drifts away over the fence.
- **New words:** vamos, hasta mañana.
- **Back again:** adiós, hola, abuela, mamá, papá, abuelo, puerta, casa, abre, gracias, amigo, ven.
- **Heard along the way:** a, la noche.

**Start:** dusk in the yard. The family stands at the back door of the house. Luis and Carmen are on the grass. The bird sits on the gate.

1. NARR: "Mira el sol." The sun sinks behind the fence. The first star comes out.
2. The bird flaps at the frog and hops toward the gate. NARR: "El pájaro dice: ¡Vamos, Coquí! ¡Vamos!" COQUÍ: "¡Vamos!"

**The opening** (big sign, nothing can go wrong)

3. NARR: "Abuela dice: Adiós, Coquí. ¡Hasta mañana!" She waves from the door. Two speaking bubbles: "¡Hasta mañana, abuela!" and "¡Hola, abuela!" The big sign is on "¡Hasta mañana, abuela!" He touches it. COQUÍ: "¡Hasta mañana, abuela!" Right signal. A firefly drifts over and stays beside him.

**The turn**

4. It gets darker. The two fireflies still out over the grass blink. The signs go away.

**The game** (no sign). Decisions A and B come in an order drawn fresh; C is last.

5. **Say decision A: the children say goodbye.** NARR: "Luis dice: ¡Hasta mañana, Coquí!" Two speaking bubbles: "¡Hasta mañana!" and one other, drawn fresh from "¡Hola!" and "De nada."
   - Right: COQUÍ: "¡Hasta mañana!" Right signal. A firefly comes to him and stays.
   - Wrong: the frog says the other line. Luis scratches his head. That firefly drifts away over the fence. The sign shows the right bubble; it speaks again; he touches it. COQUÍ: "¡Hasta mañana!"
6. **Say decision B: abuelo brings a gift.** Abuelo comes down from the door with a slice of cake in a napkin. NARR: "Abuelo dice: Toma, Coquí." Two speaking bubbles: "¡Gracias, abuelo!" and one other, drawn fresh from "¡Hasta mañana, abuelo!" and "De nada."
   - Right: COQUÍ: "¡Gracias, abuelo!" Right signal. He takes the cake. A firefly comes and stays.
   - Wrong: the frog says the other line. Abuelo laughs and waits. That firefly drifts away. The sign shows the right bubble; it speaks; he touches it. COQUÍ: "¡Gracias, abuelo!" He takes the cake.
7. **Do decision C, last (vamos a la puerta, or a la casa).** The bird hovers. NARR: "El pájaro dice: ¡Vamos a la puerta!" (Or "¡Vamos a la casa!" Drawn fresh.) He can touch the garden gate or the house.
   - Right: the frog hops there. Right signal. If it is the gate, it swings open. If it is the house, abuela hands him a little lantern, and then the gate swings open.
   - Wrong: he hops to the other place. NARR: "No. Esa es la casa." (Or "No. Esa es la puerta.") One firefly that was with him drifts off; if he has none, the star goes behind a cloud. The sign shows the right place. He goes there, and the right side plays.

**The ending** (the same lines in every ending)

8. The gate is open. Everyone waves. NARR: "La familia dice: ¡Adiós, Coquí! ¡Hasta mañana!" COQUÍ: "¡Adiós! ¡Hasta mañana!"
9. He hops out along the path, the bird flying beside him. By the fireflies:
   - Three: they fly around him in a ring and the path is bright. Stars and a heart.
   - One or two: a small glow.
   - None: it is dark; then he sings his own name into the night, "¡Co-quí!", and fireflies light up all along the path.
10. His song, from far off: "¡Co-quí! ¡Co-quí!"

**Fewest hearings of each new word on a run with no mistakes:** vamos 4, hasta mañana 6 (2 before it is first staked under the sign, 4 before it can cost).

**A miss is filed under:** A, hasta mañana; B, gracias; C, vamos.

## For the owner, one line each

- Aquí and allí are taught as they are used: "aquí" is where the person speaking stands, "allí" is the other place.
- Story 10 is where words from the whole house come back by ear: he hears "¿Qué es esto?" and picks the frog's answer.
- Story 11 closes La casa: goodbyes to the family, and the way home.

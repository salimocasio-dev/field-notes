# El banco de la plaza: three stories (draft 1, before the reviews)

Place 6, La plaza. Scene 1 of 4 (scene id `plaza`; "banco" is taken by El parque's bench). Stories 60 to 62. The learner is the little frog (Coquí). He has finished stories 1 to 59.

Scene words (page 02): ¿cómo te llamas?, nombre, mucho gusto, ¿de dónde eres?, soy de, vivo en, hablo, español, inglés, entiendo, no sé.

The picture: a bench in the town square. One view. A long seat with a back, facing us, under a big tree; paving all round; pigeons; behind, a low wall, flower beds and, far off, the square's clock tower. At the left a notice board on two posts (story 61). Everybody stands or sits on the paving in front of the seat or on it.

New people: Ana, a girl of Carmen's age who has just come to live here (two plaits, a yellow dress); her mamá; her small brother Beto (four years old, a red cap); her abuela, who is on a visit (a sun hat, a camera round her neck). The others: Luis, Carmen, Canelo, the piragüero.

Answer bubbles (stories 60 and 62): two round bubbles over the frog, each with a small picture; touching one makes the frog say its line. A person's speech in English is never heard: a bubble with a small flag shows which language somebody is speaking (the flag of Puerto Rico: Spanish; the flag of the United States: English).

Voices, the signal for a right move, what follows a wrong move, pokes and the corner card are as in La calle. Every story has two scored rounds; the card shows two places. Every story ends with the shared `fin`. Words: the everyday Puerto Rican word wins over the plan's (page 16).

## Story 60: Mucho gusto

- **Goal he can see:** three pigeons walk along the seat's back towards the frog, who holds a little bag of bread. He wants them to stay.
- **At stake:** the pigeons (3). A wrong answer makes the asker say the question again, loud and slow: a pigeon flies off.
- **New words:** ¿cómo te llamas?, nombre, mucho gusto.
- **Back again:** me llamo, ¿cómo estás?, bien, gracias, hola, amigo, hermano, mamá, niña, perro, tiene, pan, pájaro, sí, aquí, mira, juega.

**Start:** morning. Luis, Carmen and the frog on the seat; Canelo under it. The frog has his bag of bread; three pigeons on the seat's back. Ana comes across the square with her mamá and Beto, and stops, shy.

**The opening** (big sign)

1. NARR: "Mira. Una niña nueva en la plaza." Luis jumps up. NARR: "Luis dice: ¡Hola! ¿Cómo te llamas?" NARR: "Ana dice: Me llamo Ana." NARR: "Luis dice: Mucho gusto, Ana. Yo me llamo Luis." They shake hands. NARR: "Ana dice: Mucho gusto."
2. Carmen comes up to her. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¿Cómo te llamas?" NARR: "Ana dice: Mi nombre es Ana. ¿Y tú?" NARR: "Carmen dice: Mi nombre es Carmen. Mucho gusto." Ask (signed, Ana): "¿Dónde está la niña nueva?" He touches her: Ana waves.
3. Ana kneels down to Canelo. NARR: "Ana dice: ¿Y el perro? ¿Tiene nombre?" NARR: "Carmen dice: Sí. Su nombre es Canelo." Canelo wags. Ana looks up at the frog and the pigeons. NARR: "Ana dice: ¿Y tú? ¿Cómo te llamas?" COQUÍ [TURN: me llamo (me llamo)]: "Me llamo Coquí." NARR: "Ana dice: ¡Mucho gusto, Coquí!"

**The turn**

4. The frog holds out a crumb; the three pigeons come close. Ana's mamá and Beto walk up. Two bubbles appear over the frog: one with a name badge, one with a smiling face. Ask (signed, the badge bubble): "Toca aquí." He touches it. COQUÍ: "Me llamo Coquí." Ask (signed, the smiling bubble): "Y aquí." He touches it. COQUÍ: "Bien, gracias." The card shows two places. The signs go.

**The game.** Somebody new speaks to the frog and asks one of two questions, drawn, each round on its own: his name, or how he is. He answers with a bubble.

5. **Decision A, touch a bubble (two bubbles; tests the question asked).** NARR: "La mamá de Ana dice: ¡Hola! ¿Cómo te llamas?" (Or, drawn: "La mamá de Ana dice: ¡Hola! ¿Cómo estás?")
   - Right: the frog says the bubble's line. To the name: NARR: "La mamá de Ana dice: ¡Mucho gusto, Coquí!" To how he is: NARR: "La mamá de Ana dice: ¡Qué bien!" A star.
   - Wrong: the frog says the other line. She laughs, bends down and says it again, loud and slow. NARR: "La mamá de Ana dice: No, no. ¿Cómo te llamas?" (Or: "No, no. ¿Cómo estás?") One pigeon flies off. A cross. The sign is on the right bubble; he touches it: the line, and her reply.
6. **Decision B, touch a bubble (the same game; Beto).** Beto pulls at the frog's arm. NARR: "Beto dice: ¿Cómo estás?" (Or, drawn: "Beto dice: ¿Cómo te llamas?") Right and wrong as in A. Beto's replies: "Beto dice: ¡Coquí! ¡Mucho gusto!" or "Beto dice: ¡Yo también!"; his correction: "Beto dice: ¡No! ¿Cómo estás?" (or the other).

**The ending**

7. Ana sits down beside the frog. NARR: "Ana dice: ¿Tienes pan para los pájaros?" COQUÍ [TURN: sí (sí, si)]: "¡Sí! Toma." He gives her a crumb.
   - Three pigeons left: one hops onto Ana's hand, one onto Beto's cap, one onto the frog's head. Stars and a heart.
   - Two left: one on Ana's hand, one on the frog's head; Beto claps.
   - One left: it eats from the frog's hand; Ana and Beto watch, very still.
8. NARR: "Luis dice: Ana, Beto: ¡mucho gusto! ¿Juegan?" The children run off across the square; the frog hops after them. NARR: "Coquí tiene amigos nuevos."

**Fewest hearings before a word can cost:** ¿cómo te llamas? 3 (steps 1, 2, 3). Not deciding: mucho gusto 5 before the game; nombre 4 (steps 2 and 3).
**A miss is filed under:** ¿cómo te llamas? (whichever question was asked, the pair is what is tested).
**Chance without the Spanish:** one in two, each round on its own. The two questions differ only after "¿Cómo...".
**Speaking turns:** two (steps 3, 7): me llamo; sí.

## Story 61: ¿De dónde eres?

- **Goal he can see:** the notice board, with a row of five empty hooks under a picture of the island and a row under a picture of tall buildings; and, on the seat under a stone, three blank cards and a pencil: the frog's own, for later.
- **At stake:** the blank cards (3). A photo hung under the wrong picture has to come down again; while it is changed the wind takes a card.
- **New words:** ¿de dónde eres?, soy de, vivo en.
- **Back again:** ¿cómo te llamas?, me llamo, nombre, mucho gusto, casa, familia, abuela, mamá, hermano, aquí, allá, ahora, grande, lejos, cerca, mira, foto (heard), yo, tú.

**Start:** afternoon. Ana and the frog at the notice board with a box of small photos (faces). On the board, two pictures: at the left the island (a green island, a palm, the sea), at the right a city of tall buildings. Luis and Carmen look on.

**The opening** (big sign)

1. NARR: "Ana y Coquí hacen fotos de los amigos." Ana points at the island. NARR: "Ana dice: Esto es Puerto Rico." At the buildings. NARR: "Ana dice: Y esto es Nueva York. Está lejos." **(Puerto Rico, Nueva York: names; the pictures carry them)**
2. NARR: "Luis dice: Ana, ¿de dónde eres?" NARR: "Ana dice: Soy de Nueva York. Ahora vivo en Puerto Rico." Ana holds up her own photo. Ask (signed, the hooks under the buildings): "Ana es de Nueva York. ¿Dónde va la foto de Ana?" He touches them: Ana hangs her photo there.
3. NARR: "Ana dice: ¿Y tú, Luis? ¿De dónde eres?" NARR: "Luis dice: Soy de Puerto Rico. Vivo en Puerto Rico." Ask (signed, the hooks under the island): "¿Dónde va la foto de Luis?" He touches them: Luis's photo hangs there.
4. NARR: "Ana dice: ¿Y tú, Coquí? ¿De dónde eres?" COQUÍ [TURN: aquí (aquí, aqui)]: "¡De aquí!" The frog points at the island; his photo goes up beside Luis's. NARR: "Ana dice: Coquí es de Puerto Rico. Vive aquí, cerca de la plaza."

**The turn**

5. Ana's abuela walks up with her camera, and behind her the piragüero pushing his cart. The wind lifts the corner of the top card on the seat; the frog puts the stone back on the cards. NARR: "Ana dice: Mira, Coquí. ¡Más amigos!" The card shows two places. The signs go.

**The game.** Somebody says where he is from and where he lives: one is the island, the other the city, drawn, each round on its own; which half comes first is drawn too. The photo goes under the place he is FROM.

6. **Decision A, touch a place (the two rows of hooks; tests soy de).** NARR: "Ana dice: Abuela, ¿de dónde eres?" NARR: "La abuela de Ana dice: Soy de Puerto Rico. Vivo en Nueva York." (Or, drawn: "Vivo en Nueva York. Soy de Puerto Rico."; or the two places the other way round.)
   - Right: the frog hangs her photo there. She nods and takes a picture of the board. A star.
   - Wrong: the frog hangs it under the other picture. She shakes her head, smiling. NARR: "La abuela de Ana dice: No, Coquí. Soy de Puerto Rico. Vivo en Nueva York." The frog takes the photo down; the stone rolls off the cards and the wind takes one away over the wall. A cross. The sign is on the right row; he touches it: the photo hangs there.
7. **Decision B, touch a place (the same game; the piragüero).** NARR: "Ana dice: Señor, ¿de dónde es usted?" **(es usted: heard)** NARR: "El piragüero dice: Soy de Nueva York. Vivo en Puerto Rico." (Drawn, as in A.) Right and wrong as in A.

**The ending**

8. The board is full of faces. Ana gives the frog the pencil. NARR: "Ana dice: Y ahora, tus fotos. ¿Dónde vives, Coquí?" COQUÍ [TURN: casa (casa)]: "En la casa de abuela."
   - Three cards left: the frog draws three pictures and hangs them: abuela's house, abuela, the whole family. Stars and a heart.
   - Two left: the house, and abuela.
   - One left: the house.
9. NARR: "Ana dice: ¡Qué casa bonita! Yo vivo en una casa azul. ¿Vienes mañana?" **(bonita: heard)** COQUÍ [TURN: sí (sí, si)]: "¡Sí!" NARR: "Coquí es de Puerto Rico. Y Ana vive en Puerto Rico."

**Fewest hearings before a word can cost:** soy de 3 (steps 2, 3; "es de" twice more), vivo en 3 (steps 2, 3; "vive" once). Not deciding: ¿de dónde eres? 3 (steps 2, 3, 4).
**Forms:** es de counts with soy de; vive, vives count with vivo en.
**A miss is filed under:** soy de.
**Chance without the Spanish:** one in two, each round on its own: both places are named in every line, and only soy de and vivo en say which is which.
**Speaking turns:** three (steps 4, 8, 9): aquí; casa; sí.

## Story 62: Hablo español

- **Goal he can see:** Beto on the seat with an ice cream of three scoops, licking slowly, and looking at the frog's empty hands.
- **At stake:** the scoops (3). While the frog fetches the wrong helper Beto gets tired of waiting and eats a scoop.
- **New words:** hablo, español, inglés, entiendo, no sé.
- **Back again:** ¿de dónde eres?, soy de, vivo en, hermano, abuela, mamá, señor, perdón, ¿dónde está?, correo, cerca, sí, no, gracias, espera, quiero, piragua.

**Start:** midday. Beto on the seat with his ice cream. Ana's abuela stands in the middle of the paving with a letter in her hand, looking about her. The frog, Luis and Carmen near the tree; Ana a little way off; the piragüero at his cart.

**The opening** (big sign)

1. Ana's abuela speaks to Luis: a bubble with the flag of the United States, and a letter in it. Luis scratches his head. NARR: "Luis dice: Perdón. No entiendo. No hablo inglés." **(the bubble shows she speaks English; nothing in English is heard)** Ask (signed, her bubble): "La abuela de Ana habla inglés. Toca aquí." He touches it: the bubble shakes; she says it again.
2. Ana runs up. NARR: "Ana dice: Yo entiendo. Yo hablo inglés y hablo español." Her own bubble shows both flags; she listens to her abuela and turns to Luis. NARR: "Ana dice: Abuela dice: ¿dónde está el correo?" NARR: "Luis dice: ¡Ah! No sé." NARR: "Carmen dice: Yo sé. Está cerca." Carmen points; the abuela nods and thanks them.
3. NARR: "Ana dice: Abuela no habla español. Y Beto no habla español." Ask (signed, Beto): "¿Quién no habla español?" He touches him: Beto holds up his ice cream and says something: a bubble with the flag of the United States. NARR: "Ana dice: Coquí, ¿entiendes?" COQUÍ [TURN: no (no)]: "No. No entiendo."

**The turn**

4. Ana's mamá calls her from across the square; Ana runs off. NARR: "Ana dice: ¡Ya vengo!" The abuela comes back, lost again, with a new question: her bubble shows the flag and a guagua. Everybody looks at everybody. The card shows two places. The signs go.

**The game.** Somebody needs a helper who speaks his language. Two people come up, drawn from Luis, Carmen, the señor, the señora and Ana's mamá; each says what he speaks: one English, one Spanish; who, and who speaks first, is drawn, each round on its own. The frog touches the one who can help.

5. **Decision A, touch a person (two people; tests inglés).** The abuela needs somebody who speaks English. NARR: "La señora dice: Hablo español." NARR: "La mamá de Ana dice: Hablo inglés." (Drawn.)
   - Right: the helper listens to the abuela, nods and points the way; the abuela goes off, pleased. A star.
   - Wrong: the one he touched listens, and shrugs. NARR: "La señora dice: Perdón. No entiendo." Beto, on the seat, sighs and eats a scoop. A cross. The sign is on the right one; he touches her: she helps.
6. **Decision B, touch a person (two others; tests español).** Beto has finished waiting: he stands at the piragüero's cart and points; the piragüero spreads his hands. NARR: "El piragüero dice: No entiendo. No hablo inglés." Beto needs somebody who speaks Spanish for him. Two people come up. NARR: "Luis dice: Hablo español." NARR: "El señor dice: Hablo inglés." (Drawn: in this round it is the one who speaks Spanish who can help, because he can ask the piragüero; if he speaks Spanish only he and Beto point at the red bottle together.)
   - Right: the helper speaks to the piragüero, who fills a cone. A star.
   - Wrong: the one he touched bends down to Beto and they talk, but the piragüero still spreads his hands. NARR: "El piragüero dice: Perdón. No entiendo." Beto eats a scoop. A cross. The sign; the right one helps.

**The ending**

7. Ana comes back. Beto has a piragua in one hand and his ice cream in the other.
   - Three scoops left: Beto holds the ice cream out to the frog, who takes the top scoop on his tongue. Stars and a heart.
   - Two left: Beto and the frog lick it in turn.
   - One left: Beto gives the frog the cone's last bite.
8. NARR: "Ana dice: Coquí, ¿hablas inglés?" COQUÍ [TURN: no (no)]: "No. Hablo español." **(a turn that says a new word: to settle in the reviews; the turn word is no)** NARR: "Ana dice: Yo hablo inglés y español. ¡Y Beto habla mantecado!" **(mantecado: heard; the ice cream carries it)**

**Fewest hearings before a word can cost:** inglés 4 (steps 1, 2), español 4 (steps 2, 3), hablo 4 with habla 3. Not deciding: entiendo 3 (steps 1, 2, 3) with entiendes once; no sé 1 and sé 1 before the game.
**To fix in the reviews if they agree:** no sé is heard once. Luis can say it again in round B's set-up ("¿Qué quiere Beto? No sé.").
**Forms:** habla, hablas count with hablo; entiendes with entiendo.
**A miss is filed under:** inglés in round A, español in round B.
**Chance without the Spanish:** one in two, each round on its own: nothing about the two shows what they speak.
**Speaking turns:** two (steps 3, 8): no; no.

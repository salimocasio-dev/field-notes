# Escuchar, place 1 (La casa): first set (draft 3, final: after two rounds of fresh reviews)

Escuchar is listening outside the map. It opens when the place's eleven stories are done. Nothing here is tested or scored, and nothing can be lost.
Three kinds of thing are in it:

1. **Otra vez.** Each of the eleven map stories, told straight through with nothing to touch: the story answers itself. Already built; no new lines.
2. **Cuentos con dibujos.** New picture stories in the four scenes already drawn. The picture carries the meaning. Words he has not met are allowed and are never tested. He only watches and listens.
3. **Haz esto.** Do-this routines: he is told to do things on screen, one short line at a time.

The learner is the little frog (Coquí). He has finished the eleven stories of La casa.
Words he has met: casa, puerta, ventana, abuela, hola, toca, abre, cierra, sí, no, grande, pequeña, adiós, silla, se sienta, vaso, jugo, agua, toma, quiero, por favor, gracias, de nada, ¿cómo estás?, bien, mamá, papá, abuelo, bebé, familia, hermano, hermana, él, ella, yo, tú, soy, eres, me llamo, niño, niña, juega, flor, sol, aquí, allá, amigo, ven, mira, vamos, ¿qué es esto?, hasta mañana.
Also heard: pájaro, bizcocho, lluvia, mesa, balcón, sala, patio, bola, portón, foto, hace calor, para, con, también, ahora, quiere, tiene, entra, come, está, dónde, quién, mi, Luis, Carmen.

Voices: NARR is the narrator. "X dice:" lines are the narrator reporting a person; the words show in a bubble at that person. COQUÍ is the frog's own voice. The bird only chirps.
Every line below is said while the picture shows exactly that. (In brackets: what the picture does.)
The stories keep the plain present of the map stories ("Abuela abre la puerta", "Abuelo duerme").

## Cuentos con dibujos

### 1. Toc, toc (El frente)

1. NARR: "Esta es la casa de abuela." [the front of the house; the frog in the yard]
2. NARR: "La puerta está cerrada." [close on the shut door] "La ventana está cerrada." [close on the shut window]
3. NARR: "Coquí toca la puerta. ¡Toc, toc!" [he hops to the door and knocks]
4. NARR: "Abuela no abre." [nothing moves]
5. NARR: "Coquí toca la ventana. ¡Toc, toc!" [he knocks at the window]
6. NARR: "Abuela no abre la ventana." [nothing moves]
7. NARR: "¡Mira! Ahí viene la lluvia." [thunder; a dark cloud comes in; the first drops fall on the frog; he jumps back]
8. NARR: "Coquí toca la puerta. ¡Toc, toc, toc!" [he knocks hard]
9. NARR: "Abuela dice: ¿Quién es?" [the lights come on inside; her words show in a bubble on the door]
10. COQUÍ: "¡Soy yo, abuela! ¡Abre!" (the line he knows from story 1)
11. NARR: "Abuela abre la puerta." [the door opens; abuela stands in it]
12. NARR: "Abuela dice: ¡Hola, Coquí! Entra." [she waves]
13. NARR: "Coquí entra a la casa." [he hops in]
14. NARR: "Ahora está lloviendo." [rain] NARR: "Abuela cierra la puerta." [the door shuts]
15. NARR: "Abuela abre la ventana." [the shutters open; abuela and the frog at the window, dry, the rain outside]
16. NARR: "Abuela y Coquí miran la lluvia."

### 2. Agua para el pájaro (El balcón)

1. NARR: "Hace calor." [the porch; a hot sun; the frog in the garden with a sweat drop]
2. NARR: "Abuela dice: Ven al balcón, Coquí." [she waves from the porch; he hops up]
3. NARR: "Abuela se sienta en la silla grande." [she sits]
4. NARR: "Coquí se sienta en la silla pequeña." [he hops onto the small chair]
5. NARR: "Abuela entra a la casa." [she goes in] NARR: "¡Mira! Abuela tiene dos vasos." [she comes out with two glasses]
6. NARR: "Abuela pone los vasos en la mesa." [two glasses on the table] NARR: "Un vaso de jugo. Y un vaso de agua." [each twinkles] [abuela sits again]
7. NARR: "Abuela dice: El jugo es para ti, Coquí." [the frog hops off his chair to the table] NARR: "Coquí toma el jugo." [he lifts the juice and drinks it]
8. NARR: "¡Mira! El pájaro." [the bird flies in and lands by the glass of water] NARR: "El pájaro toma el agua." [it dips its beak; the glass goes down]
9. NARR: "El pájaro se sienta en la silla pequeña." [the bird lands on the frog's chair]
10. COQUÍ: "¡Mi silla!" [the frog looks at the bird on his chair]
11. NARR: "Abuela dice: Ven acá, Coquí." [she waves; the frog hops over beside her; a heart]

### 3. El bizcocho del bebé (La sala)

1. NARR: "Esta es la sala. Aquí está la familia." [mamá and papá on the sofa, abuelo in his armchair, the baby on its blanket, abuela standing, a covered dish on the low table]
2. NARR: "Abuelo duerme." [his eyes shut, a big Z over him]
3. NARR: "¡Mira! Un bizcocho." [the cover comes off: a cake cut in slices]
4. NARR: "Abuela dice: ¿Quién quiere bizcocho?" NARR: "Mamá dice: ¡Yo!" [she waves] NARR: "Papá dice: ¡Yo también!" [he waves]
5. NARR: "El bebé también quiere." [the baby claps]
6. NARR: "Abuela dice: Este es para mamá." [she puts a slice in the frog's hands; he carries it to mamá; she takes a bite] NARR: "Mamá dice: Gracias, Coquí."
7. NARR: "Abuela dice: Y este es para papá." [the same, to papá] NARR: "Papá dice: Gracias."
8. NARR: "El bebé no tiene bizcocho. El bebé llora." [the baby cries, tears]
9. NARR: "Abuela dice: Este es para el bebé." [the frog carries a slice to the baby; the baby eats and claps] NARR: "Ahora el bebé no llora. El bebé come."
10. NARR: "¿Y abuelo? Abuelo duerme." [the Z again]
11. NARR: "Coquí toca a abuelo." [the frog hops to him; abuelo wakes and laughs]
12. NARR: "Abuelo dice: ¿Bizcocho? ¡Sí, por favor!" [the frog brings him a slice; he eats] NARR: "Abuelo dice: Gracias, Coquí." COQUÍ: "De nada."
13. NARR: "Abuela dice: Y este es para ti, Coquí." [she brings the frog a plate] COQUÍ: "¡Gracias, abuela!"
14. NARR: "La familia come bizcocho." [everyone eats]

### 4. La foto de la familia (La sala)

1. NARR: "Abuelo tiene una cámara." [abuelo stands with a camera at his face; Luis and Carmen are in the room; three empty frames on the wall]
2. NARR: "Abuelo dice: Carmen, ven. Una foto." [Carmen runs to the middle and stands still] [a flash] NARR: "¡Mira! La foto de Carmen." [her photo is in the first frame]
3. NARR: "Abuelo dice: Ahora Luis." [Luis runs to the middle] [a flash] NARR: "La foto de Luis." [second frame]
4. NARR: "Abuelo dice: Ahora mamá y el bebé." [mamá stands in the middle with the baby on her arm] [a flash] NARR: "La foto de mamá y el bebé." [third frame]
5. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¿Y tú, abuelo?" [the three photos twinkle; then Carmen points at abuelo]
6. NARR: "Abuelo pone la cámara en la mesa." [he walks to the low table and sets the camera on it]
7. NARR: "Abuelo dice: ¡Vengan todos! ¡Ven, Coquí!" [everyone comes together in front of the sofa, abuelo with them, the frog in the middle]
8. [the camera on the table flashes by itself] NARR: "¡Mira! La foto de la familia." [a big framed photo of all of them, abuelo and the frog too, shows over the room]
9. COQUÍ: "¡Y yo también!"

### 5. La bola (El patio)

1. NARR: "Luis y Carmen están en el patio." [the yard, the beds full of flowers, the bird in its nest, the frog by the door]
2. NARR: "Luis juega con la bola." [he kicks it up and catches it] NARR: "Carmen juega también." [they kick it back and forth]
3. NARR: "Luis dice: ¡Mira, Carmen! ¡Mira!" [he kicks it very high]
4. NARR: "La bola está en el árbol." [the ball sits up in the tree, by the nest]
5. NARR: "Luis dice: ¡Mi bola!" NARR: "Carmen dice: La bola está allá." [she points up]
6. NARR: "Coquí va al árbol." [he hops under the tree] COQUÍ: "¡Amigo! ¡Mira! ¡La bola!"
7. NARR: "El pájaro mira la bola." [the bird hops out of the nest next to the ball]
8. NARR: "El pájaro toca la bola." [it pecks; the ball falls]
9. NARR: "¡La bola está aquí!" [the ball lands on the frog's head and bounces to Luis]
10. NARR: "Luis dice: ¡Gracias, amigo!" [the bird flies a loop]
11. NARR: "Ahora Luis juega, Carmen juega y Coquí juega." [the ball goes round the three of them]

### 6. Buenas noches (El patio)

1. NARR: "Mira el sol." [dusk; the sun sinks behind the fence] NARR: "¡Mira! Una estrella." [the first star]
2. NARR: "Mamá dice: ¡Niños! ¡Vamos para adentro!" [the family at the back door; mamá waves the children in]
3. NARR: "Carmen dice: Hasta mañana, Coquí." NARR: "Luis dice: Hasta mañana, amigo." COQUÍ: "¡Hasta mañana!" [they wave]
4. NARR: "Los niños entran a la casa." [Luis and Carmen go in at the door] NARR: "Mamá, papá y el bebé entran." [they go in]
5. NARR: "Abuelo dice: Buenas noches, Coquí." NARR: "Abuela dice: Buenas noches." COQUÍ: "Buenas noches, abuela. Buenas noches, abuelo." [abuelo and abuela wave, then go in]
6. NARR: "Abuela cierra la puerta." [the back door shuts]
7. NARR: "Ahora es de noche." [the sky goes dark, more stars]
8. NARR: "El pájaro duerme." [the bird in its nest, eyes shut, a Z]
9. NARR: "¡Mira! Un cucubano." [one firefly lights up] NARR: "Y otro. Y otro más." [two more]
10. NARR: "Coquí no duerme. Coquí canta." [the frog sings: the coquí call, three times; the fireflies blink back]
11. NARR: "Buenas noches, Coquí."

## Haz esto

How a routine works:
- Each line is said once. If he waits, it is said again; after the second repeat the big sign shows what to touch.
- Right: the thing happens. That is the reward; there is no praise after each one. One "¡Muy bien!" at the end.
- Wrong: nothing is lost. "No. Esa es la ventana." (the name of what he touched), then the big sign shows the right thing and he touches it.
- Only things with a name he knows can be wrong. A touch on anything else is just a poke.
- A thing he has only heard in passing is named once first ("Mira el portón."), with a twinkle.
- The lines are not all "toca": some are his own question form ("¿Dónde está…?"), some are other things to do (abre, cierra), and each routine ends with a line in two parts.
- Where two touches are both fair ("El agua es para abuela": the water, or abuela), both count.
- A line in two parts ("Toca a mamá y a papá") can be done in either order. A second touch on the part already done is only a poke.

### A. Abre y cierra (El frente). The door and the window start shut.

1. "Toca la puerta." [the frog knocks at the door]
2. "¿Dónde está la ventana?" [he hops to the window and knocks]
3. "Abre la puerta." [he opens the door; abuela is there and waves. "Abuela dice: ¡Hola!" She goes back in.]
4. "Abre la ventana." [he opens the shutters]
5. "Cierra la puerta." [he shuts it]
6. "Cierra la ventana." [he shuts them]
7. "Abre la ventana y toca a abuela." [first touch, the window: he opens it and abuela is there. Second touch, abuela: she waves. "Abuela dice: ¡Muy bien, Coquí!"]

### B. En el balcón (El balcón). Abuela stands by her chair.

0. NARR: "Esta es la mesa." [the table twinkles]
1. "Toca la silla grande." [abuela sits down on it]
2. "¿Dónde está la silla pequeña?" [the frog hops to it]
3. "Toca la mesa." [abuela brings two glasses and puts them on it]
4. "¿Dónde está el jugo?" [the frog takes the juice and drinks it]
5. "Toca el agua." [he takes the water and drinks it]
6. NARR: "¡Mira! El jugo y el agua." [two jugs on the table now; each twinkles]
7. "El agua es para abuela." [the water jug, or abuela, both count: the frog takes the water to her and fills her glass; she drinks. "Abuela dice: Gracias."]
8. "El jugo es para abuela." [the same with the juice]
9. "Toca la silla pequeña y la silla grande." [two touches, in either order: the frog hops to the small chair; abuela waves from the big one. A touch on abuela, who sits on it, counts as the big chair.]

### C. La familia (La sala). The family sits; the cake is covered; Luis and Carmen are in the room; abuelo is asleep.

1. "Toca a mamá." [she waves. "Mamá dice: ¡Hola!"]
2. "¿Dónde está papá?" [he waves. "Papá dice: ¡Hola!"]
3. "¿Dónde está el bebé?" [the baby claps]
4. "Toca a abuelo." [he wakes up and waves]
5. "Toca a Luis." [Luis waves]
6. "¿Dónde está Carmen?" [Carmen waves]
7. NARR: "¡Mira! El bizcocho." [the cover comes off]
8. "El bizcocho es para papá." [papá, or the cake, both count: the frog carries a slice to papá; he eats. "Papá dice: Gracias."]
9. "El bizcocho es para el bebé." [the same, to the baby; it eats and claps]
10. "Toca a mamá y a papá." [two touches, in either order: each waves; then both wave together]

### D. En el patio (El patio). Luis and Carmen in the yard; the ball; some flowers already in the beds.

0. NARR: "Mira el portón." [the gate twinkles]
1. "Toca al niño." [Luis waves]
2. "¿Dónde está la niña?" [Carmen waves]
3. "¿Dónde está el sol?" [it beams]
4. "Toca la bola." [Luis kicks it up and catches it]
5. "Toca al pájaro." [the bird flies a loop]
6. "Abre el portón." [the gate swings open]
7. Carmen walks behind the left bed. "Carmen dice: Una flor aquí." [her bed, or Carmen, both count: the frog plants one there]
8. "Carmen dice: Una flor allá." [the other bed: the frog plants one]
9. "Toca al niño y a la niña." [two touches, in either order: each waves; then Luis kicks the ball to Carmen and she kicks it back]

## Sizes (a first set, not the plan's four hours)

- Otra vez: eleven stories, about 15 minutes in all.
- Cuentos con dibujos: six stories, about 1 to 2 minutes each.
- Haz esto: four routines, about 1 to 2 minutes each.
- About 30 minutes of listening in this first set. The plan sizes Escuchar at about 4 hours a place; more sets would follow the same pattern.

## Not taken from the reviews, and why

- The Spanish reviewer would use the progressive in speech ("Abuelo está durmiendo"). The map stories use the plain present throughout, so these do too.
- "¡Soy yo, abuela! ¡Abre!" stays as it is: it is the line he already knows from story 1.
- "Toma" for drinking stays ("Coquí toma el jugo"), as in El jugo.

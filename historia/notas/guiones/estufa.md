# La estufa: three stories (draft 1, before reviews)

Place 3, La cocina. Scene 2 of 4. The learner is the little frog (Coquí). He has finished La casa (stories 1 to 11), El parque (12 to 23) and the first scene of La cocina, La mesa (24 to 26).

Words he has met (place 1): casa, puerta, ventana, abuela, hola, toca, abre, cierra, sí, no, grande, pequeña, adiós, silla, se sienta, vaso, jugo, agua, toma, quiero, por favor, gracias, de nada, ¿cómo estás?, bien, mamá, papá, abuelo, bebé, familia, hermano, hermana, él, ella, yo, tú, soy, eres, me llamo, niño, niña, juega, flor, sol, aquí, allá, amigo, ven, mira, vamos, ¿qué es esto?, hasta mañana.
Words he has met (place 2): perro, gato, árbol, parque, camina, corre, espera, rápido, despacio, hay, ¿dónde está?, uno, dos, tres, bola, tira, coge, salta, dame, mi, tu, arriba, abajo, más, otra vez, tiene, pájaro, pato, pez, vuela, nada, rojo, azul, verde, amarillo, blanco, negro, ¿de qué color?, ¿cuál?, hombre, mujer, señor, señora, joven, viejo, cansado, contento, triste, come, bebe, duerme.
Heard along the way so far: bizcocho, lluvia, mesa, balcón, sala, patio, portón, foto, hace calor, para, con, también, ahora, quiere, quieres, entra, está, están, dónde, quién, es, un, una, y, de, en, se llama, este, esta, ese, esa, a jugar, muy, va, van, por arriba, por abajo, no más, pan, lago, bote, pinta, piragua, banco, del, de la, dice, ay, pequeño, contenta, amigos, otra, su, luna, Luis, Carmen, Canelo.

Words he has met (place 3, La mesa): mesa, plato, taza, cuchara, tenedor, cuchillo, comida, cocina, pone, tengo hambre, tengo sed, listo. Heard there: chocolate, lista, a comer, tiene hambre, tiene sed, tienes, esa.

Scene words for La estufa (from the 40-scene list): arroz, habichuelas, pollo, huevo, olla, sal, caliente, frío, hace, huele, cuidado, ya.

The picture: abuela's stove, seen from the front and a little from above. It stands in the middle and has two burners; a small flame shows under a pot or pan that is on. Pots and pans are open at the top, so what is in them can be seen. On the left, a counter for what goes into the pots; a helper stands there. On the right, a counter for plates and cups, and a waste bowl. The cooks stand at the two sides of the stove. The frog stands on a stool in front, lower than the pots, so he hides nothing.

Voices, the signal for a right move, what follows a wrong move, pokes, answer bubbles and the corner card are as in La mesa (historia/notas/guiones/mesa.md). In short: a wrong move costs something in the picture that stays; then "No, Coquí." and a whole sentence, true at that moment; then the big sign shows the right one and he does it, with no star. A poke never names a thing that decides a round. Nobody points at or looks toward the answer in a decision.

The three games open differently: 27 with a touch on a thing, 28 (to come), 29 with something the frog does himself.

## Story 27: Arroz con habichuelas

- **Goal he can see, from the first frame:** two pots of water on the stove, a sack of rice and a bowl of red beans on the counter, three empty plates on the right. Help abuela and Luis cook the rice and the beans.
- **At stake:** abuela's food. Whatever goes into the wrong pot has to come out again: the cook skims it off into the waste bowl, and it lies there to the end. A pot that stays on the flame after it is ready burns: smoke, and a black pot, to the end. The card shows a cross for that round.
- **New words:** arroz, habichuelas, olla, hace.
- **Back again:** pone, dame, por favor, más, para, hay, aquí, otra, listo, mira, tengo hambre, familia, come.
- **Heard along the way:** listas.

**Start:** two pots of water steam on the stove, a flame under each. Abuela stands at the right of the stove, Luis at the left, each with a wooden spoon. Carmen stands at the counter by the sack of rice and the bowl of beans; a cup stands in each. The frog is on his stool.

1. NARR: "Aquí hay una olla." (The left pot twinkles.) NARR: "Y aquí hay otra olla." (The right pot.) NARR: "Aquí hay arroz." (The sack.) NARR: "Y aquí hay habichuelas." (The bowl.)

**The opening** (big sign, nothing can go wrong)

2. NARR: "Abuela dice: Dame arroz, por favor." The big sign is on the sack of rice. He touches it. The frog carries a cup of rice to abuela's pot and tips it in; while he does: NARR: "Coquí pone el arroz en la olla." Right signal. The water turns white with rice. Abuela stirs; while she does: NARR: "Abuela hace arroz."
3. NARR: "Luis dice: Dame habichuelas, por favor." The sign is on the bowl of beans. He touches it. The frog tips a cup of beans into Luis's pot. Right signal. The water turns red. Luis stirs; while he does: NARR: "Luis hace habichuelas."
4. **He calls for more, taught.** Abuela holds an empty cup up and looks at Carmen. Two speaking bubbles: "¡Arroz!" and "¡Habichuelas!" The big sign is on "¡Arroz!" He touches it. COQUÍ: "¡Arroz!" Carmen brings a cup of rice and tips it into abuela's pot; while she does: NARR: "Más arroz para abuela." Right signal.
5. Luis holds an empty cup up. The two speaking bubbles; the sign is on "¡Habichuelas!" He touches it. COQUÍ: "¡Habichuelas!" Carmen tips a cup of beans into Luis's pot; while she does: NARR: "Más habichuelas para Luis." Right signal.
6. **Which one is ready, taught.** Both pots are full and steaming. NARR: "Abuela dice: El arroz está listo." The big sign is on the pot of rice. He touches it. The frog turns its knob: the flame under the rice goes out. Right signal.
7. NARR: "Abuela dice: Las habichuelas están listas." The sign is on the pot of beans. He touches it. The flame goes out. Right signal.

**The turn**

8. Abuelo, mamá and papá look in at the door. NARR: "La familia dice: ¡Tengo hambre!" Abuela fills the three plates from the two pots, rice and beans on each, and Carmen carries them out; while she does: NARR: "La familia come arroz con habichuelas." The pots are empty. Luis looks into his. NARR: "Luis dice: ¡Tengo hambre!" Abuela and Luis fill the pots with water and light the flames again. Three clean plates stand on the right. The card with three empty places shows in the corner. The signs go away.

**The game** (no sign). Who cooks what is not fixed: it follows from what the first cook asks for.

9. **Decision A, Touch (which one).** Both pots hold water only. One of the two cooks (drawn fresh) holds a hand out. NARR: "Abuela dice: Dame habichuelas, por favor." (Or: "Dame arroz, por favor." Or Luis says it. Drawn fresh.) He touches the sack or the bowl.
   - Right: the frog tips a cup of it into that cook's pot. Right signal. A star. The other cook fetches a cup of the other thing for the other pot.
   - Wrong: the frog tips a cup of the other thing into that cook's pot. The cook looks into the pot, takes the skimmer and lifts it all out into the waste bowl. A cross. NARR: "No, Coquí. Mira. Aquí hay habichuelas." (The bowl of beans twinkles. Or: "Aquí hay arroz.", the sack.) The sign is on the right one; he touches it; the frog tips a cup of it in. The other cook fetches a cup of the other thing for the other pot.
10. **Decision B, Say (what it needs more of).** One pot is white with rice, the other red with beans. One of the two cooks (drawn fresh) holds an empty cup up and looks at Carmen, who stands between the sack and the bowl. Two speaking bubbles: "¡Arroz!" and "¡Habichuelas!"
   - Right: Carmen tips a cup of it into that pot. Right signal. A star. The other cook gets a cup of his own thing from Carmen too.
   - Wrong: Carmen tips a cup of what the frog called into that pot: beans into the rice (or rice into the beans). The cook skims it off into the waste bowl. A cross. NARR: "No, Coquí. Mira. Luis hace arroz." (The true one: that cook and what is in his pot.) The sign shows the right bubble; he says it; Carmen tips it in. The other cook gets his too.
11. **Decision C, Touch, last (which one is ready).** Both pots are full and steaming, both flames on. NARR: "Abuela dice: Las habichuelas están listas." (Or: "El arroz está listo." Drawn fresh. Abuela says it whoever cooks it.) He touches one of the two pots.
   - Right: the frog turns its flame off. Right signal. A star. A moment later abuela turns the other one off herself.
   - Wrong: the frog turns the flame off under the other pot. The pot that was ready stays on its flame: smoke rises from it and its side turns black. A cross. NARR: "No, Coquí. Mira las habichuelas." (Or: "Mira el arroz." The smoking pot twinkles.) The sign is on it; he touches it; its flame goes out. It stays black and smokes a little. Abuela lights the other pot again, and turns it off when it is ready.

**The ending** (the same lines in every ending)

12. Abuela fills the three plates from the two pots: rice and beans on each. From a pot that burnt, what goes on the plates is dark. By what went wrong:
   - Nothing (three stars): three good plates, an empty waste bowl. Abuela gives the frog a little plate of his own. Stars and a heart.
   - Otherwise: the waste bowl holds what was skimmed off; a burnt pot stands black on the stove, and its food is dark on the plates.
13. NARR: "Abuela hace arroz con habichuelas." NARR: "Abuela dice: ¡A comer!" COQUÍ: "¡Mmm! ¡Arroz con habichuelas!"

**Fewest hearings before a word can cost:** arroz 7 (step 1; step 2 three times; step 4: the bubble, the frog, the line after; step 5, the bubble; step 6; the turn), habichuelas 7 (step 1; step 3 twice; step 4, the bubble; step 5: the bubble, the frog, the line after; step 7; the turn). Not deciding, but heard: olla 3 (steps 1 and 2), hace 2 before the game and again in the corrections and the last line.

**A miss is filed under:** A: the thing that was asked. B: the thing that pot needed. C: the thing that was ready.

**Chance without the Spanish:** A one in two. B one in two. C one in two.

## Story 29: ¡Cuidado!

- **Goal he can see, from the first frame:** a pan of hot oil and a pot of hot chocolate on the stove; a jug of cold chocolate with ice in it and four clean cups on the counter; a bowl with three spoonfuls of batter beside abuela. Fry the fritters, and bring everybody the chocolate he wants.
- **At stake:** the merienda. There are three fritters, one each for Luis, Carmen and the frog: one that comes out too soon falls on the floor, and one that stays in too long burns black. There are four clean cups: a cup of the wrong chocolate is sipped once and put aside, and it stands there to the end. Whoever comes last goes without. The card shows a cross for that round.
- **New words:** caliente, frío, cuidado, ya.
- **Back again:** olla, chocolate, quiero, bebe, toma, más, mira, espera.
- **Heard along the way:** está caliente, está frío.

Hot chocolate is drawn steaming; cold chocolate has ice in it and a straw. A thought bubble shows one or the other.
The frog's own moves, in doing bubbles: he keeps his hands back and waits; or he takes the fritter out with the tongs.

**Start:** abuela stands at the pan, on the left of the stove. The pot of chocolate steams on the right burner. Luis stands at the counter between the jug of cold chocolate and the cups. The frog is on his stool, with the tongs.

1. NARR: "La olla está caliente." (The pot twinkles; steam.) NARR: "Aquí hay chocolate caliente." NARR: "Y aquí hay chocolate frío." (The jug twinkles.)

**The opening** (big sign, nothing can go wrong)

2. Papá comes in. NARR: "Papá dice: Quiero chocolate caliente." The big sign is on the pot. He touches it. Luis fills a cup from the pot. NARR: "Luis dice: ¡Ya!" The frog carries the cup to papá; while he does: NARR: "Abuela dice: ¡Cuidado! Está caliente." Right signal. While papá drinks: NARR: "Papá bebe chocolate caliente."
3. Carmen comes in. NARR: "Carmen dice: Quiero chocolate frío." The sign is on the jug. He touches it. Luis fills a cup from the jug. NARR: "Luis dice: ¡Ya!" The frog carries it to Carmen. Right signal. While she drinks: NARR: "Carmen bebe chocolate frío."
4. **What he does himself, taught.** Abuela drops a spoonful of batter into the pan; it sizzles. NARR: "Abuela dice: ¡Cuidado, Coquí!" Two doing bubbles: the frog with his hands back; the frog with the tongs out. The big sign is on the first. He touches it. The frog keeps his hands back and waits; while he does: NARR: "Coquí espera." Right signal. The fritter turns golden.
5. NARR: "Abuela dice: ¡Ya, Coquí!" The two doing bubbles; the sign is on the tongs. He touches it. The frog lifts the fritter out onto the plate. Right signal.
6. **He calls it out, taught.** Abuelo comes in; a thought bubble over him shows a steaming cup. Luis holds an empty cup and looks at the frog. Two speaking bubbles: "¡Caliente!" and "¡Frío!" The big sign is on "¡Caliente!" He touches it. COQUÍ: "¡Caliente!" Luis fills the cup from the pot and gives it to abuelo. Right signal. While he drinks: NARR: "Abuelo bebe chocolate caliente."
7. Mamá comes in; her thought bubble shows a cup with ice. The two speaking bubbles; the sign is on "¡Frío!" He touches it. COQUÍ: "¡Frío!" Luis fills a cup from the jug. Right signal. While she drinks: NARR: "Mamá bebe chocolate frío."

**The turn**

8. Luis and Carmen take the fritter from the plate, break it in two and eat it. The four cups are empty; everybody holds his out. NARR: "La familia dice: ¡Más chocolate!" Luis washes the four cups and stands them on the counter. Abuela shows the bowl: three spoonfuls of batter. NARR: "Abuela dice: ¡Otra vez!" The card with three empty places shows in the corner. The signs go away.

**The game** (no sign)

9. **Decision A, Do (wait, or take it out).** Abuela drops a spoonful of batter into the pan. It looks the same either time. NARR: "Abuela dice: ¡Ya, Coquí!" (Or: "¡Cuidado, Coquí!" Drawn fresh.) Two doing bubbles: hands back; tongs out.
   - Right, "¡Ya!": the frog lifts it out, golden, onto the plate. Right signal. A star.
   - Right, "¡Cuidado!": the frog waits; the fritter turns golden, abuela nods, and he lifts it out. Right signal. A star.
   - Wrong, he takes it out at "¡Cuidado!": it is soft and too hot to hold; it slips from the tongs and lands on the floor. A cross. NARR: "No, Coquí. Abuela dice: ¡Cuidado!" The sign is on the hands back; abuela drops the next spoonful in; he waits; it turns golden and comes out.
   - Wrong, he waits at "¡Ya!": the fritter turns black; smoke. Abuela lifts it out onto the edge of the stove. A cross. NARR: "No, Coquí. Abuela dice: ¡Ya!" The sign is on the tongs; abuela drops the next spoonful in; he lifts it out, golden.
   Abuela fries what batter is left. On the plate: three golden fritters, or two.
10. **Decision B, Touch (which chocolate).** One of the family (drawn fresh among papá, mamá, abuelo and Carmen) holds his empty cup out; what he had before does not matter. NARR: "Mamá dice: Quiero chocolate caliente." (Or: "chocolate frío". Drawn fresh.) He touches the pot or the jug.
   - Right: Luis fills a clean cup from it; the frog carries it over. Right signal. A star.
   - Wrong: Luis fills a clean cup from the one he touched; the frog carries it over. Mamá takes one sip: she fans her mouth (or: she shivers), and puts the cup aside on the right counter. A cross. A thought bubble over her shows the chocolate she asked for. NARR: "No, Coquí. Mira. Mamá quiere chocolate caliente." The sign is on the right one; he touches it; Luis fills another clean cup; she drinks.
11. **Decision C, Say, last (which chocolate).** Another of the family (drawn fresh) comes to the counter; a thought bubble over him shows a steaming cup, or a cup with ice (drawn fresh). Luis holds a clean cup and looks at the frog. Two speaking bubbles: "¡Caliente!" and "¡Frío!"
   - Right: Luis fills the cup from the pot (or the jug) and hands it over. Right signal. A star.
   - Wrong: Luis fills it with what the frog called. One sip; it is put aside. A cross. NARR: "No, Coquí. Mira. Abuelo quiere chocolate frío." (The thought bubble twinkles.) The sign shows the right bubble; he says it; Luis fills another clean cup.

**The ending** (the same lines in every ending)

12. Luis and Carmen take a fritter each. If a third is on the plate, it is the frog's. If a clean cup is left, abuela fills it for the frog; if another is left, she fills it for herself. By what went wrong:
   - Nothing (three stars): the frog has a fritter and a cup, abuela has a cup. Stars and a heart.
   - Otherwise: the fallen or the burnt fritter lies where it is, the cups that were put aside stand full on the counter, and the frog, or the frog and abuela, go without.
13. NARR: "La familia bebe chocolate." COQUÍ: "¡Mmm! ¡Chocolate!"

**Fewest hearings before a word can cost:** cuidado 3 (steps 2 and 4; and "¡Cuidado!" is heard a third time only in the game: one more is needed in the opening), ya 3 (steps 2, 3 and 5), caliente 8 (step 1 twice; step 2 three times; step 6: the bubble, the frog, the line after; step 7, the bubble), frío 6 (step 1; step 3 twice; step 6, the bubble; step 7: the bubble, the frog, the line after).

**A miss is filed under:** A: cuidado or ya (the one abuela said). B and C: caliente or frío (the one that was wanted).

**Chance without the Spanish:** A one in two. B one in two. C one in two.

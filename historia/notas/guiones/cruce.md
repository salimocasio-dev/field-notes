# El cruce: three stories (draft 1, before the reviews)

Place 5, La calle. Scene 4 of 4 (scene id `cruce`). Stories 57 to 59. The learner is the little frog (Coquí). He has finished stories 1 to 56.

Scene words (page 02): izquierda, derecha, derecho, al lado de, delante, detrás, entre, sigue, dobla, hasta, perdón.

The picture: a crossing of two streets in town, seen from the street the frog comes up: he stands at the bottom with his back to us, so his left is the picture's left. One view. Three ways leave the crossing: a street to the left, a street to the right, a street straight on. Where each leads is out of the picture: nothing in it says what is down a street (no signs, no shop fronts at the corners; the same low walls and trees on all three). At the near right corner is a small square: a tree, a bench with a back, a low wall (story 59: the family's photo). A set of cones can close the street to the right (story 58).

New person: the piragüero with his cart (he is the piragua man of El parque), story 58. The others: the family, the señora and the señor, don Pepe on his bicycle, the obrero of story 50.

How a walk is shown: whoever takes a street walks or rides out of the picture along it. NARR: "Luego..." Then they come up from the bottom again: the same crossing stands for the next one.

Voices, the signal for a right move, what follows a wrong move, pokes, answer bubbles and the corner card are as in El colmado. Every story has two scored rounds; the card shows two places. Every story ends with the shared `fin`.

## Story 57: ¿Izquierda o derecha?

- **Goal he can see:** a full bottle of water with three marks, standing on abuela's bag; a high, hot sun. Abuela fans herself.
- **At stake:** the water (3 parts). A walk down the wrong street and back, in the sun: the frog drinks a part.
- **New words:** izquierda, derecha, dobla, perdón.
- **Back again:** calle, correo, farmacia, banco, tienda, escuela, ¿adónde vas?, voy, vas, bicicleta, señor, señora, agua, tengo sed, cansado, luego, toma, gracias.

**Start:** midday. Abuela and the frog come up to the crossing. Abuela has a letter in her hand and looks left, right and straight on.

**The opening** (big sign)

1. NARR: "Abuela y Coquí están en el cruce." **(cruce: heard)** NARR: "Abuela dice: Tengo una carta. ¿Dónde está el correo?" Don Pepe rides up on his bicycle and stops. NARR: "Don Pepe dice: ¡Buenos días, Coquí! ¿Adónde vas?" COQUÍ [TURN: correo (correo)]: "¡Al correo!"
2. NARR: "Abuela dice: Perdón, don Pepe. ¿Dónde está el correo?" Don Pepe points; the street to the left twinkles. NARR: "Don Pepe dice: Dobla a la izquierda." Ask (signed, the left street): "Toca la calle de la izquierda." He touches it: abuela and the frog walk out along it. NARR: "Luego..." They come up from the bottom again without the letter.
3. The señora crosses in front of them. NARR: "Abuela dice: Perdón, señora. ¿Dónde está la farmacia?" The señora points; the street to the right twinkles. NARR: "La señora dice: Dobla a la derecha." Ask (signed, the right street): "Toca la calle de la derecha." He touches it: they walk out along it, and come up again with a small paper bag.
4. Luis rides up from behind them. NARR: "Luis dice: Perdón, abuela. ¿Dónde está la escuela?" NARR: "Abuela dice: Dobla a la izquierda, Luis." Ask (signed, the left street): "¿Dónde dobla Luis?" He touches it: Luis rides off to the left. NARR: "Luis va a la izquierda. La farmacia está a la derecha."

**The turn**

5. Abuela sits down on the bench in the square, in the tree's shade, and stands the bottle beside her. She holds her purse out to the frog. NARR: "Abuela dice: ¡Qué calor! Estoy cansada. Coquí, ¿vas tú?" COQUÍ [TURN: voy (voy)]: "¡Sí, voy!" The card shows two places. The signs go.

**The game.** The frog goes for two things. At the crossing he stops somebody and asks; the answer is left or right, drawn, each round on its own. Where he is going is drawn too (the banco, the tienda, the colmado).

6. **Decision A, touch a thing (the left street, the right street; tests the word said).** The señor walks by. COQUÍ: "Perdón. ¿Dónde está el banco?" NARR: "El señor dice: Dobla a la derecha." (Or, drawn: "Dobla a la izquierda.")
   - Right: the frog hops out along that street. NARR: "Luego..." He comes up from the bottom with the thing (a coin purse; a kite; a loaf). A star.
   - Wrong: the frog hops out along the other street. He comes back along it with nothing, slowly, tongue out. Abuela holds the bottle to his mouth: the water goes down a mark. A cross. NARR: "El señor dice: No, Coquí. Dobla a la derecha." The sign is on the right street; he touches it: out, and back with the thing.
7. **Decision B, touch a thing (the same game; Carmen is asked).** COQUÍ: "Perdón, Carmen. ¿Dónde está la tienda?" NARR: "Carmen dice: Dobla a la izquierda." (Drawn.) Right and wrong as in A.

**The ending**

8. The frog puts the two things on the bench. NARR: "Abuela dice: ¡Gracias, Coquí! ¿Tienes sed?" COQUÍ [TURN: tengo sed (tengo sed)]: "¡Sí! Tengo sed."
   - Three parts left: abuela drinks, the frog drinks, and abuela pours the last part over the frog's head; he shuts his eyes and smiles. Stars and a heart.
   - Two left: abuela drinks, the frog drinks.
   - One left: abuela gives it to the frog and fans herself.
9. NARR: "Abuela dice: Y ahora, a casa. ¿Izquierda o derecha?" The frog points both ways at once; abuela laughs. NARR: "Abuela dice: ¡Derecha! Vamos."

**Fewest hearings before a word can cost:** izquierda 5 (steps 2 and 4), derecha 3 (steps 3 and 4). Not deciding: dobla 4; perdón 3 before the game, then twice in the frog's own voice.
**A miss is filed under:** the word that was said (izquierda or derecha).
**Chance without the Spanish:** one in two, each round on its own: the three streets look alike.
**Speaking turns:** three (steps 1, 5, 8): correo; voy; tengo sed.

## Story 58: Sigue derecho

- **Goal he can see:** a big block of ice in the basket of Luis's bicycle, dripping. A thought over Luis and the frog: a piragua.
- **At stake:** the ice (3 sizes). A ride down the wrong street and back melts it a size.
- **New words:** derecho, sigue, hasta.
- **Back again:** dobla, izquierda, perdón, calle, bicicleta, esquina, banco, farmacia, cerrado, rápido, piragua, frío, señor, señora, toma, gracias, va, viene.

**Start:** afternoon, hot. Luis rides up to the crossing with the block of ice in his basket and the frog on the handlebar. The street to the right is shut off by cones; the obrero stands there.

**The opening** (big sign)

1. NARR: "Luis y Coquí van en bicicleta. Tienen hielo para el piragüero." **(hielo, piragüero: heard; the picture carries them)** A drop falls from the basket. The señor walks across the crossing, away from them. NARR: "Luis dice: ¿Dónde está el piragüero? El señor sabe. ¡Coquí!" COQUÍ [TURN: perdón (perdón, perdon)]: "¡Perdón, señor!" The señor turns round.
2. NARR: "El señor dice: ¿El piragüero? Sigue derecho hasta la esquina." He points straight on; that street twinkles. Ask (signed, the street straight on): "Luis sigue derecho. Toca la calle." He touches it: Luis rides straight on and out. NARR: "Luego..." They come up from the bottom again.
3. Don Pepe stands at the corner. NARR: "Don Pepe dice: ¿El piragüero? Dobla a la izquierda. Luego sigue derecho hasta el banco." Ask (signed, the left street): "¿Dónde dobla Luis?" He touches it: Luis rides out to the left; they come up from the bottom again. NARR: "Y ahora Luis sigue derecho hasta el banco." Ask (signed, the street straight on): "Toca la calle. Luis sigue derecho." He touches it: straight on and out; up from the bottom again.

**The turn**

4. Luis looks into the basket: the block drips fast; a small puddle under the bicycle. The obrero waves at the cones. NARR: "El obrero dice: Esta calle está cerrada." NARR: "Luis dice: ¡El hielo! ¿Vamos rápido o despacio?" COQUÍ [TURN: rápido (rápido, rapido)]: "¡Rápido!" The card shows two places. The signs go.

**The game.** The right street is closed. Somebody at the crossing says: straight on, or turn left. Drawn, each round on its own; the place named after "hasta" is drawn too (la farmacia, el correo, la tienda) and changes nothing.

5. **Decision A, touch a thing (the street straight on, the left street; tests the word said).** The señora is asked. NARR: "Luis dice: Perdón, señora. ¿El piragüero?" NARR: "La señora dice: Sigue derecho hasta la farmacia." (Or, drawn: "Dobla a la izquierda, hasta la farmacia.")
   - Right: Luis rides out along it; they come up from the bottom again. A star.
   - Wrong: Luis rides out along the other street, and comes back along it, puffing; a trail of drops behind the bicycle. The block is a size smaller. A cross. NARR: "La señora dice: No. Sigue derecho hasta la farmacia." The sign is on the right street; he touches it: out, and up from the bottom again.
6. **Decision B, touch a thing (the same game; Carmen is asked).** NARR: "Carmen dice: Dobla a la izquierda, hasta el correo." (Drawn.) Right and wrong as in A.

**The ending**

7. A bell: the piragüero pushes his cart into the square from the street straight on, looking for them. NARR: "El piragüero dice: ¡Mi hielo! ¡Gracias, Luis! ¡Gracias, Coquí!" He lifts the block onto his cart and scrapes it.
   - A big block: three piraguas: Luis, the frog, and one the piragüero eats himself. Stars and a heart.
   - A smaller block: two piraguas: Luis and the frog.
   - A small block: one piragua with two spoons.
8. NARR: "El piragüero dice: Toma, Coquí. ¿Está fría?" COQUÍ [TURN: frío (fría, fria, frío, frio)]: "¡Sí! ¡Muy fría!" NARR: "Luis y Coquí siguen derecho hasta la casa."

**Fewest hearings before a word can cost:** sigue 5 and derecho 5 (steps 2 and 3). Not deciding: hasta 3 before the game (steps 2 and 3), then in every round.
**A miss is filed under:** derecho when "Sigue derecho" was said; dobla when "Dobla a la izquierda" was said.
**Chance without the Spanish:** one in two, each round on its own. "Derecho" is never set against "derecha": the right street is closed in this story.
**Speaking turns:** three (steps 1, 4, 8): perdón; rápido; frío.

## Story 59: La foto

- **Goal he can see:** papá's camera, the kind whose picture slides out of the front. Three white sheets show at its side (and on the card).
- **At stake:** the good photos (3 sheets). The frog in the wrong place when the flash goes: the picture comes out spoiled (his back over a face, or only his eyes at the edge).
- **New words:** al lado de, delante, detrás, entre.
- **Back again:** familia, abuela, abuelo, mamá, papá, bebé, hermano, hermana, perro, árbol, aquí, uno, dos, tres, yo, soy, mira, también, contento.

**Start:** late afternoon, a soft light. The small square at the crossing: the tree, the bench, the low wall. The family walks in; papá has the camera round his neck.

**The opening** (big sign)

1. NARR: "La familia está en el cruce. Papá tiene una cámara." **(cámara: heard)** NARR: "Papá dice: ¡Una foto de la familia!" Abuelo, mamá with the bebé, and Carmen stand in a row behind the bench.
2. NARR: "Papá dice: Abuela, delante de abuelo." Abuela sits down on the bench in front of abuelo. NARR: "Papá dice: Luis, delante de Carmen." Luis sits down in front of Carmen. Ask (signed, Luis): "¿Quién está delante de Carmen?" He touches him: Luis waves.
3. NARR: "Abuelo está detrás de abuela. Carmen está detrás de Luis." Ask (signed, abuelo): "¿Quién está detrás de abuela?" He touches him: abuelo lays his hands on abuela's shoulders.
4. Canelo trots in. NARR: "Papá dice: Canelo, al lado de Luis." Canelo sits down at the bench's end, beside Luis. NARR: "Carmen está al lado de mamá." Ask (signed, Canelo): "¿Quién está al lado de Luis?" He touches him: Canelo barks once.
5. Mamá lifts the bebé. NARR: "El bebé está entre abuelo y mamá. Y mamá está entre abuelo y Carmen." Ask (signed, mamá): "¿Quién está entre abuelo y Carmen?" He touches her: she smiles; the bebé claps.

**The turn**

6. Papá looks through the camera and lowers it. NARR: "Papá dice: ¿Y Coquí? ¿Dónde está Coquí?" The frog hops out from behind the tree. COQUÍ [TURN: aquí (aquí, aqui)]: "¡Aquí!" Papá taps the three sheets. NARR: "Papá dice: Tres fotos. Y Coquí va en las tres." The card shows two places and the three sheets. The signs go.

**The game.** Papá says where the frog goes; then he presses the button. Round A is in front of or behind one person (abuela or Luis, drawn): the ground at that person's feet, or the top of the bench's back behind that person's shoulder. Round B is beside abuela (the free end of the bench) or between abuela and Luis (the gap on the bench). Which is said is drawn, each round on its own.

7. **Decision A, touch a place (two places; tests the word said).** NARR: "Papá dice: Coquí, ¡detrás de abuela!" (Or, drawn: "¡delante de abuela!"; or Luis.)
   - Right: the frog hops there. NARR: "Papá dice: ¡Uno, dos...!" COQUÍ [TURN: tres (tres)]: "¡Tres!" The flash. A sheet slides out and is shown big for a moment: everybody, and the frog where he should be. A star.
   - Wrong: the frog hops to the other place. NARR: "Papá dice: ¡Uno, dos, tres!" The flash. The sheet is shown big: the frog's back over a face, or only his eyes at the bottom edge. Papá laughs and shakes his head. A cross: a sheet spoiled. NARR: "Papá dice: No, Coquí. Detrás de abuela." The sign is on the right place; he touches it: the frog hops there, and papá gives him a thumb up (no picture is taken).
   - The turn "¡Tres!" is in the right branch only when round A is right; when it is wrong papá counts to three himself. (To settle in the reviews: a turn that some runs do not have.)
8. **Decision B, touch a place (two places; tests the word said).** NARR: "Papá dice: Coquí, ¡entre abuela y Luis!" (Or, drawn: "¡al lado de abuela!") Right and wrong as in A; papá counts to three himself.

**The ending**

9. Papá stands the camera on the low wall, presses something and runs to the bench. NARR: "Papá dice: ¡Y ahora yo también!" He kneels beside Canelo; the frog hops onto abuelo's head. The flash: the last sheet, always a good one. The photos are laid out on the bench.
   - Three good photos: the family looks at all three; abuela holds one to her heart. Stars and a heart.
   - Two good, one spoiled: they laugh at the spoiled one; Luis holds it up.
   - One good, two spoiled: the bebé chews a spoiled one; mamá takes it away, laughing.
10. Abuela holds out the last photo and points at the frog in it. NARR: "Abuela dice: Mira la foto. ¿Quién es?" COQUÍ [TURN: yo (yo)]: "¡Soy yo!" NARR: "Coquí está en la foto, entre abuela y abuelo. La familia está contenta."

**Fewest hearings before a word can cost:** delante 3 (step 2), detrás 3 (step 3), al lado de 3 (step 4), entre 3 (step 5).
**A miss is filed under:** the word that was said.
**Chance without the Spanish:** round A one in two (one person, two places). Round B: a learner who knows the names and not the two words is right three times in four ("entre abuela y Luis" names Luis, and only the gap is next to him; "al lado de abuela" fits both places for such a learner).
**Speaking turns:** three or two (steps 6, 7, 10): aquí; tres (only on a right round A); yo.

# La cuenta: three stories (draft 1, before the reviews)

Place 8, La fonda. Scene 4 of 4 (scene id `cuenta`). Stories 93 to 95. The learner is the little frog (Coquí). He has finished stories 1 to 92.

Scene words (page 02): cuenta, tarjeta, efectivo, propina, total, postre, flan, helado, nada más, satisfecho, hasta luego, buenas noches.
How they are shared out: 93 postre, flan, helado, nada más; 94 cuenta, total, tarjeta, efectivo; 95 propina, satisfecho, hasta luego, buenas noches.

The picture: the front of the fonda, late at night. One view. Along the back wall, a counter: at its left a glass case with desserts; in its middle the register (the caja), a card machine, a spike with paper slips, a glass jar; at the right of the counter, the open door, with the night street beyond it: three street lamps in a row going away, and far off the lights of a guagua. In front of the counter, at the left, the family's table (they sit in a row along its far side); the rest of the floor is free. The dessert cart stands by the table.

People: the mesera, the mesero, the cocinero; abuela, abuelo, Luis, Carmen; guests (the señor, the señora, the joven, the mujer).

Voices, the signal for a right move, what follows a wrong move, pokes and the corner card are as in La calle. Every story has two scored rounds; the card shows two places. Every story ends with the shared `fin`. Every story starts fresh. Words: the everyday Puerto Rican word wins over the plan's (page 16).

## Story 93: El postre

- **Goal he can see:** the mesera makes a cup of ice cream for her helper: three scoops. She sets it on the cart's top, beside the frog. For afterwards.
- **At stake:** the three scoops. The night is warm: each mix-up takes time, and a scoop melts away.
- **New words:** postre, flan, helado, nada más.
- **Back again:** delicioso, prueba, para mí, quisiera, mesera, quiero, también, hay, frío, por favor, gracias, no, sí, después, ¿algo más?, toca.

**Start:** late evening. The family at its table; the plates are empty. The mesera rolls up the dessert cart: small caramel flans on plates, a tub of ice cream with a scoop, cups; a domed cover hangs at the cart's side. The frog hops onto the cart.

**The opening** (big sign)

1. NARR: "La mesera dice: ¿Postre? Hay flan y hay helado." Ask (signed, a flan): "¿Dónde está el flan?" He touches it: it wobbles. NARR: "Luis dice: ¡Postre! ¡Helado, helado!" Ask (signed, the tub): "¿Dónde está el helado?" He touches it: the frog scoops a ball into a cup; it goes to Luis.
2. NARR: "Carmen dice: De postre, flan, por favor." Ask (signed, a flan): "Flan para Carmen. ¿Cuál es?" He touches it: the frog pushes a flan to her. NARR: "Abuela dice: Y para mí, flan también."
3. The mesera turns to abuelo. NARR: "La mesera dice: ¿Y para el señor? ¿Flan o helado?" Abuelo pats his belly. NARR: "Abuelo dice: No, gracias. Nada más. Estoy satisfecho." **(satisfecho: heard; a word of story 95)** NARR: "La mesera dice: ¿Nada más?" NARR: "Abuelo dice: Nada más, gracias." Ask (signed, the cover): "Nada más. ¿Qué toca Coquí?" He touches it: the frog swings the cover down over the desserts.
4. The cover goes up again. NARR: "La mesera dice: Helado para Luis. Flan para Carmen." COQUÍ [TURN: para mí (y para mí, para mí)]: "¿Y para mí?" The mesera laughs. She fills a cup: one scoop, two, three, and sets it on the cart's top. NARR: "La mesera dice: Helado para ti, Coquí. Después."

**The turn**

5. The mesera pushes the cart, with the frog on it, to the next table. The three scoops shine; a first drop runs down the cup. The card shows two places. The signs go.

**The game.** A guest at the next table (drawn) says one of three things (drawn): flan, ice cream, or nothing more. The frog pushes a flan across, scoops a cup of ice cream, or swings the cover down.

6. **Decision A, touch a thing (the flans, the tub, the cover; tests flan, helado and nada más).** NARR: "El señor dice: De postre, helado, por favor." (Or, drawn: "De postre, flan, por favor." Or: "Nada más, gracias.")
   - Right: the frog does it. A nod (after "nada más": he pats his belly). A star.
   - Wrong: the frog does another thing; the guest holds up a hand. NARR: "El señor dice: No, Coquí. Helado." While the mesera puts the cart right again, a scoop in the frog's cup melts away. A cross. The sign is on the right thing; he touches it.
7. **Decision B, touch a thing (the same game; another guest).** NARR: "La mujer dice: Nada más, gracias." (Drawn.) Right and wrong as in A.

**The ending**

8. The mesera hands the frog his cup.
   - Three scoops. Stars and a heart.
   - Two scoops, in a little pool.
   - One scoop, in a big pool; the frog drinks the pool too.
9. The frog takes a spoonful. NARR: "La mesera dice: ¿Cómo está el helado, Coquí?" COQUÍ [TURN: delicioso (delicioso, está delicioso)]: "¡Delicioso!" NARR: "De postre, helado para Coquí. Y nada más."

**Fewest hearings before a word can cost:** flan 6 (steps 1 to 4), helado 7 (steps 1, 3, 4), nada más 4 (step 3). Not deciding: postre 3 (steps 1, 2).
**A miss is filed under:** the one that was said (flan, helado or nada más).
**Chance without the Spanish:** one in three, each round on its own.
**Speaking turns:** two (steps 4, 9): para mí; delicioso.

## Story 94: La cuenta, por favor

- **Goal he can see:** the glass jar beside the register. Guests who go away content drop a coin in it: three coins shine in it. The mesera taps the jar, and the frog's nose: for the two of us.
- **At stake:** the three coins. A guest who gets a muddle takes his coin back out.
- **New words:** cuenta, total, tarjeta, efectivo.
- **Back again:** nada más, ¿algo más?, ¿cuánto es?, peso, cambio, caja, paga, veinte, treinta, cuarenta, cincuenta, sesenta, setenta, ochenta, noventa, mesera, señor, aquí tiene, toma, toca, con, por favor, gracias, no.

**Start:** late evening. The mesera behind the register; the frog on the counter beside the jar. On the counter: the card machine, the spike with slips. The family in front of the counter; abuelo has his wallet in his hand, and no glasses on.

**The opening** (big sign)

1. NARR: "Abuelo dice: La cuenta, por favor." The mesera pulls a slip off the spike. NARR: "La mesera dice: La cuenta. Aquí tiene." Ask (signed, the slip): "¿Dónde está la cuenta?" He touches it: the frog carries the slip to abuelo.
2. Abuelo holds the slip far off, and near; he cannot read it. NARR: "Abuelo dice: ¿Cuánto es?" NARR: "La mesera dice: El total es cuarenta pesos." The slip grows for a moment: at its foot, a big 40. NARR: "Abuela dice: Cuarenta pesos. Mira, Coquí: el total." Ask (signed, the number at the slip's foot): "¿Dónde está el total?" He touches it: the 40 shines.
3. NARR: "La mesera dice: ¿Tarjeta o efectivo?" Abuelo opens his wallet: a card, and bills. He takes out the card. NARR: "Abuelo dice: Tarjeta." Ask (signed, the card machine): "Tarjeta. ¿Qué toca Coquí?" He touches it: the frog pushes the machine to abuelo; a beep. NARR: "La mesera dice: Con tarjeta. Gracias." Abuelo drops a coin in the jar.
4. The señor steps up. NARR: "El señor dice: La cuenta, por favor." NARR: "La mesera dice: El total es treinta pesos. ¿Tarjeta o efectivo?" He lays bills on the counter. NARR: "El señor dice: Efectivo." Ask (signed, the register's drawer): "Efectivo. ¿Qué toca Coquí?" He touches it: the drawer springs open; the frog puts the bills in. NARR: "La mesera dice: En efectivo. Aquí tiene el cambio." The señor drops a coin in the jar.
5. The mesera holds out the bowl of mints to the frog. He takes one. NARR: "La mesera dice: Toma, Coquí. ¿Algo más?" COQUÍ [TURN: nada más (nada más, nada más gracias)]: "Nada más, gracias."

**The turn**

6. A third coin drops into the jar. More guests come up to pay: a line. The mesera taps the jar, and the frog's nose. The card shows two places. The signs go.

**The game.** Two rounds, two games.

7. **Decision A, touch a slip (three slips lie on the counter, each with a big number at its foot; tests the number under "el total").** A guest asks for the bill. NARR: "La señora dice: La cuenta, por favor." The mesera reads from the register, which is turned away from us. NARR: "La mesera dice: El total es sesenta pesos." (Drawn from veinte to noventa; of the two other slips, one is the number nearest in sound, as setenta is to sesenta.)
   - Right: the frog carries the slip over; the guest nods and pays. A star.
   - Wrong: the frog carries another slip over; the guest reads it, and her eyebrows go up. NARR: "La mesera dice: No, Coquí. El total es sesenta pesos." The guest takes her coin back out of the jar. A cross. The sign is on the right slip; he touches it.
8. **Decision B, touch a thing (the card machine, the register's drawer; tests tarjeta against efectivo).** NARR: "La mesera dice: ¿Tarjeta o efectivo?" The guest's hands are in his pockets. NARR: "El joven dice: Efectivo, por favor." (Or, drawn: "Con tarjeta, por favor.")
   - Right: for "efectivo" the drawer springs open and he lays down his bills; for "tarjeta" he takes the machine: a beep. A star.
   - Wrong: the frog pushes the machine at a guest who holds out bills (or springs the drawer at a guest who holds out a card). NARR: "El joven dice: No. Efectivo." He takes his coin back out of the jar. A cross. The sign is on the right thing; he touches it.

**The ending**

9. The last guest goes. The mesera tips the jar out on the counter.
   - Three coins: she counts them: one for her, one for the frog, and the third she spins on the counter: for the frog too. Stars and a heart.
   - Two coins: one each.
   - One coin: she slides it to the frog.
10. NARR: "La mesera dice: Esto es para ti, Coquí." COQUÍ [TURN: gracias (gracias, muchas gracias)]: "¡Gracias!" NARR: "La cuenta, el total, y todo en la caja."

**Fewest hearings before a word can cost:** total 4 (steps 2, 4), tarjeta 5 (steps 3, 4), efectivo 4 (step 4). Not deciding: cuenta 5 (steps 1, 4).
**A miss is filed under:** A: the number that was said; B: the one that was said (tarjeta or efectivo).
**Chance without the Spanish:** A one in three; B one in two.
**Speaking turns:** two (steps 5, 10): nada más; gracias.

## Story 95: Buenas noches

- **Goal he can see:** through the open door, the street: three lamps in a row, and far away, under the last one, the lights of the guagua: the last one tonight. Abuela points at it, and at the stop outside the door.
- **At stake:** the time before the guagua comes (three lamps). Each mix-up takes time: the guagua's lights come one lamp nearer.
- **New words:** propina, satisfecho, hasta luego, buenas noches.
- **Back again:** cuenta, mesero, mesera, cocinero, flan, para, guagua, parada, vamos, rápido, también, todo, bien, casa, adiós, esto, por favor, gracias, no, sí.

**Start:** late night; chairs stand on the tables. By the door, in a row, the mesera, the mesero and the cocinero, each with a small cup in his hand. The frog on the counter, beside the door. The family at the counter.

**The opening** (big sign)

1. Abuelo nudges the frog and nods towards the mesero. COQUÍ [TURN: la cuenta por favor (la cuenta por favor, la cuenta)]: "¡La cuenta, por favor!" The mesero brings the slip; abuelo pays: a beep. NARR: "El mesero dice: Gracias. ¿Todo bien?" Abuelo pats his belly. NARR: "Abuelo dice: Todo bien. Estoy satisfecho." NARR: "Abuela dice: Y yo, satisfecha." NARR: "Luis dice: ¡Yo también estoy satisfecho!"
2. Abuelo takes a coin out of his pocket. NARR: "Abuelo dice: Y esto es la propina. La propina es para el mesero." Ask (signed, the mesero): "La propina es para el mesero. ¿Dónde está el mesero?" He touches him: the frog carries the coin over; it clinks in the mesero's cup. NARR: "El mesero dice: ¡Una propina! Gracias."
3. NARR: "Abuela dice: Una propina para la mesera." Ask (signed, the mesera): "¿Dónde está la mesera?" He touches her: the frog carries abuela's coin over; a clink. NARR: "Carmen dice: ¡Y una propina para el cocinero! Me gusta el flan." The frog carries her coin over; a clink; the cocinero bows.
4. The family goes to the door. NARR: "La mesera dice: Buenas noches. ¡Hasta luego!" NARR: "Abuela dice: Buenas noches. Hasta luego." NARR: "El cocinero dice: ¡Hasta luego, Luis! ¡Hasta luego, Carmen!" Abuela looks out at the street: far off, the guagua's lights. NARR: "Abuela dice: ¡La guagua! Vamos, Coquí."

**The turn**

5. The frog turns to go; but the last guests, on their way out, stop in front of him, each with a coin. The family waits in the doorway. The guagua's lights stand under the far lamp. The card shows two places. The signs go.

**The game.** The mesera, the mesero and the cocinero stand in a row (their order is drawn each round: they are putting chairs up). A guest (drawn) gives the frog a coin and says whom it is for (drawn). The frog carries it to one of the three.

6. **Decision A, touch a person (three; tests mesera against mesero, and cocinero).** NARR: "El señor dice: Buenas noches. La propina es para la mesera." (Drawn.)
   - Right: a clink in her cup; she waves. NARR: "El señor dice: Hasta luego." A star.
   - Wrong: a clink in the wrong cup. NARR: "El señor dice: No, Coquí. Para la mesera." The frog fishes the coin out and runs across with it. In the door, the guagua's lights have come one lamp nearer. A cross. The sign is on the right person; he touches her.
7. **Decision B, touch a person (the same game; the three in a new order, another guest).** NARR: "La mujer dice: Estoy satisfecha. La propina es para el cocinero." (Drawn.) Right and wrong as in A.

**The ending**

8. The frog hops out of the door after the family.
   - The guagua is still far: they walk to the stop; the guagua comes; a seat for each of them. Stars and a heart.
   - It is at the middle lamp: they walk fast, and get on.
   - It is at the stop: they run; abuelo holds his hat; they jump on, and the door shuts behind the frog.
9. From the guagua's window, the fonda's door: the three wave. NARR: "La mesera dice: ¡Buenas noches, Coquí! ¡Hasta luego!" COQUÍ [TURN: adiós (adiós, adios)]: "¡Adiós!" NARR: "La familia va a casa. Buenas noches, fonda."

**Fewest hearings before a word can cost:** no new word decides here; the game turns on mesera, mesero and cocinero. Heard before the game: propina 6 (steps 2, 3), satisfecho 3 with satisfecha (step 1), hasta luego 4 (step 4), buenas noches 2 (step 4) and again in the rounds and the ending.
**A miss is filed under:** the one that was said (mesera, mesero or cocinero).
**Chance without the Spanish:** one in three, each round on its own.
**Speaking turns:** two (steps 1, 9): la cuenta por favor; adiós.

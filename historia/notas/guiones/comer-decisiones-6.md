# A comer: the orchestrator's decisions for draft 6 (Nov 16)

From the second last gate (on draft 5). Apply all of these to historia/notas/guiones/comer.md. Where these words and the draft differ, these win. Change nothing else. Add a section "What the second gate changed (draft 5 to draft 6)" at the end, one bullet per story plus one for the pairs.

## Story 33
- Kept as it is. "Mi pan no tiene…" is made of old words, and "tiene" alone can decide those draws. Every design with "me gusta / no me gusta" leaves one old word that can decide ("no" or "tiene"); "tiene" is the better of the two. "For the owner" says it in one plain line.

## Story 34
1. Step 3: Luis's "¡El arroz está rico!" comes first, during his last forkfuls; then "Quiero más arroz, por favor." and "¿Y tú, Coquí?" with nothing between them.
2. The frog's two bubbles (steps 3, 4 and round A) become "¡Yo también!" / "Yo estoy lleno." (the same length by ear). "¡Buen provecho!" is no longer one of his bubbles in A or in steps 3 and 4. Right "Yo estoy lleno.": Luis eats the spoon's mound too. Step 4 shows the same thought bubble as A (his round belly beside his clean plate), so the bubble is seen under the sign before it matters.
3. The three lines of round B and steps 5 and 6: "Yo también." / "Yo estoy lleno." (mamá and Carmen: "llena") / "Yo me levanto." (each within one syllable of the others). After the touch, whoever said "Yo me levanto." gets up and says to the table: "¡Buen provecho!" (not part of the choice).
4. "Buen provecho" now decides nothing in this story: it is heard (step 2: the family and the frog's own signed line; after the touch in steps 5, 6 and B; the ending "¡Buen provecho, papá!") and tested the next day by its pair. "Lleno" decides with "también" in A and B: count its hearings (the frog's own line in step 4, steps 5 and 6, and "Tú estás lleno."); it must be three before A.
5. A miss is filed under: A, the word of the right bubble (también or lleno); B, también. Rewrite the hearings line, the chance line, "Words that decide" and "For the owner".

## Story 35
1. The milk: whatever is left in the milk pan after the frog's cup in round B, abuela always tips into Canelo's bowl on the floor, and Canelo laps it (in every branch). So the pan is empty in every branch, and "No hay más leche." is true. Her own cup takes only coffee, as before.
2. The children leaving in steps 4 and 5 stay as they are.

## Pairs
1. "me gusta": the sentence becomes "Luis dice: Me gusta el pollo." "no me gusta": "Carmen dice: No me gusta la manzana." (both people are in both pictures).
2. "no me gusta", the other picture is a "no" too: Carmen shaking her head and pushing away a plate of rice, a manzana lying beside the plate. The right one: Carmen pushing away a plate with a manzana on it, frowning.
3. "quieres" ("¿Quieres pan?"): right, abuela holding a roll out to Luis, whose hands are empty; other, Luis holding his empty plate up to abuela with a question mark over him, the basket of rolls beside abuela. Both are asks about bread; only one is an offer to "you".
4. "¿qué quieres?" ("Abuela dice: ¿Qué quieres?"): a question mark over abuela in both pictures. Right, abuela with a spoon in each hand over two pots, looking at Carmen; other, abuela holding one full plate out to Carmen.
5. "buen provecho": keep the draft-5 pictures.
6. Keep the others ("Canelo se lo come todo." stays).

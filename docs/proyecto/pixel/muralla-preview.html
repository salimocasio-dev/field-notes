DEV TOOL, not part of the game. The set of La muralla (muralla-set.html, drawn in a hidden frame: the top of the fort's wall at night, place 10, scene 4: the parapet with the sea, the moon and the road below at the right; the two sentry boxes with their red and blue doors; the bench against the parapet; the small table at the left front; the head of the steps at the right front) with the characters and the small things drawn over it as sprites, each placed from muralla-sheet.json: the lowest drawn row of the sprite (its feet; for a person who sits, the bottom of the hips) just above the spot's screen row, its middle column on the spot's column (the sheet's rules.anchor), hidden by every set pixel nearer than its feet (the sheet's front rule), with the shadows of those who stand on the floor. The three lanterns hang from the cord (their top middle on the spot). Whoever is inside a sentry box with its door open is drawn standing at dentro_roja and CLIPPED to the door's box (the open door is a dark stand-in). The guagua and the two walking músicos are far away: drawn at picture pixels on the road, behind everything the set draws. The characters are today's sprite makers (historia/engine.js, escenas/kit.js): abuela (mkAbuela), abuelo (kit's FAM.abuelo), Luis, Carmen, the frog; the others (the two músicos, don Pepe, the maestra, the piragüero, the señor, the señora, two guest children) are kit's mkPersona in other colours (stand-ins). The lanterns, the camera, the candle, the glass, the plate, the chair, the guitar, the hat, the bag, the box, the book, the guagua and the open door are small plain stand-ins drawn here, not final art. Nobody is drawn turned (abuela at her camera, the guests at the steps look at us). The make reads the moments through PREVIEW.inspect(m, overrides), PREVIEW.silhouette(name) and PREVIEW.moment(m) (?nodraw=1 skips the first drawing).

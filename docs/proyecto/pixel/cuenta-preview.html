DEV TOOL, not part of the game. The set of La cuenta (cuenta-set.html, drawn in a hidden frame: the front of the fonda late at night, place 8, scene 4) with the characters and the small things drawn over it as sprites, each placed from cuenta-sheet.json: the lowest drawn row of the sprite (its feet) just above the spot's screen row, its middle column on the spot's column (the sheet's rules.anchor), hidden by every set pixel nearer than its feet (the sheet's front rule), with the shadows of those who stand on the ground. Then cropped to the 196 x 216 view (wide). The characters are today's sprite makers (historia/engine.js, escenas/kit.js): abuela (mkAbuela standing, mkAbuelaS sitting), Luis and Carmen (kit's FAM.hermano and FAM.hermana), abuelo (kit's FAM.abuelo, a grown-up man), the señor, the señora, the hombre, the mujer and the joven (the outfits of El parque); the mesera is a grown-up woman (white blouse, teal apron and skirt), the mesero a grown-up man (white shirt, grey trousers) and the cocinero a grown-up man (white, red apron): all three are stand-ins made with mkPersona. The dessert cart (76 x 22), the cup, the flans, the tub, the cover, the card machine and the jar are stand-ins drawn here (small plain shapes of the sizes the sheet gives), not final art. Nobody is drawn turned: whoever sits faces us. The make reads the moments through PREVIEW.inspect(m), PREVIEW.inspectLayers(set, layers), PREVIEW.boxes() and PREVIEW.moment(m) (?nodraw=1 skips the first drawing). Address: ?m=1 story 93 opening, ?m=2 story 93 game, ?m=3 story 94 opening, ?m=4 story 94 game, ?m=5 story 95 opening, ?m=6 story 95 game, ?m=7 story 95 ending (default 1).

DEV TOOL, not part of the game. The set of La comida (comida-set.html, drawn in a hidden frame: the fonda's terrace at night, place 8) with the characters and the small things drawn over it as sprites, each placed from comida-sheet.json: the lowest drawn row of the sprite (its feet) just above the spot's screen row, its middle column on the spot's column (the sheet's rules.anchor), hidden by every set pixel nearer than its feet (the sheet's front rule), with the shadows of those who stand on the ground. Then cropped to the 196 x 216 view (wide). The characters are today's sprite makers (historia/engine.js, escenas/kit.js): abuela (sitting: mkAbuelaS; standing: mkAbuela), abuelo (KIT.FAM.abuelo), Luis, Carmen, the frog and the dog (Canelo, sitting); the señora, the señor and the joven are El banco's outfits (historia/escenas/banco.js QUIEN) without that scene's own extras (the ponytail, the hat and the stick, the cap and the bag); the mesera and the cocinero are stand-ins (mkPersona, a grown-up maker, in new outfits: a long-haired woman in a white blouse with a teal apron; a bald man with a moustache in white). The plates, the jar, the glasses, the four things of the station's shelf, the bowls and the dog's bowl are stand-ins drawn here, not final art. Nobody is drawn turned or holding anything. The make reads the moments through PREVIEW.inspect(m), PREVIEW.inspectLayers(set, layers), PREVIEW.nearest(...), PREVIEW.nearestClear(...) and PREVIEW.silhouettes() (?nodraw=1 skips the first drawing, &sil=1 does not read the sheet). Address: ?m=1 story 90 (the table: three small plates, the jar and the glasses), ?m=2 story 91 opening (the station's shelf and the mesera), ?m=3 story 91 game (the soup and the salad), ?m=4 story 92 game (the line at the pot), ?m=5 story 92 opening (the family in the line), default 1. Add &scale=3 to draw it 3x, &marks=1 to mark each spot, &depth=flat to test against the feet's depth alone instead of the sheet's upright plane. Serve the repo over http.

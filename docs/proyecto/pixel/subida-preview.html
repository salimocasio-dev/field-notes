DEV TOOL, not part of the game. The set of El camino al fuerte (subida-set.html, drawn in a hidden frame: the wide lawn in front of the old fort, place 10, scene 1: the wall with its gate, the two iron lamps, the pole over the gate and the sentry box; the path, the tree with its flat stone; with fiesta 1 the party table and the hammock's two posts) with the characters and the small things drawn over it as sprites, each placed from subida-sheet.json: the lowest drawn row of the sprite (its feet) just above the spot's screen row, its middle column on the spot's column (the sheet's rules.anchor), hidden by every set pixel nearer than its feet (the sheet's front rule), with the shadows of those who stand on the ground. The tree's leaves sway, so the depth the sprites are tested against is the live depth of the still frame (t = 0), not the baked one. Then cropped to the 196 x 216 view (wide). The characters are today's sprite makers (historia/engine.js, escenas/kit.js): abuela (mkAbuela), Luis, Carmen, the frog and abuelo (kit's FAM.abuelo) are real; don Pepe, the maestra, the piragüero, the señora, the joven and the boy are other grown-up (and child) makers in other clothes, stand-ins. The lanterns, the flag, Luis's paper, the basket, the dish, the jugs, the hammock (with Carmen's head at its left end, or empty), the ball and the stakes are small plain stand-ins drawn here, not final art. Nobody is drawn turned; Carmen "sitting by the tree" is drawn standing. The make reads the moments through PREVIEW.inspect(m), PREVIEW.inspectLayers(set, layers), PREVIEW.silhouette(name), PREVIEW.nearest(...), PREVIEW.nearestClear(...) and PREVIEW.nearestFree(...)

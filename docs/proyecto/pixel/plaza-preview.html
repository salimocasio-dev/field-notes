DEV TOOL, not part of the game. The set of La plaza (plaza-set.html, drawn in a hidden frame: the long seat under the tree, the notice board, the piragüero's cart) with the characters and the small things drawn over it as sprites, each placed from plaza-sheet.json: the lowest drawn row of the sprite (its feet) just above the spot's screen row, its middle column on the spot's column (the sheet's rules.anchor), hidden by every set pixel nearer than its feet (the sheet's front rule), with the shadows of those who stand on the ground. Then cropped to the 196 x 216 window of the sheet (views.wide). The characters are today's sprite makers (historia/engine.js, escenas/kit.js); Ana, her mamá, Beto, her abuela, the visitors, the piragüero and the two helpers have no drawing yet: each is the nearest maker (mkPersona, a grown-up or a child) in other clothes, for size. The pigeons (the El parque bird, mkAve, at the size drawAve draws it, grey), the cards under their stone, the photos (5 x 6) and the two answer bubbles are stand-ins drawn here, not final art. The make reads the moments through PREVIEW.inspect(m), PREVIEW.inspectLayers(set, layers), PREVIEW.nearest(...) and PREVIEW.silhouette(k) (?nodraw=1 skips the first drawing). Address: ?m=1 story 60 opening, ?m=2 story 60 game (the two bubbles), ?m=3 story 61 opening, ?m=4 story 61 game, ?m=5 story 62 opening, ?m=6 story 62 game, ?m=7 a dev aid: moment 2 with the three pigeons on the seat's back (default 1). Add &scale=3 to draw it 3x, &marks=1 to mark each spot, &depth=flat to test against the feet's depth alone instead of the sheet's upright plane. Serve the repo over http.

DEV TOOL, not part of the game. The set of La ropa (ropa-set.html, drawn in a hidden frame: where the family changes, at the back of the beach, place 7 scene 3) with the characters and the small things drawn over it as sprites, each placed from ropa-sheet.json: the lowest drawn row of the sprite (its feet) just above the spot's screen row, its middle column on the spot's column (the sheet's rules.anchor), hidden by every set pixel nearer than its feet (the sheet's front rule, the set taken after frame(0): the palms' fronds count), with the shadows of those who stand on the ground. Then cropped to the 196 x 216 view (wide). The characters are today's sprite makers (historia/engine.js, escenas/kit.js: abuela, Luis, Carmen, the frog); the clothes, the things afloat, the plate and the crate it stands on, the bag, the shoes and the driver are stand-ins drawn here, not final art. The four children of story 80 are Luis, Luis, Carmen, Carmen (stand-ins; the script has them seen from behind, the makers draw from the front). The make reads the moments through PREVIEW.inspect(m), PREVIEW.inspectLayers(set, layers), PREVIEW.nearest(...), PREVIEW.nearestClear(...), PREVIEW.boxCover(...) and PREVIEW.fronds(...) (?nodraw=1 skips the first drawing). Address: ?m=1 story 78 opening, ?m=2 story 78 game, ?m=3 story 79 opening, ?m=4 story 79 game, ?m=5 story 80 opening, ?m=6 story 80 game (default 1). Add &scale=3 to draw it 3x, &marks=1 to mark each spot, &t=2.5 to draw the fronds at that wind time, &depth=flat to test against the feet's depth alone instead of the sheet's upright plane.

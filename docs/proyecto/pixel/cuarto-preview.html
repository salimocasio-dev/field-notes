DEV TOOL, not part of the game. The set of El cuarto (cuarto-set.html, drawn in a hidden frame: the children's bedroom, place 9) with the characters and the small things drawn over it as sprites, each placed from cuarto-sheet.json: the lowest drawn row of the sprite (its feet) just above the spot's screen row, its middle column on the spot's column (the sheet's rules.anchor), hidden by every set pixel nearer than its feet (the sheet's front rule), with the shadows of those who stand on the floor. Whoever lies or sits up in a bed (cama_luis, cama_carmen) is the standing sprite hung DOWN the bed from the head's place (its highest drawn row, outline included, on the spot's row; its middle column on the spot's column), cut at the bed's foot, with a blanket over it (the bed's colour, the bed's width) from his chest (lying) or his waist (sitting up); he lies on his mattress, so his depth is the mattress's (the sheet's front.hung). Then cropped to the 196 x 216 view (wide). The characters are today's sprite makers (historia/engine.js, escenas/kit.js): abuela (mkAbuela, standing: with a plain camera for foto), abuelo (FAM.abuelo: a grown-up man), Luis (FAM.hermano), Carmen (FAM.hermana), Canelo (KIT.mkPerro), the frog; mamá is only a voice. The baby, the Z's, the plate, the bell, the big pillow, the clothes on the chair, the blankets and Canelo's nose are small plain stand-ins drawn here, not final art. The make reads the moments through PREVIEW.inspect(m), PREVIEW.inspectLayers(set, layers), PREVIEW.silhouette(name), PREVIEW.nearest(...), PREVIEW.nearestClear(...) and PREVIEW.nearestFree(...) (?nodraw=1 skips the first drawing). Address: ?m=1 story 96 start, ?m=2 story 96 game, ?m=3 story 96 looking, ?m=4 story 97 start, ?m=5 story 97 ending, ?m=6 story 98 (default 1). Add &scale=3 to draw it 3x, &marks=1 to mark each spot, &depth=flat to test against the feet's depth alone instead of the sheet's upright plane, &frog=busca_silla (any spot) to try the frog somewhere else in the moment.

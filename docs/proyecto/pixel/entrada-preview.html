DEV TOOL, not part of the game. The set of La entrada (entrada-set.html, drawn in a hidden frame: the room of the fonda seen from inside, place 8) with the characters and the small things drawn over it as sprites, each placed from entrada-sheet.json: the lowest drawn row of the sprite (its feet; for a person who sits, the bottom of the hips) just above the spot's screen row, its middle column on the spot's column (the sheet's rules.anchor), hidden by every set pixel nearer than its feet (the sheet's front rule), with the shadows of those who stand on the ground. Then cropped to the 196 x 216 view (wide). The characters are today's sprite makers (historia/engine.js, escenas/kit.js): abuela, Luis, Carmen, the frog and the people of El parque (the señor, the señora, the hombre, the mujer, the joven) are real; the mesera, the mesero and the two grown-ups who have no name are stand-ins (the grown-up maker, kit.js mkPersona, other clothes), abuelo is the family's grandfather of kit.js (FAM.abuelo: a grown-up man). The flowers, the star card, the pile of plates, the stool, the saucer of sweets, the menu card, the glasses, the tray, the candles and the two cloths are stand-ins drawn here, not final art. Nobody is drawn turned: the two at the vellonera face us, not the vellonera. The make reads the moments through PREVIEW.inspect(m), PREVIEW.inspectLayers(set, layers), PREVIEW.nearest(...), PREVIEW.nearestClear(...) and PREVIEW.boxes() (?nodraw=1 skips the first drawing). Address: ?m=1 story 84 start, ?m=2 story 84 step 2, ?m=3 story 84 round B, ?m=4 story 84 ending, ?m=5 story 85 game, ?m=6 story 85 after a right move, ?m=7 story 86 (default 1). Add &scale=3 to draw it 3x, &marks=1 to mark each spot, &depth=flat to test against the feet's depth alone instead of the sheet's upright plane. Serve the repo over http.

DEV TOOL, not part of the game. The set of La parada (parada-set.html, drawn in a hidden frame; the guaguas are drawn by the set itself through its autos= parameter, in the game they are sprites) with the characters and the small things drawn over it as sprites, each placed from parada-sheet.json: the lowest drawn row of the sprite (its feet) just above the spot's screen row, its middle column on the spot's column (the sheet's rules.anchor), hidden by every set pixel nearer than its feet (the sheet's front rule), with the shadows of those who stand or sit. Then cropped to the 196 x 216 view (sheet: views.wide). The characters are today's sprite makers (historia/engine.js, escenas/kit.js); the heads at the guagua's glass, the chofer, the tickets, the boxes, the basket of rolls and the ball are stand-ins drawn here, not final art. The people inside the guagua at its back door's glass are the guagua sprite's: not drawn here. The make reads the moments through PREVIEW.inspect(m) and the sprites' silhouettes through PREVIEW.sil() (?nodraw=1 skips the first drawing, ?nosheet=1 does not read the sheet). Address: ?m=1 story 51 opening (morning), ?m=2 story 51 game, ?m=3 story 52 opening, ?m=4 story 52 game, ?m=5 story 53 start (early), ?m=6 story 53 ending (night) (default 1). Add &scale=3 to draw it 3x, &marks=1 to mark each spot, &depth=flat to test against the feet's depth alone instead of the sheet's upright plane, &also=chofer,ventana2,fila5 to add a sprite at those spots.

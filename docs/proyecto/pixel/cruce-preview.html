DEV TOOL, not part of the game. The set of El cruce (cruce-set.html, drawn in a hidden frame: a crossing of two streets turned 45 degrees, no cars) with the characters and the small things drawn over it as sprites, each placed from cruce-sheet.json: the lowest drawn row of the sprite (its feet) just above the spot's screen row, its middle column on the spot's column (the sheet's rules.anchor), hidden by every set pixel nearer than its feet (the sheet's front rule), with the shadows of those who stand on the ground. Then cropped to the 196 x 216 view of the moment (wide for stories 57 and 58's game, plaza for 58's ending and 59). The characters are today's sprite makers (historia/engine.js, escenas/kit.js); the bicycles, the cart, the bag, the bottle and the camera are stand-ins drawn here, not final art. There is no back view of a person: papá at papa_camara and Luis on his bicycle are drawn from the front; mamá's arms that hold the bebé up are not drawn. The make reads the moments through PREVIEW.inspect(m), PREVIEW.inspectLayers(set, layers), PREVIEW.walk(...) and PREVIEW.nearest(...) (?nodraw=1 skips the first drawing). Address: ?m=1 story 57 opening, ?m=2 story 57 game, ?m=3 story 58 game (the right street shut), ?m=4 story 58 ending (plaza view), ?m=5 story 59 with the frog behind Canelo, ?m=6 the frog in the gap beside abuela, ?m=7 story 59 ending (default 1). Add &scale=3 to draw it 3x, &marks=1 to mark each spot, &depth=flat to test against the feet's depth alone instead of the sheet's upright plane. Serve the repo over http.

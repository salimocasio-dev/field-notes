DEV TOOL, not part of the game. The set of La sombrilla (sombrilla-set.html, drawn in a hidden frame: abuela's beach umbrella, close) with the characters and the small things drawn over it as sprites, each placed from sombrilla-sheet.json: the lowest drawn row of the sprite (its feet) just above the spot's screen row, its middle column on the spot's column (the sheet's rules.anchor), hidden by every set pixel nearer than its feet (the sheet's front rule), with the shadows of those who stand on the sand. Then cropped to the 196 x 216 view (wide). The people are today's sprite makers (historia/engine.js, escenas/kit.js: abuela, Luis, Carmen, Canelo, the frog); abuela lying has no maker yet (a plain stand-in drawn here, 52 x 18); the shells, the bucket, the three things in the row, the ball are stand-ins drawn here, not final art. Nobody is drawn turned. The make reads the moments through PREVIEW.inspect(m), PREVIEW.inspectLayers(set, layers), PREVIEW.nearest(...), PREVIEW.nearestClear(...), PREVIEW.nearestOutside(...) and PREVIEW.sil(name) (?nodraw=1 skips the first drawing). Address: ?m=1 story 75 opening, ?m=2 story 75 ending, ?m=3 story 76, ?m=4 story 76 after a miss, ?m=5 story 77 opening, ?m=6 story 77 ending (default 1). Add &scale=3 to draw it 3x, &marks=1 to mark each spot, &depth=flat to test against the feet's depth alone instead of the sheet's upright plane. Serve the repo over http.

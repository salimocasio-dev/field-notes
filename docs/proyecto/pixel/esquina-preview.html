DEV TOOL, not part of the game. The set of La esquina (esquina-set.html, drawn in a hidden frame; the cars and the guagua come from the set's own autos= parameter, as the set's header says) with the characters and the small things drawn over it as sprites, each placed from esquina-sheet.json: the lowest drawn row of the sprite (its feet) just above the spot's screen row, its middle column on the spot's column (the sheet's rules.anchor), hidden by every set pixel nearer than its feet (the sheet's front rule: this is what puts a car or a guagua in front of a person behind it), with the shadows of those who stand on the ground. Then cropped to the 196 x 216 view wide. The characters are today's sprite makers (historia/engine.js, escenas/kit.js); the obrero, the bebé on an arm, the bicycle, the bucket, the cones and the broom are stand-ins drawn here, not final art. Address: ?m=1 story 48 opening, ?m=2 story 48 game, ?m=3 story 48 ending, ?m=4 story 49 opening, ?m=5 story 49 game, ?m=6 story 49 ending, ?m=7 story 50 game, ?m=8 story 50 ending (default 1). The make reads the moments through PREVIEW.inspect(m) (?nodraw=1 skips the first drawing). Add &scale=3 to draw it 3x, &marks=1 to mark each spot, &depth=flat to test against the feet's depth alone instead of the sheet's upright plane, &down=1 to draw the cone at cono2 knocked over in moment 7 (in moment 8 it always is).

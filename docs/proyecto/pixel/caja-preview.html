DEV TOOL, not part of the game. The set of La caja (caja-set.html, drawn in a hidden frame) with the characters and the small things drawn over it as sprites, each placed from caja-sheet.json: the lowest drawn row of the sprite (its feet) just above the spot's screen row, its middle column on the spot's column (the sheet's rules.anchor), hidden by every set pixel nearer than its feet (the sheet's front rule), with the shadows of those who stand on the floor. The amount (the sheet's thing importe: a pale plate 13 x 9 with two numerals of 5 x 7, its middle on the screen point of the register's amount window) is drawn in front of the register (hidden only by a sprite nearer than the window), the price tags stand at the sheet's etiqueta offset from their thing, the two answer keys are cards over the picture at the sheet's boxes. Then cropped to the 196 x 216 view wide. The characters are today's sprite makers (historia/engine.js, escenas/kit.js); the small things (box of cans, tags, cups, paper holder, jar, ball, bills) are stand-ins drawn here, not final art. Address: ?m=1 story 45 (default), ?m=2 the line, ?m=3 story 46, ?m=4 story 47. The make reads the moments through PREVIEW.inspect(m) (?nodraw=1 skips the first drawing). Add &scale=3 to draw it 3x, &marks=1 to mark each spot, &depth=flat to test against the feet's depth alone instead of the sheet's upright plane. Serve the repo over http.

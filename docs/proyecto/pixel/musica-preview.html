The set of La música (musica-set.html, drawn in a hidden frame: the fort's courtyard at night, place 10, scene 2: the inner wall with its lamps and string of lights, the low stage at the left with its two stools and the rack for the instruments, the open floor, the bench against the wall at the right and the stool beside the floor) with the characters and the small things drawn over it as sprites, each placed from musica-sheet.json: the lowest drawn row of the sprite (its feet; for a person who sits, the bottom of the hips) just above the spot's screen row, its middle column on the spot's column (the sheet's rules.anchor), hidden by every set pixel nearer than its feet (the sheet's front rule), with the shadows of those who stand on the floor. A thing that hangs (the guitar, the drum, the three paper notes) has its top middle on the spot. Then cropped to the 196 x 216 view (wide). The characters are today's sprite makers (historia/engine.js, escenas/kit.js): abuela (standing: mkAbuela; sitting: mkAbuelaS), abuelo (kit's FAM.abuelo), Luis, Carmen, the frog; the two músicos (one with a hat, one with a beard) and the other people are kit's mkPersona (a grown-up) with colours of their own: stand-ins, not final art. The guitar, the drum, the microphone, the paper notes, the guitar across the knees and the camera are small plain stand-ins drawn here. Nobody is drawn turned.

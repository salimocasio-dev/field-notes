DEV TOOL, not part of the game. The set of El baño (bano-set.html, drawn in a hidden frame: the tiled back wall with the shower at the left, the sink under its mirror with a shelf at each side, the water heater and the door at the right, a stool and a basket; place 9, scene 2) with the characters and the small things drawn over it as sprites, each placed from bano-sheet.json: the lowest drawn row of the sprite (its feet) just above the spot's screen row, its middle column on the spot's column (the sheet's rules.anchor), hidden by every set pixel nearer than its feet (the sheet's front rule: the shower's low wall hides whoever stands in it from its top down), with the shadows of those who stand on the floor. Then cropped to the 196 x 216 view (wide). The characters are today's sprite makers (historia/engine.js, escenas/kit.js): abuela (mkAbuela, standing), abuelo (FAM.abuelo: the family's grandfather, a grown-up man), Luis (FAM.hermano), Carmen (FAM.hermana), Canelo (KIT.mkPerro, standing) and the frog; the child in a raincoat is a stand-in (Luis's maker in yellow, a yellow hood round the head); the soap, the towels, the tube, the tiny brush, the toothbrush and the comb, the stool under Carmen, the towels over the children's heads and the gauge's three bars are small plain stand-ins drawn here, not final art. Nobody is drawn turned: whoever stands in the shower or at the sink faces us (the makers have no back view). Mama is only a voice. The make reads the moments through PREVIEW.inspect(m), PREVIEW.inspectLayers(set, layers), PREVIEW.silhouette(name), PREVIEW.sprite(name), PREVIEW.nearest(...), PREVIEW.nearestClear(...) and PREVIEW.nearestFree(...) (?nodraw=1 skips the first drawing).

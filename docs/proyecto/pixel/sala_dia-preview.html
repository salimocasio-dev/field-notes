DEV TOOL, not part of the game. The set of La sala, de día (sala_dia-set.html, drawn in a hidden frame: the living room in the afternoon, place 9, scene 3: the desk with the computer, the low table with its two cushions, the armchair and its small table, the shelf, the window, the kitchen's door) with the characters and the small things drawn over it as sprites, each placed from sala_dia-sheet.json: the lowest drawn row of the sprite (its feet) just above the spot's screen row, its middle column on the spot's column (the sheet's rules.anchor), hidden by every set pixel nearer than its feet (the sheet's front rule), with the shadows of those who stand on the floor. Then cropped to the 196 x 216 view (wide). The characters are today's sprite makers (historia/engine.js, escenas/kit.js): abuela (mkAbuela standing), abuelo (kit's FAM.abuelo, a grown-up man: bald, white moustache, glasses, cream shirt), Luis and Carmen (kit's FAM.hermano and FAM.hermana), the frog. Whoever sits is hung by the lap and faces us. The book, the pad, the telephone, the three album pages, the box of stars and the photo in Carmen's hands are small plain stand-ins drawn here, not final art. The make reads the moments through PREVIEW.inspect(m), PREVIEW.inspectLayers(set, layers), PREVIEW.silhouette(name), PREVIEW.nearest(...), PREVIEW.nearestClear(...) and PREVIEW.nearestFree(...) (?nodraw=1 skips the first drawing). Address: ?m=1 story 102 opening, ?m=2 story 102 game, ?m=3 story 103 game, ?m=4 story 103 opening, ?m=5 story 104 (default 1). Add &scale=3 to draw it 3x, &marks=1 to mark each spot, &boxes=1 to outline the sheet's boxes on abuelo (oreja_abuelo, boca_abuelo) and the set things, &depth=flat to test against the feet's depth alone instead of the sheet's upright plane.

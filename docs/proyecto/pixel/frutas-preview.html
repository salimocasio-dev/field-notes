DEV TOOL, not part of the game. The set of Las frutas (frutas-set.html, drawn in a hidden frame: the street front and the stand of colmado-fuera.js) with the characters and the small things drawn over it as sprites, each placed from frutas-sheet.json: the lowest drawn row of the sprite (its feet) just above the spot's screen row, its middle column on the spot's column (the sheet's rules.anchor), hidden by every set pixel nearer than its feet (the sheet's front rule), with the shadows of those who stand on the pavement. Then cropped to the 196 x 216 view. The characters are today's sprite makers (historia/engine.js, escenas/kit.js); the goat and the small things (coins, the baskets, the fruit, the crates, the knife, a fly) are stand-ins drawn here, not final art. Address: ?m=1 story 42 opening, ?m=2 story 42 game, ?m=3 story 43, ?m=4 story 44 (default 1). Add &scale=3 to draw it 3x, &marks=1 to mark each spot, &depth=flat to test against the feet's depth alone instead of the sheet's upright plane, &order=list to draw the sprites in the order they are listed instead of by depth (each still hidden by the set), &also=pepe.carro,senora.puerta to add a sprite at those spots (pepe.*, abuela.*, senora.*, cabra.*).

DEV TOOL, not part of the game. The set of El cielo (cielo-set.html, drawn in a hidden frame: the porch of the beach house and a great deal of sky, place 7) with the characters and the small things drawn over it as sprites, each placed from cielo-sheet.json: the lowest drawn row of the sprite (its feet) just above the spot's screen row, its middle column on the spot's column (the sheet's rules.anchor), hidden by every set pixel nearer than its feet (the sheet's front rule), with the shadows of those who stand on the ground. The set's palm sways: the picture and the depth are taken after frame(0) (DEPTH.live()), so a frond in front of a sprite hides it. Then cropped to the 196 x 216 view (wide). The characters are today's sprite makers (historia/engine.js, escenas/kit.js: abuela sitting, Luis, Carmen, the frog); the radio, the candles, the glass cover, the shawl, the tray, the umbrella (shut and open), the kite, the ball, the telescope and the round picture are stand-ins drawn here, not final art. Nobody is drawn turned. The make reads the moments through PREVIEW.inspect(m), PREVIEW.inspectLayers(set, layers), PREVIEW.boxes(set, boxes), PREVIEW.nearest(...) and PREVIEW.nearestClear(...) (?nodraw=1 skips the first drawing). Address: ?m=1 story 81 opening (blue sky), ?m=2 story 81 game (clouded), ?m=3 story 82 opening (grey), ?m=4 story 82 game (grey, the blind down, the cover over the candles), ?m=5 story 83 (night), ?m=6 story 82 ending (pink), default 1.

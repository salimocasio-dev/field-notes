DEV TOOL, not part of the game. The set of La orilla (orilla-set.html, drawn in a hidden frame: the shore, place 7 scene 1) with the characters and the small things drawn over it as sprites, each placed from orilla-sheet.json: the lowest drawn row of the sprite (its feet) just above the spot's screen row, its middle column on the spot's column (the sheet's rules.anchor), hidden by every set pixel nearer than its feet (the sheet's front rule; the set taken at frame(0), so a swaying frond counts), with the shadows of those who stand on the ground. Then cropped to the 196 x 216 view (wide). The characters are today's sprite makers (historia/engine.js, escenas/kit.js): abuela, Luis, Carmen, Canelo and the frog are real; whoever sits or kneels on the sand is today's sitting drawing cut off below the hips (no maker draws a person on the sand: a stand-in); the shells, the toy boats, the towels, the pile, the basket and the frog's boat are plain shapes drawn here, not final art. The make reads the moments through PREVIEW.inspect(m), PREVIEW.inspectLayers(set, layers), PREVIEW.nearest(...) and PREVIEW.nearestClear(...) (?nodraw=1 skips the first drawing). Address: ?m=1 story 72 opening, ?m=2 story 72 ending, ?m=3 story 73 opening, ?m=4 story 73 game, ?m=5 story 74 opening, ?m=6 story 74 game (default 1). Add &scale=3 to draw it 3x, &marks=1 to mark each spot, &depth=flat to test against the feet's depth alone instead of the sheet's upright plane. Serve the repo over http.

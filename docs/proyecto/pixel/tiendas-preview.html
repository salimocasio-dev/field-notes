DEV TOOL, not part of the game. The set of Las tiendas (tiendas-set.html, drawn in a hidden frame: fila tiendas, the row of four shops, or fila altos, the two buildings of story 55) with the characters and the small things drawn over it as sprites, each placed from tiendas-sheet.json: the lowest drawn row of the sprite (its feet) just above the spot's screen row, its middle column on the spot's column (the sheet's rules.anchor), hidden by every set pixel nearer than its feet (the sheet's front rule), with the shadows of those who stand on the ground. Then cropped to the 196 x 216 view of its fila (the sheet's views.tiendas; for altos the sheet's altos.views.altos). The characters are today's sprite makers (historia/engine.js, escenas/kit.js); the stroller with the bebé, the bicycle with its basket and tins, the umbrella and the bag of bread are stand-ins drawn here, not final art. The make reads the moments through PREVIEW.inspect(m) and the lone sprites through PREVIEW.probe(...) (?nodraw=1 skips the first drawing). Address: ?m=1 story 54 start, ?m=2 story 54 at the correo's door, ?m=3 story 54 ending (the horse), ?m=4 story 56 game (nube 1), ?m=5 story 56 ending (nube 2, rain), ?m=6 story 55 (altos, par a, lado izq), ?m=7 story 55 (par b, lado der), ?m=8 story 55 ending (the kerb) (default 1). Add &scale=3 to draw it 3x, &marks=1 to mark each spot, &depth=flat to test against the feet's depth alone instead of the sheet's upright plane. Serve the repo over http.

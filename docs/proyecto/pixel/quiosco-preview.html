DEV TOOL, not part of the game. The set of El quiosco (quiosco-set.html, drawn in a hidden frame: the stand of the square with its counter, four stools and the round table) with the characters and the small things drawn over it as sprites, each placed from quiosco-sheet.json: the lowest drawn row of the sprite (its feet; for a person who sits, the bottom of the hips) just above the spot's screen row, its middle column on the spot's column (the sheet's rules.anchor), hidden by every set pixel nearer than its feet (the sheet's front rule), with the shadows of those who stand on the ground. Then cropped to the 196 x 216 view (wide). The characters are today's sprite makers (historia/engine.js, escenas/kit.js) in stand-in outfits: the people who have no maker yet (the vendedor, the maestra, the doctor, the cocinero, the policía, the young man, the young woman, Beto, Ana, Ana's mamá, the niño, the niña) are a grown-up or a child of the nearest kind, for size; sitting people are the front view of the sitting drawing (no side view yet: the sheet says which way they face). The dome, the cake stand, the box, the limbers, the candles, the tray and the thing over the frog's head are plain stand-ins drawn here, not final art. The make reads the moments through PREVIEW.inspect(m), PREVIEW.inspectLayers(set, layers), PREVIEW.nearest(...), PREVIEW.boxes() (?nodraw=1 skips the first drawing). Address: ?m=1 story 66 opening, ?m=2 story 66 game, ?m=3 story 67 opening, ?m=4 story 67 game, ?m=5 story 68 opening, ?m=6 story 68 game (default 1).

DEV TOOL, not part of the game. The set of El reloj (reloj-set.html, drawn in a hidden frame: the foot of the square's clock tower, laid out square to the picture in u, v) with the characters and the small things drawn over it as sprites, each placed from reloj-sheet.json: the lowest drawn row of the sprite (its feet) just above the spot's screen row, its middle column on the spot's column (the sheet's rules.anchor), hidden by every set pixel nearer than its feet (the sheet's front rule), with the shadows of those who stand on the ground. Then cropped to the 196 x 216 window of the sheet's view (wide). The characters are today's sprite makers (historia/engine.js, escenas/kit.js); people who have no maker yet are STAND-INS of the same size: don Tito, the señor and the vendedor are grown-up men (the vendedor in a white apron and a paper hat (4 rows tall)), the señora who calls the bingo is the señora of today's makers, abuela sits (mkAbuelaS), Luis and Carmen are the family's children. The two hands of the clock (dark lines 2 px wide: the long 19 px, the short 12 px), the time cards (a white square with a small dial), the plate of pastelillos, the cage (20 x 22), the three prizes (16 x 16), the beans and the card of five numerals are stand-ins drawn here, not final art. The cards and the card of numerals are drawn over everything (they are the game's own signs); everything else is placed by the depth rule. The make reads the moments through PREVIEW.inspect(m), PREVIEW.inspectLayers(set, layers), PREVIEW.moment(m) and PREVIEW.nearest(...) (?nodraw=1 skips the first drawing).

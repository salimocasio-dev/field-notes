DEV TOOL, not part of the game. The set of El pedido (pedido-set.html, drawn in a hidden frame: the fonda's back wall with the kitchen's hatch, the two boards, the family's long table, the next table) with the characters and the small things drawn over it as sprites, each placed from pedido-sheet.json: the lowest drawn row of the sprite (its feet) just above the spot's screen row, its middle column on the spot's column (the sheet's rules.anchor), hidden by every set pixel nearer than its feet (the sheet's front rule), with the shadows of those who stand on the floor. Then cropped to the 196 x 216 view (wide). The characters are today's sprite makers (historia/engine.js, escenas/kit.js): abuela, abuelo (FAM.abuelo), Luis, Carmen, the frog; the señor, the señora, the joven and the mujer are El parque's outfits (as in El quiosco's preview); the mesera, the mesero and the cocinero are stand-ins (a grown-up maker in other clothes: see PEOPLE below); the cat is the cat of El camino's maker (mkGato, the sitting pose, copied here: that scene's file is not loadable on its own); the plates, the small plate, the pictures on the boards, the plantains, the drinks and the tall glass are small plain stand-ins drawn here, not final art. Nobody is drawn turned: whoever sits faces us. The make reads the moments through PREVIEW.inspect(m), PREVIEW.inspectLayers(set, layers), PREVIEW.silhouette(name), PREVIEW.nearest(...), PREVIEW.nearestClear(...) and PREVIEW.nearestFree(...) (?nodraw=1 skips the first drawing). Address: ?m=1 story 87 opening, ?m=2 story 87 game, ?m=3 story 87 wrong move, ?m=4 story 88 game, ?m=5 story 89 opening (default 1). Add &scale=3 to draw it 3x, &marks=1 to mark each spot, &depth=flat to test against the feet's depth alone instead of the sheet's upright plane. Serve the repo over http.

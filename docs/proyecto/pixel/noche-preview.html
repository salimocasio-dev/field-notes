DEV TOOL, not part of the game. The set of La noche (noche-set.html, drawn in a hidden frame: the living room at night, place 9, scene 4: the back wall with the arch into the bedroom, the lamps, the window, the clock and the sideboard; the sofa, the armchair and the rug; the television; with mesa 1 the dinner table) with the characters and the small things drawn over it as sprites, each placed from noche-sheet.json: the lowest drawn row of the sprite (its feet; for a person who sits, the bottom of the hips) just above the spot's screen row, its middle column on the spot's column (the sheet's rules.anchor), hidden by every set pixel nearer than its feet (the sheet's front rule), with the shadows of those who stand on the floor. Whoever lies in the bed is the standing sprite hung from the head's place (its top middle on the spot) with a blanket over it from the chest to the bed's foot (the sheet's rules.lying: his depth is the bed's own plane). Then cropped to the 196 x 216 view (wide). The characters are today's sprite makers (historia/engine.js, escenas/kit.js): abuela (sitting: mkAbuelaS; standing: mkAbuela), abuelo (kit's FAM.abuelo: a grown-up man), Luis, Carmen, the frog and Canelo (kit's mkPerro). The scarf, the book, the juice, the flan, the cheese, the fruit and the candle are small plain stand-ins drawn here, not final art; the children on the rug sit with their feet on the spot. Nobody is drawn turned.

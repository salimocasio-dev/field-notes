DEV TOOL, not part of the game. The set of El calendario (calendario-set.html, drawn in a hidden frame: the square's board of the week, close) with the characters and the small things drawn over it as sprites, each placed from calendario-sheet.json: the lowest drawn row of the sprite (its feet) just above the spot's screen row, its middle column on the spot's column (the sheet's rules.anchor), hidden by every set pixel nearer than its feet (the sheet's front rule), with the shadows of those who stand on the ground. Then cropped to the 196 x 216 view (wide). The characters are today's sprite makers (historia/engine.js, escenas/kit.js); the people who have no maker yet are stand-ins (the señora, the señor: the cruce preview's outfits; don Tito: a grown-up in other clothes; Beto: a child in other clothes; Luis, Carmen, abuela: their own makers); the cards, the sun peg, the balloons, the kite and its bows, the box of cards are stand-ins drawn here, not final art. Nobody is drawn turned: whoever sits on the seat faces us, not left. The make reads the moments through PREVIEW.inspect(m), PREVIEW.inspectLayers(set, layers), PREVIEW.nearest(...) and PREVIEW.nearestClear(...) (?nodraw=1 skips the first drawing). Address: ?m=1 story 69 opening, ?m=2 story 69 game, ?m=3 story 70 opening, ?m=4 story 70 game, ?m=5 story 71 opening, ?m=6 story 71 ending (default 1). Add &scale=3 to draw it 3x, &marks=1 to mark each spot, &depth=flat to test against the feet's depth alone instead of the sheet's upright plane. Serve the repo over http.

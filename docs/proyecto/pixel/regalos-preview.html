DEV TOOL, not part of the game. The set of Los regalos (regalos-set.html, drawn in a hidden frame: a corner of the fort's courtyard at night, place 10, scene 3: the inner wall with the arch onto the dance floor and its cord, the bench, the long party table, the photos' cord, the step stool and the low stool) with the characters and the small things drawn over it as sprites, each placed from regalos-sheet.json: the lowest drawn row of the sprite (its feet; a thing on a table, a bench or a stool: its bottom edge; a photo on the cord and the curtain hang from the cord: the top edge) just above the spot's screen row, its middle column on the spot's column (the sheet's rules.anchor), hidden by every set pixel nearer than its feet (the sheet's front rule), with the shadows of those who stand on the floor. Then cropped to the 196 x 216 view (wide). The characters are today's sprite makers (historia/engine.js, escenas/kit.js): abuela (mkAbuela), abuelo (kit's FAM.abuelo), Luis, Carmen, the frog; don Pepe (El colmado's man without his apron), the maestra, the piragüero and the señor are stand-ins made from mkPersona with other clothes. The curtain, the cake, the box with the bow, the flowers, the box of candles, the four photos, the packets are small plain stand-ins drawn here, not final art. Nobody is drawn turned. The four heads behind the table (m=3) are the top 16 rows of a sprite, their bottom on the table's far edge. The make reads the moments through PREVIEW.inspect(m), PREVIEW.inspectLayers(set, layers), PREVIEW.silhouette(name), PREVIEW.nearest(...), PREVIEW.nearestClear(...) and PREVIEW.nearestFree(...) (?nodraw=1 skips the first drawing).

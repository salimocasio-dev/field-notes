NOCHE · MOTION BETWEEN SELECTIONS

One thing moves, once

FIVE TIMES
press 90 ms Finger down. The thing you touch answers.
quick 140 ms Colour, outline and weight changing in place.
move 220 ms A mark travelling inside a screen: pill, thumb, knob, dash.
page 320 in · 200 out A screen or a sheet. Leaving is always the shorter one.
settle 480 ms A result: the ring on a right answer, bars growing, a count.

Distances: 8 px a row or a line · 16 px a tab's content · 24 px a page · rows follow each other 30 ms apart, the first five only.

THREE CURVES · NO BOUNCE ANYWHERE
enter
.2, 0, 0, 1
Arriving, and all the finger starts.
travel
.4, 0, .2, 1
Between two places you can see.
exit
.4, 0, 1, 1
Leaving. Nothing lingers.
NINE RULES
1

Answer the finger first. What is touched dims and shrinks to 97% in 90 ms, before anything else moves.

2

One mark travels. The pill, thumb or outline slides from the old choice to the new one. It never blinks out and back in.

3

Content follows the choice. Pick toward the end side and the new content comes in from the end side. Right-to-left mirrors it.

4

Going in grows from what was touched. Going back returns to it, and faster: 320 ms in, 200 ms out.

5

Pixel pictures never zoom. Their window grows and the picture slides behind it, at the same whole-number scale.

6

Orange moves last, and once. The next action lands 80 ms after the content, rises 8 px, then stays still.

7

A miss is quiet. A white outline draws in 140 ms and the line plays again. No shake, no colour.

8

Only live things loop: mic bars, thinking dots, a voice playing. With reduced motion, every move is a 140 ms fade in place.

9

A trip on the map is short. The coquí takes the shortest road in at most 6 hops of 200 ms. The camera travels with him. As he lands the card becomes that place and grows; a swipe up or a tap opens it.

# The readers' verdicts behind `historia/datos/repaso.json`

`veredictos/<book>.json`: for each candidate sentence of a story book, the pages whose picture it clearly fits (`si`), clearly does not (`no`), or neither (`duda`), and the pairs of pages that look almost the same (`parecidas`). Judged Nov 20 2026 by fresh readers who saw the pictures and the sentences and nothing else.

`veredictos-par/<slug>.json`: for each sentence of a word, whether it fits picture A or B of the word's own review pair, both, neither, or is unclear. The readers did not know which picture the course calls right.

They are inputs of `coqui/tool/make_repaso.py` (`--veredictos`, `--veredictos-par`). Its `--hojas-libro` and `--hojas-par` make the sheets a reader needs; its validators check a verdict file. When stories or books change, the tool refuses stale verdicts: judge the changed books and words again.

`arrastra.py` and `arrastre.json`: when stories are added, `--hojas-par` gives a word's sentences new ids (they follow the mixed order of its items), so a verdict file judged before no longer matches its sheet. `arrastra.py --hace` carries each old verdict over by sentence text (same word, same text, the same `par.png` byte for byte; nothing is carried for a word whose pictures changed) and writes the record `arrastre.json`: for every word whose sheet is new or changed, each sentence of its new sheet with the carried verdict (text, old id, value) or `sin juzgar`. `--hojas DIR --lectores N` makes the sheets of only the unjudged sentences (whole words to one reader, no answer key); `--junta OUT...` merges the readers' files with the carried verdicts into `veredictos-par/` and runs the check; `--check` proves the record against the sheets made now and the verdict files (with `--antes` and `--veredictos-antes`, the old side too). Made Nov 21 2026 for A comer: 37 words, 465 verdicts carried from the sheets of commit 64c11ae, 180 sentences to readers. `test_all.sh` runs `--check`.
